Body rules

  • Heads tall: boy 4.5, adults 5.7 to 6. Cartoon heads stay a little big, so adults are 6 heads, not the real 7.5.
  • Torso: shoulder, chest, waist, hip. Men are widest at the shoulder; the mother is widest at the hip.
  • Limbs (from the Tala sheets): one smooth taper from shoulder to wrist and hip to ankle. No pinch, bump or crease at the elbow or knee, so joints never show. Bends are wide, soft curves.
  • Hands: tucked under the end of the sleeve or arm, so there is no seam at the wrist.
  • Arms: hang close to the body and reach mid-thigh, with a slight bend at the elbow.
  • Hands and feet: a hand is about two thirds of a face. Feet are short and rounded, a little over half a head long.
  • Stance: legs straight with a small gap between them, feet under the hips.
  • Joints: knees bend forward only and elbows bend back only, always in a soft curve.

One sheet per character

Each sheet is a turnaround like your references: front, both 3/4 sides and the back against a head ruler, plus three expressions, the colours and my drawing notes.

Help me get it right

  • Is this closer to the anatomy in your turnaround sheets? Which view looks most off?
  • Should I add a straight side view (profile) as a fifth view?
  • Any character whose face, hair or clothes look wrong for the village?
  • Should hands get separate fingers, or stay simple mitten shapes for faster animation?
