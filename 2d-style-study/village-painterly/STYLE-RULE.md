# Village Painterly: locked style rule

Set by Pushpam on 29 Oct 2026. Applies to every video made in the Village Painterly thread.

1. **Characters:** use the village cast from `03 Mockups/cast.js` exactly as locked in commit 1f90270:
   - 6 heads tall with human proportions and smooth tapered limbs;
   - detailed 4-finger hands;
   - the nose sits below the eye line;
   - front and 3/4 views.
   - New characters are built with the same rig, not a new style.
2. **Backgrounds:** use the same painterly Indian-village backgrounds as the style board (`03 Mockups/scenes.js`):
   - highly detailed and painted, with warm light;
   - soft cast shadows and highlights;
   - characters sit inside the scene, with no overlaps.
3. **Changes:** the look does not change for a video without Pushpam's OK.
4. **Light (Pushpam, 29 Oct 12:01):** every shot gets dynamic shadows and lighting:
   - a clear key light direction;
   - cast shadows from characters and props that move with them;
   - rim light on the edges;
   - light that shifts over the shot (sun flicker, torch or fire flicker, passing clouds).
5. **Experiments:** the noodle (Tala) anatomy is an experiment only and is not used in videos.
