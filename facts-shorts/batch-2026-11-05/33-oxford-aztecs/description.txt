Oxford University is older than the Aztec Empire.
Teaching at Oxford was happening by 1096. The Aztecs founded their capital, Tenochtitlan, around 1325, more than 200 years later. Oxford is still teaching today.
Sources: University of Oxford, Introduction and history; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Tenochtitlan.
