## Idea
Spinal discs lose water under gravity during the day, so people shrink about 1 cm by night and regain it while lying down; astronauts grow up to 5 cm in space.

## Voiceover (62 words)
You're taller in the morning than at night. Between the bones of your spine sit soft, squishy discs. All day, gravity squashes them and they slowly lose water, so you shrink by about one centimetre. When you lie down to sleep, the discs soak the water back up and you stretch out again. Astronauts in space can grow up to five centimetres.
