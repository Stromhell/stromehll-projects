You're about 1 cm taller when you wake up.
The soft discs between your vertebrae get squashed by gravity all day and lose water, so you shrink about 1 cm. Lying down at night lets them soak it back up. In space, astronauts can grow up to 5 cm.
Sources: NASA, astronauts grow taller in space; Tyrrell, Reilly & Troup, Spine (1985), circadian variation in stature.
