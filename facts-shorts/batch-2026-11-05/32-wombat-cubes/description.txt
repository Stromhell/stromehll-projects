This animal poops perfect cubes.
Wombats are the only animal known to make cube-shaped poop. Stiff and stretchy bands in the end of their gut squeeze the drying poop into corners. They can leave up to 100 cubes a night and stack them on rocks and logs to mark territory.
Sources: Yang et al., Soft Matter (2021), Georgia Tech / University of Tasmania; Georgia Tech news.
