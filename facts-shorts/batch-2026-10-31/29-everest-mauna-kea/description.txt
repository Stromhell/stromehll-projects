Highest isn't the same as tallest.
Everest's peak is about 8,849 m above sea level. Mauna Kea in Hawaii pokes only about 4,207 m above the sea, but measured from its base on the ocean floor it is over 10,000 m tall.
Sources: BBC Science Focus; Guinness World Records.
