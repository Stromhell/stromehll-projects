Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth, but it's not the tallest. Everest's peak is about eight thousand eight hundred metres above sea level. Mauna Kea in Hawaii only pokes about four thousand metres out of the sea. But most of it is hidden underwater. Measured from its base on the ocean floor, Mauna Kea is over ten thousand metres tall. That beats Everest by more than a kilometre.
