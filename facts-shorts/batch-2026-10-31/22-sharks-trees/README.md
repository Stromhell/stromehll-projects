## Voiceover (65 words)
Sharks are older than trees. The first shark-like fish were swimming about four hundred and fifty million years ago. The first real trees didn't appear until around three hundred and eighty-five million years ago. That's a gap of more than sixty million years. Sharks also survived every mass extinction, including the one that wiped out the dinosaurs. They were here first, and they're still here.
