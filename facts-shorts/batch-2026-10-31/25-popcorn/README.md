## Idea

## Voiceover (66 words)
Why does popcorn pop? Every kernel has a tiny drop of water inside, sealed in by a tough shell. Heat turns that water into steam, and the pressure builds and builds, up to about ten times normal air pressure. Finally the shell bursts. The soft starch puffs out into a foam and sets as it cools. That fluffy white piece is the kernel, turned inside out.
