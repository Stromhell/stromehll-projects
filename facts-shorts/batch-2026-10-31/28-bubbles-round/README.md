## Idea
Surface tension shrinks a soap film to the least area for the air inside, and that shape is a sphere.

## Voiceover (69 words)
Why are bubbles round? A soap film acts like a stretchy skin that's always trying to shrink. It pulls inward until it wraps the air inside using the least skin possible. And for any amount of air, the shape with the smallest surface is a sphere. A cube or a pyramid would need more film. So even if you blow through a square wand, you still get a ball.
