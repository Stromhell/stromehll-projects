## Voiceover (65 words)
Big Ben isn't the name of the tower. It's the name of the giant bell inside it. The bell weighs over thirteen tonnes and has rung out over London since eighteen fifty-nine. The tower itself was simply called the Clock Tower, and in twenty twelve it was renamed the Elizabeth Tower for the Queen's Diamond Jubilee. So when you hear those bongs, that's Big Ben.
