## Idea

## Voiceover (68 words)
How do cats almost always land on their feet? It's called the righting reflex. As a cat falls, its inner ear tells it which way is up. It twists its front half first, then its back half, thanks to a super bendy spine and no fixed collarbone. Then it arches its back and spreads its legs like a tiny parachute. Kittens have it mastered by seven weeks old.
