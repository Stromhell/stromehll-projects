## Voiceover (64 words)
Why do we get goosebumps? Under every hair on your skin is a tiny muscle. When you're cold or scared, it pulls the hair upright. For our furry ancestors, that fluffed up their fur, trapping warm air or making them look bigger to scare off enemies. We've lost most of that fur, but the reflex stayed. Now all it gives us is little bumps.
