```
Every kernel is a tiny pressure cooker.
```

## 30-stars-twinkle

- Cover: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1K2mmPHkayHAW2GceRTY35p52sxhKk-kK/view

- Caption:

```
Look up tonight and check.
Starlight arrives as a single point, so moving air bends it around and it flickers. Planets are closer and show a tiny disc, so the wobbles average out and they shine steady. From space, stars don't twinkle at all.
```
