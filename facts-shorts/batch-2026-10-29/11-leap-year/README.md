## Idea
Leap years exist because Earth's orbit takes about 365.25 days; most century years skip it to fix the overshoot.

## Voiceover (70 words)
Why does February get an extra day every four years? Earth takes about three hundred and sixty-five and a quarter days to go around the Sun. Skip that quarter, and the calendar slips by almost a day every four years. After a century, the seasons would be off by more than three weeks. So every fourth year gets February twenty-ninth. That overshoots a little, so most century years skip it.
