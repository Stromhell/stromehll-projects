Why are airplane windows round? The first jet airliner, the de Havilland Comet, had square ones. In 1954, two Comets broke apart in mid-air. Investigators put a Comet body in a giant water tank and pressurised it over and over, like real flights. Cracks grew from the sharp window corners, where stress piles up. Curves spread that stress out evenly. That's why plane windows have rounded corners.
