## Idea
Ice floats because water molecules lock into a roomy crystal when frozen, making ice less dense than water.

## Voiceover (68 words)
Why does ice float? Most things get denser when they freeze solid. Water is weird. When it freezes, its molecules lock into a roomy crystal, so a chunk of ice is lighter than the same amount of water. That's lucky for fish. Lakes freeze from the top down, and the ice works like a lid. It keeps the water underneath liquid all winter, so life can survive below.
