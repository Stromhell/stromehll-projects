## Idea

## Voiceover (66 words)
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. So why is Venus the hottest one? Mercury has almost no air, so its heat leaks straight back into space. Venus is wrapped in a thick blanket of carbon dioxide. Sunlight gets in, but the heat can't get out. Its surface stays above four hundred and sixty degrees Celsius, day and night. That's hot enough to melt lead.
