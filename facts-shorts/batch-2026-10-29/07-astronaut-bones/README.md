## Voiceover (67 words)
Astronauts float around with no weight on them. So why do they come home weaker? Your bones rebuild themselves based on the load they carry. In space, your legs and hips carry almost nothing. So the body starts taking bone away. Studies for NASA found astronauts lose around one to two percent of bone a month. That's why crews on the space station exercise every single day.
