## Idea
Bath-wrinkled fingers are switched on by your nervous system, possibly to improve grip on wet things.

## Voiceover (67 words)
Why do your fingers wrinkle in the bath? It's not just skin soaking up water. If the nerve to a finger is damaged, that finger stays smooth. Your nervous system switches the wrinkles on. It shrinks the blood vessels under the skin and pulls it into ridges. One idea is grip. In tests, people handled wet objects better with wrinkly fingers. Basically, tyre treads for your hands.
