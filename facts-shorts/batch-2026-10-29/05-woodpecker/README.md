## Idea
Woodpecker skulls are not shock absorbers; their tiny brains simply tolerate the jolts.

## Voiceover (71 words)
A woodpecker slams its face into a tree over and over. So why doesn't it get a concussion? People thought its skull was a shock pad. In 2022, a team filmed woodpeckers with high-speed cameras and found the opposite. The head works like a stiff hammer. If it soaked up the hit, the bird would have to peck even harder. Its secret is size: a tiny brain can take bigger jolts.
