Bang your head on a tree like this and you'd be knocked out.
For years people thought a woodpecker's skull was a shock absorber. A 2022 high-speed camera study found the opposite: the head works like a stiff hammer. The real secret is size. A tiny brain can take much bigger jolts.
Sources: Smithsonian Magazine; Van Wassenbergh et al., Current Biology 2022.
