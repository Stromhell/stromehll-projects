## Voiceover (65 words)
Why do flamingos rest standing on one leg? In 2017, scientists at Georgia Tech and Emory tested two flamingos that had died at a zoo. With no muscles working at all, the body still balanced on one leg. The bird's own weight locks its joints in place. On two legs, it couldn't hold steady. So for a flamingo, one leg is basically the lazy option.
