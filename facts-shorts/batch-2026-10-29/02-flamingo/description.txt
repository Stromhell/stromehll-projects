Standing on one leg looks hard. For a flamingo it's the easy way.
In 2017, scientists found a flamingo's body locks into a stable one-leg pose with almost no muscle work. Even a dead flamingo balanced on one leg. Two legs actually take more effort.
Sources: The Conversation (Georgia Tech / Emory study, Biology Letters 2017); Science News Explores.
