Crows never forget your face, and they hold grudges.
University of Washington scientists wore a mask while catching crows. For years, crows scolded anyone in that mask, even crows that never saw the capture, because others taught them.
Sources: Marzluff et al., Animal Behaviour (2010), lasting recognition of threatening people by wild crows; University of Washington News, Crows remember human faces.
