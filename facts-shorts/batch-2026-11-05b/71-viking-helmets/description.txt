Vikings never wore horned helmets.
The only nearly complete Viking helmet, the Gjermundbu helmet, has no horns. The horns came from Carl Emil Doepler's costumes for Wagner's Ring cycle in 1876.
Sources: Museum of Cultural History, University of Oslo, Gjermundbu helmet; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Did Vikings wear horned helmets.
