## Voiceover (55 words)
Vikings never wore horned helmets. The only nearly complete Viking helmet ever found, the Gjermundbu helmet from Norway, has no horns at all. The horns came from an opera. In 1876, costume designer Carl Emil Doepler gave horned helmets to the characters in Wagner's Ring cycle. In a real fight, horns would just get grabbed.
