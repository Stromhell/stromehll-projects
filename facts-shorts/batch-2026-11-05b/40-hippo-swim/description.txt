Hippos live in water, but they can't swim.
They spend up to 16 hours a day in rivers but are too heavy to float. They walk and bounce along the bottom, hold their breath about 5 minutes, and even surface to breathe while asleep.
Sources: San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance, Hippopotamus; National Geographic, Hippopotamus.
