## Voiceover (57 words)
On Mars, sunsets are blue. The fine dust in Mars's air lets blue light through near the Sun, while the other colours get scattered away. So the daytime sky looks like butterscotch, but at sunset a blue glow surrounds the Sun. NASA's rovers have photographed it many times. It's the opposite of what we get on Earth.
