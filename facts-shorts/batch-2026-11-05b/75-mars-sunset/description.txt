On Mars, sunsets are blue.
Fine Martian dust lets blue light through near the Sun while other colours scatter. The daytime sky is butterscotch, but sunset glows blue. NASA rovers have photographed it many times.
Sources: NASA JPL, Curiosity captures blue sunset on Mars; NASA Science, Mars facts.
