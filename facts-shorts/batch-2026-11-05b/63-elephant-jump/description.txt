Elephants can't jump.
All four feet never leave the ground at once. Their leg bones point straight down and their ankles aren't springy. Even running, one foot always stays down.
Sources: Hutchinson et al., Nature (2003), are fast-moving elephants really running?; San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance, Elephant.
