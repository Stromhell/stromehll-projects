There's a jellyfish that can live forever.
When hurt, old or starving, Turritopsis dohrnii turns back into a baby polyp and grows up again. In theory it can repeat forever; in the wild most get eaten, but not by old age.
Sources: Pascual-Torner et al., PNAS (2022), comparative genomics of Turritopsis dohrnii; American Museum of Natural History, The immortal jellyfish.
