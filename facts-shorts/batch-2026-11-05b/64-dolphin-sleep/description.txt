Dolphins sleep with half their brain awake.
One side rests while the other stays alert, and one eye stays open. They keep swimming, breathing and watching for sharks, then the sides swap.
Sources: Rattenborg et al., Neurosci Biobehav Rev (2000), unihemispheric sleep; Scientific American, How do dolphins sleep.
