Your stomach makes a new lining every few days.
Stomach acid is strong enough to dissolve some metals, so lining cells wear out fast and are replaced every 3 to 5 days. Thick mucus coats the walls so your stomach doesn't digest itself.
Sources: Cleveland Clinic, Stomach: anatomy and function; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Stomach.
