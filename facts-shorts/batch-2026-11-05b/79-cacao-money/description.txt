The Aztecs used chocolate as money.
Cacao beans were real currency: a turkey cost about 100 beans and an avocado about 3. Fakers filled empty shells with mud, and the rich drank their money as a spicy drink.
Sources: Smithsonian Magazine, A brief history of chocolate; Sophie & Michael Coe, The True History of Chocolate (Thames & Hudson, 1996).
