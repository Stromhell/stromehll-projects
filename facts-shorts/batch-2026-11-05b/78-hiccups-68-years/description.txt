One man had hiccups for 68 years.
Charles Osborne started hiccuping in 1922 while lifting a pig and didn't stop until 1990, an estimated 430 million hiccups. He still married twice and had eight children.
Sources: Guinness World Records, Longest attack of hiccups; Los Angeles Times (1991), Charles Osborne obituary.
