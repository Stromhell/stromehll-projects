This tiny shrimp makes a bubble nearly as hot as the Sun.
The pistol shrimp snaps its claw so fast it fires a water jet. The bubble collapses with a bang louder than a gunshot and reaches about 4,700 °C for a split second, stunning its prey.
Sources: Lohse et al., Nature (2001), snapping shrimp make flashing bubbles; Versluis et al., Science (2000), how snapping shrimp snap.
