There are more trees on Earth than stars in our galaxy.
About 3 trillion trees grow on Earth, counted with satellites and forest surveys. The Milky Way has 100 to 400 billion stars. That's several trees per star.
Sources: Crowther et al., Nature (2015), mapping tree density at a global scale; NASA, How many stars in the Milky Way.
