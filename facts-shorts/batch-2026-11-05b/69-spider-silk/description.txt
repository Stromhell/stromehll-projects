Spider silk is stronger than steel.
Weight for weight, some silk beats steel and is tougher than Kevlar. Darwin's bark spider makes the toughest silk known and spins webs across rivers up to 25 m wide.
Sources: Agnarsson et al., PLoS ONE (2010), bioprospecting finds the toughest biological material; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Spider silk.
