Woolly mammoths were still alive when the Great Pyramid was built.
The pyramid went up around 2560 BC. A small group of mammoths survived on Wrangel Island off Siberia until about 2000 BC, centuries later.
Sources: Vartanyan et al., Nature (1993), Holocene dwarf mammoths from Wrangel Island; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Pyramids of Giza.
