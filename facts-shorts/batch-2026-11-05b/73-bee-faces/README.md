## Voiceover (56 words)
Honeybees can learn to recognise human faces. In a 2005 experiment, scientists trained bees with sugar water to pick one photo of a face out of several. Two days later, the bees still picked the right face. They don't see faces the way we do. They piece together the eyes, nose and mouth, like a puzzle.
