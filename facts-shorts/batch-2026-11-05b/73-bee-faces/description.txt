Honeybees can learn to recognise human faces.
In a 2005 study, bees trained with sugar water picked out one face photo from several, and still remembered it two days later. They piece faces together like a puzzle.
Sources: Dyer, Neumeyer & Chittka, J Exp Biol (2005), honeybee face recognition; Avarguès-Weber et al., J Exp Biol (2010), configural processing in honeybees.
