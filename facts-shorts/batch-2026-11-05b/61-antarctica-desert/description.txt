The biggest desert on Earth isn't the Sahara.
It's Antarctica. A desert means very little rain or snow, and Antarctica gets less than many hot deserts. Some Dry Valleys haven't seen rain in thousands of years.
Sources: NASA Earth Observatory, Antarctica's Dry Valleys; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Desert.
