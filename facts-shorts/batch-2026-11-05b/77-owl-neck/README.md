Owls can turn their heads two hundred and seventy degrees. Their eyes are fixed in place, so they move their whole head instead. They have fourteen neck bones, twice as many as we do. Special pools of blood keep the brain supplied while the neck twists. If we tried it, we could tear our arteries.
