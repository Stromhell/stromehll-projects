Owls can turn their heads 270 degrees.
Their eyes are fixed, so the head moves instead. They have 14 neck bones, twice ours, and blood pools that keep the brain fed while twisting. We'd tear our arteries.
Sources: de Kok-Mercado et al., Science (2013), adaptations in owls for head rotation; Johns Hopkins Medicine (2013), how owls turn their heads.
