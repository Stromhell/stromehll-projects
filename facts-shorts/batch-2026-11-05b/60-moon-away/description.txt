The Moon is running away from Earth.
It drifts about 3.8 cm further every year, about as fast as fingernails grow. We measure it by bouncing lasers off mirrors Apollo astronauts left. Our days are slowly getting longer too.
Sources: NASA, Apollo 11 laser ranging retroreflector; NASA Solar System Exploration, Moon facts.
