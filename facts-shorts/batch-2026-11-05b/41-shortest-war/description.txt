The shortest war in history was over before breakfast.
In 1896 Britain and Zanzibar went to war after a new sultan took power without British approval. Ships opened fire at 9:00. By about 9:40 the palace was wrecked and the war was over.
Sources: Encyclopaedia Britannica, Anglo-Zanzibar War; Guinness World Records, Shortest war.
