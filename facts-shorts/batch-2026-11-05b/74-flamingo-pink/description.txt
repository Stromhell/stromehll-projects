Flamingos aren't born pink.
Their food, algae and tiny shrimp, is full of carotenoid pigments that colour their feathers. Stop eating them and the pink fades, so zoos add pigment to their food.
Sources: Smithsonian's National Zoo, Flamingo facts; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Flamingo.
