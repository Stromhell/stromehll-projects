## Voiceover (58 words)
Flamingos are born grey. They turn pink from what they eat. Their food, algae and tiny shrimp, is full of pigments called carotenoids. The flamingo's body breaks them down and puts the colour into its feathers. If a flamingo stops eating them, its feathers slowly fade. Zoos even add the pigment to their food to keep them pink.
