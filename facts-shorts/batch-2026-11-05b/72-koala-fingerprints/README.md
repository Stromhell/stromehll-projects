## Voiceover (55 words)
Koalas have fingerprints almost identical to ours. Under a microscope, even experts struggle to tell a koala print from a human one. They have the same loops and swirls. Koalas and humans aren't close relatives at all. Scientists think the prints evolved separately to help grip and to feel which leaves are good to eat.
