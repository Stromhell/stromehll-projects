Koalas have fingerprints almost identical to ours.
Even experts struggle to tell them apart under a microscope: same loops and swirls. Koalas aren't close relatives, so the prints evolved separately for grip and feeling leaves.
Sources: Henneberg, Lambert & Leigh, Nature (1996), fingerprint homoplasy: koalas and humans; Australian Koala Foundation, Koala facts.
