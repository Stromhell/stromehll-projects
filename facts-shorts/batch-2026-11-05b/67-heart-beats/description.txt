Your heart beats about 100,000 times a day.
That's around 35 million beats a year and over 2.5 billion in a lifetime. It pushes blood through about 100,000 km of vessels, enough to wrap Earth more than twice.
Sources: American Heart Association, How the heart works; Franklin Institute, Blood vessels: the circulatory system.
