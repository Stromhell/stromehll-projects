## Voiceover (57 words)
Your fingernails grow about twice as fast as your toenails. A fingernail grows around three millimetres a month, so a whole nail is replaced in about six months. A toenail can take over a year. Scientists think it's because your hands get more blood flow and more knocks. Even your nails on your writing hand grow faster.
