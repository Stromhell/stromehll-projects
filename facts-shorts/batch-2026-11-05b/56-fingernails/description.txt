Your fingernails grow twice as fast as your toenails.
Fingernails grow about 3 mm a month, so a whole nail is replaced in ~6 months. Toenails can take over a year. Hands get more blood flow and more knocks.
Sources: American Academy of Dermatology, Nail facts; Yaemsiri et al., J Eur Acad Dermatol Venereol (2010), growth rate of human fingernails and toenails.
