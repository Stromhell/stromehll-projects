That banana in your kitchen is radioactive.
Bananas are full of potassium, and a tiny part of it is radioactive potassium-40. The dose is so small scientists use it as a joke unit: the banana equivalent dose. You'd need about 10 million at once to get sick.
Sources: US EPA, Radiation sources and doses (potassium-40 in food); Health Physics Society, Ask the Experts: bananas and radiation.
