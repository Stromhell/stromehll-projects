## Voiceover (58 words)
Bananas are radioactive. Seriously. They're packed with potassium, and a tiny part of natural potassium is radioactive potassium-40. Eat one and you get a dose so small that scientists made a joke unit for it, the banana equivalent dose. You'd have to eat about ten million bananas at once to get sick, so relax and enjoy your snack.
