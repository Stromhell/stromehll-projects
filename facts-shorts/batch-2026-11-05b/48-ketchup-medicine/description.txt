Ketchup used to be sold as medicine.
In the 1830s Dr John Cook Bennett claimed tomatoes cured indigestion. Companies sold tomato pills promising to fix almost anything, until fakes and wild claims ended the craze.
Sources: Smithsonian Magazine, Ketchup was once sold as medicine; Andrew F. Smith, The Tomato in America (University of South Carolina Press, 1994).
