## Voiceover (54 words)
A single fluffy cloud can weigh about five hundred tonnes. That's like a hundred elephants floating over your head. It stays up because the water is split into trillions of tiny droplets, spread across a huge space. Rising warm air keeps pushing them up. When the droplets grow too heavy, they fall as rain.
