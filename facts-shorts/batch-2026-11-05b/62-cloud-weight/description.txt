A fluffy cloud can weigh 500 tonnes.
That's like 100 elephants floating overhead. The water is split into trillions of tiny droplets spread across a huge space, and rising warm air holds them up until they grow and fall as rain.
Sources: USGS Water Science School, How much does a cloud weigh?; NOAA SciJinks, Why do clouds float.
