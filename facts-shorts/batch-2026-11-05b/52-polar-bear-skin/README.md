## Voiceover (49 words)
Polar bears aren't white. Under the fur, their skin is black, which soaks up warmth from the sun. Their fur isn't white either. Each hair is clear and hollow, and it scatters light, so it only looks white. Shave a polar bear and you'd see a big black bear.
