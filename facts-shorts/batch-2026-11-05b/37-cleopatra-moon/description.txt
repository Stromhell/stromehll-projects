Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the Great Pyramid.
The Great Pyramid was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra was born about 69 BC, nearly 2,500 years later. The Moon landing came only about 2,000 years after her.
Sources: Encyclopaedia Britannica, Pyramids of Giza; Encyclopaedia Britannica, Cleopatra.
