That smell after rain has a name.
It's petrichor. Most of it is geosmin, made by soil bacteria. Raindrops fling it into the air, and your nose can detect just a few drops of it in a swimming pool.
Sources: Bear & Thomas, Nature (1964), nature of argillaceous odour; MIT News (2015), Rain releases aerosols.
