## Voiceover (58 words)
That smell after rain has a name. It's called petrichor. Most of it comes from geosmin, a chemical made by tiny bacteria living in soil. When raindrops hit the ground, they fling it into the air as tiny bubbles. Your nose is so sensitive to it that you can smell just a few drops in a swimming pool.
