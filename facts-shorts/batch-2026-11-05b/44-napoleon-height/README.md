## Voiceover (52 words)
Napoleon wasn't short. He was about five foot seven, roughly 1.69 metres, which was average or even a bit tall for a Frenchman of his time. The myth came from two things. Old French inches were longer than British ones, so his height got mistranslated, and British cartoonists loved drawing him tiny.
