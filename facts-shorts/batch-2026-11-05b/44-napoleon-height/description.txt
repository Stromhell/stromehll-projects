Napoleon wasn't short. History lied to you.
He was about 1.69 m (5 ft 7), average or a bit tall for a Frenchman then. Longer French inches got mistranslated, and British cartoonists loved drawing him tiny.
Sources: Encyclopaedia Britannica, Was Napoleon short?; Napoleon Foundation (fondationnapoleon.org), Napoleon's height.
