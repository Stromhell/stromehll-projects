## Voiceover (59 words)
Carrots didn't used to be orange. The first farmed carrots, grown in Central Asia over a thousand years ago, were purple and yellow. Orange carrots took off in the Netherlands in the sixteen hundreds. There's a popular story that the Dutch grew them to honour William of Orange. Historians say that's probably a myth, but the orange carrot stuck.
