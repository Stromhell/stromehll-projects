Carrots didn't used to be orange.
The first farmed carrots in Central Asia were purple and yellow. Orange carrots took off in the Netherlands in the 1600s. The William of Orange story is probably a myth.
Sources: World Carrot Museum, History of carrot colours; Smithsonian Magazine, Why are carrots orange.
