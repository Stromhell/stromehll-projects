## Voiceover (59 words)
This animal survived the vacuum of space. Tardigrades, also called water bears, are smaller than a millimetre. In 2007, scientists sent them into orbit on a European space mission and left them exposed to open space. Many came back alive, and some even laid eggs. Their trick is drying out and almost switching their bodies off until water returns.
