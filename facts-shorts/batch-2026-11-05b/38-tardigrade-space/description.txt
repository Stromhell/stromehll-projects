This tiny animal survived the vacuum of space.
Tardigrades are smaller than a millimetre. In 2007 they rode the FOTON-M3 mission and sat exposed to open space. Many came back alive, and some laid eggs. Their trick: drying out and switching off until water returns.
Sources: Jönsson et al., Current Biology (2008), tardigrades survive exposure to space; European Space Agency, FOTON-M3 mission.
