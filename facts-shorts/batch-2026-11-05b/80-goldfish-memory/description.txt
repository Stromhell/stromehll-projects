Goldfish don't have a three-second memory.
Experiments show they remember for months. They've been trained to push levers for food, swim to a sound, learn feeding times, and even swim through a small maze.
Sources: Plymouth University research on goldfish lever training (BBC News, 2003); Smithsonian Magazine, Goldfish memory myth.
