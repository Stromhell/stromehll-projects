Butterflies taste with their feet.
Their feet are covered in taste sensors. A female drums a leaf with her feet to taste it, and only lays eggs if it is the right food plant for her caterpillars.
Sources: Smithsonian, Butterflies taste with their feet; Briscoe et al., PNAS (2013), gustatory receptors in Heliconius butterfly legs.
