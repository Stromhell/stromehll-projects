## Voiceover (56 words)
Butterflies taste with their feet. Their feet are covered in tiny taste sensors. When a female lands on a leaf, she drums it with her feet to taste the plant. If it tastes right, she lays her eggs there, because that leaf will be her babies' first meal. So every landing is a little taste test.
