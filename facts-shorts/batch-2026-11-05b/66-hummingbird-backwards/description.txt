Hummingbirds can fly backwards.
They twist their wings in a figure eight, getting lift on the downstroke and the upstroke. Some beat about 80 times a second, so they can hover, go sideways and reverse.
Sources: Warrick, Tobalske & Powers, Nature (2005), aerodynamics of the hovering hummingbird; Smithsonian's National Zoo, Hummingbird facts.
