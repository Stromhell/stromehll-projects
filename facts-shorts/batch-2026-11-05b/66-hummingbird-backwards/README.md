## Voiceover (62 words)
Hummingbirds can fly backwards. Most birds only get lift when their wings push down. A hummingbird twists its wings in a figure eight, so it gets lift on the way down and on the way back up. Some beat their wings around eighty times a second. That's how they hover in one spot, fly sideways, and zoom backwards out of a flower.
