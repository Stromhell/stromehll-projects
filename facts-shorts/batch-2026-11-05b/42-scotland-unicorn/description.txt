Scotland's national animal is a unicorn. Seriously.
Scottish kings put the unicorn on their royal arms over 500 years ago as a symbol of purity and power. Legend said only a king could tame one, which is why it wears a chain.
Sources: VisitScotland, Why is the unicorn Scotland's national animal?; National Records of Scotland / Court of the Lord Lyon, Royal arms of Scotland.
