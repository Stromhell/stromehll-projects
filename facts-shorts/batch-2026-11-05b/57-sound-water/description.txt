Sound travels over four times faster in water than in air.
In air it moves about 340 m/s. In water, nearly 1,500 m/s, because packed molecules pass the vibration quicker. That's how whales call across oceans.
Sources: NOAA Ocean Service, How far does sound travel in the ocean?; Discovery of Sound in the Sea (University of Rhode Island).
