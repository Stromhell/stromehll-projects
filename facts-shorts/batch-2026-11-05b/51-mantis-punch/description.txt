This shrimp punches with the acceleration of a bullet.
The mantis shrimp swings its club so fast the water boils into bubbles. When they collapse they hit the prey again with heat and light. It can crack aquarium glass.
Sources: Patek, Korff & Caldwell, Nature (2004), deadly strike of the mantis shrimp; Natural History Museum (London), Mantis shrimp.
