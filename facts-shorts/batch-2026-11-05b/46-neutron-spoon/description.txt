One teaspoon of this star weighs a billion tonnes.
A neutron star is the crushed core of an exploded giant star. It squeezes more mass than the Sun into a ball about 20 km wide. A spoonful dropped on Earth would punch straight through the ground.
Sources: NASA Goddard, Neutron stars; NASA, NICER mission: neutron star size.
