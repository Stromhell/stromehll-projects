London burned for 4 days, and the death toll is a mystery.
The fire started in a bakery on Pudding Lane in 1666 and destroyed over 13,000 homes, St Paul's Cathedral and 87 churches. Only 6 deaths were recorded; historians think there were many more.
Sources: Museum of London, The Great Fire of London; UK National Archives, Great Fire of London.
