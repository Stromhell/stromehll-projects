## Voiceover (57 words)
Glass frogs have see-through bellies. Flip one over and you can watch its heart beating and its food being digested. When they sleep, they pull most of their red blood cells out of their blood and hide them in their liver. That makes them up to sixty one percent more transparent. Hungry birds look right through them.
