You can see this frog's heart beating through its belly.
Glass frogs have transparent bellies. When they sleep they hide most red blood cells in their liver, becoming up to 61% more transparent so birds look right through them.
Sources: Taboada et al., Science (2022), glassfrogs conceal blood in their liver; Duke University News (2022), glass frogs hide red blood cells.
