## Idea
Cutting an onion mixes sulfur chemicals with an enzyme, making an irritating gas that triggers tears.

## Voiceover (64 words)
Why do onions make you cry? Inside an onion, sulfur chemicals and an enzyme are kept apart. When your knife breaks the cells, they mix and make an irritating gas. That gas floats up and reaches your eyes. Nerves in your eyes feel the sting, so your tear glands flood them to wash it away. Tip: chill the onion first, and less gas escapes.
