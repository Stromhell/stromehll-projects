## Idea
Air molecules scatter short blue waves much more than red ones, so blue reaches us from every direction; at sunset the blue is scattered away.

## Voiceover (68 words)
Sunlight looks white, so why is the sky blue? White light is every colour mixed together. When it hits the tiny gas molecules in our air, short blue waves get bounced around far more than long red waves. So blue light reaches your eyes from every part of the sky. At sunset, the light crosses much more air, the blue gets scattered away, and the reds are left.
