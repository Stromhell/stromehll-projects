## 13-blue-sky
- Video: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zAkkG8u_TFsmm1wk8YpLnBiedm2Mz95W/view
- Cover: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1V4I_a9Z9iDVeHkYzlTyOayIDfDSCmQu6/view
- YouTube title: Sunlight is white… so why is the sky BLUE?
- Caption:

```
Sunlight is white. So where does all that blue come from?
Tiny gas molecules in the air bounce short blue light waves around far more than long red ones, so blue reaches your eyes from every part of the sky. At sunset the light crosses much more air, the blue is scattered away and the reds are left.
Sources: Royal Museums Greenwich; The Conversation.

- Caption:

```
Most food rots in days. Honey can last for years.
Honey has so little water that germs landing in it dry out and die. It's also acidic, and bees add an enzyme that slowly makes hydrogen peroxide, a natural germ killer. Keep the jar sealed so it can't soak up water.

- YouTube title: The Eiffel Tower GROWS every summer 🔥
- Caption:

```
The Eiffel Tower is taller in summer than in winter.
Iron grows a tiny bit when it heats up. Each metre only stretches a fraction of a millimetre, but over 300 metres it adds up: on the hottest days the tower can be up to 15 cm taller. In winter it shrinks back.
Sources: Interesting Engineering; FlaglerLive.

A banana is a berry. A strawberry isn't.
To a botanist, a true berry grows from one flower with one ovary and keeps its seeds inside the flesh: bananas, grapes, tomatoes, even watermelons. A strawberry grows from a flower with many ovaries, and the dots on the outside are tiny fruits, each holding a seed.

## 17-onion-tears
- Video: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lHCHEMW92FMfjwoGsYfG-lBRi76m8VJa/view
- Cover: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cRBT5gl_hmd2uAfIaF0jhtt9S7wW7Ky3/view
- YouTube title: Why onions make you CRY (and the trick to stop it)
- Caption:

```
Why does chopping an onion make you cry?
An onion keeps sulfur chemicals and an enzyme apart. Your knife breaks the cells, they mix and make an irritating gas that floats up to your eyes. Nerves feel the sting and your tear glands flood them. Tip: chill the onion first and less gas escapes.
Sources: The Conversation; Chemistry World.

- Caption:

```
One bolt of lightning is hotter than the surface of the Sun.
The Sun's surface is about 5,500°C. Lightning heats the air around it to about 30,000°C in a split second. That air blasts outward so fast it makes a shock wave, and that boom is thunder.

```
Why does your voice sound so weird on a recording?
When you talk you hear yourself two ways: through the air to your ears, and through the bones of your skull. Bones carry low notes well, so you sound deeper inside your head. A microphone only hears the air part, and that's how everyone else hears you.
