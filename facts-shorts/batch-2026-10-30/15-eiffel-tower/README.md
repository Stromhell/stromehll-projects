## Voiceover (66 words)
The Eiffel Tower is taller in summer than in winter. How? It's made of iron, and iron grows a tiny bit when it heats up. Each metre stretches only a fraction of a millimetre, but the tower is three hundred metres tall. Add it all up, and on the hottest days it can stand up to fifteen centimetres taller. Then in winter, it shrinks back down.
