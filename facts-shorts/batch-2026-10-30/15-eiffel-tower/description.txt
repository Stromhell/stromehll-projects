The Eiffel Tower is taller in summer than in winter.
Iron grows a tiny bit when it heats up. Each metre only stretches a fraction of a millimetre, but over 300 metres it adds up: on the hottest days the tower can be up to 15 cm taller. In winter it shrinks back.
