## Voiceover (63 words)
An octopus has three hearts and blue blood. Why? Two small hearts pump blood through the gills, and a bigger one sends it around the body. Its blood carries oxygen using copper, not iron, and that copper turns blue. But copper blood holds less oxygen than ours, so the octopus needs extra hearts to push it harder. Three hearts, one very blue octopus.
