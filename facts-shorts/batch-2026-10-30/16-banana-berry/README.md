## Idea
Botanically, bananas are berries but strawberries are not; a strawberry's dots are its real fruits.

## Voiceover (64 words)
A banana is a berry. A strawberry is not. To a botanist, a true berry grows from one flower with one ovary, and keeps its seeds inside the soft flesh. Bananas, grapes, tomatoes and even watermelons fit that rule. A strawberry grows from a flower with many ovaries, and those little dots on the outside are actually tiny fruits, each one holding a seed.
