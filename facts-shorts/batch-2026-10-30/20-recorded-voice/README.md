## Idea
You hear your own voice through air and through skull bones, which boost low notes; a recording only catches the air part.

## Voiceover (66 words)
Why does your voice sound weird on a recording? When you talk, you hear yourself two ways. Sound travels through the air to your ears, and it also vibrates through the bones of your skull. Those bones carry low notes really well, so your voice sounds deeper inside your head. A microphone only hears the air part. That thinner voice? That's how everyone else hears you.
