In the Dead Sea you float without even trying.
It's almost ten times saltier than the ocean, so the water is much heavier than your body. When you get in, you push aside water that weighs more than you do, and it shoves you back up. You bob like a cork. Just keep it out of your eyes.
Sources: How It Works; University of Illinois Physics Van.
