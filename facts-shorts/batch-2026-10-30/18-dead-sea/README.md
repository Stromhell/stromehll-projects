## Voiceover (63 words)
In the Dead Sea, you float without even trying. Why? It's almost ten times saltier than the ocean. All that dissolved salt makes the water much heavier than your body. When you get in, you push aside water that weighs more than you do, so it shoves you back up. You bob like a cork. Just never get that water in your eyes.
