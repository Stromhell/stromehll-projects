## Voiceover (63 words)
You've probably heard you can see the Great Wall of China from space. Astronauts say, not really. The wall is incredibly long, but in most places it's only a few metres wide, about as wide as a road. From orbit, hundreds of kilometres up, that's far too thin. And it's made of stone and earth, the same colour as the land around it.
