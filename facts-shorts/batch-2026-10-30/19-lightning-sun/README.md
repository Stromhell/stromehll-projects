## Idea

## Voiceover (68 words)
A single bolt of lightning is hotter than the surface of the Sun. The Sun's surface is about five and a half thousand degrees Celsius. Lightning can heat the air around it to about thirty thousand degrees, in a fraction of a second. That air blasts outward so fast it makes a shock wave, and that boom is thunder. Every thunderclap is air jumping out of the way.
