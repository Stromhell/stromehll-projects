## Idea
Honey is so dry, acidic and full of a bee enzyme that makes hydrogen peroxide that germs can't survive in it.

## Voiceover (61 words)
Most food rots in days. So why can honey last for years? Honey has very little water, so germs that land in it dry out and die. It's also quite acidic. And bees add an enzyme that slowly makes hydrogen peroxide, a natural germ killer. Keep the jar sealed so it can't soak up water, and honey basically never goes bad.
