## Idea
Why someone stranded at sea can't drink the water around them: kidneys can't make urine as salty as seawater, so every sip costs more water than it gives.

## Voiceover (70 words)
You're stuck on a raft in the middle of the ocean. Water everywhere. So why can't you just drink it? About three and a half percent of seawater is salt. Your kidneys have to flush that salt out, but they can't make pee as salty as the sea. So to clear the salt from one cup, you'd pee out more than a cup. Every sip leaves you thirstier than before.
