## Studio > Settings > Channel > Advanced
- Audience: "Review this setting for every video" (do NOT set the whole channel as made for kids)

## Why "not made for kids"
YouTube marks a video "made for kids" only when children are the PRIMARY audience. That setting turns off comments, the notification bell, end screens, cards and personalised ads, which kills growth for a new channel. DumDown targets teens plus young Marvel fans watching with family, so keep the content clean (no swearing, no adult gags) and aim titles and thumbnails at that audience, but leave the flag off.
