# DumDown: Deadpool & Wolverine, Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 15 min of narration (~2,200 VO words), plus 12 cutaway gags (~4 min). Target full length about 19-20 min.
**Voice:** FREE local Kokoro am_michael (same as Loki, Deadpool 2, Ironheart, Agatha). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices. No ElevenLabs except the thumbnail.
**Visuals:** original parody animation in the DumDown anime cel style over highly detailed painted backgrounds (a snowy North Dakota graveyard, a used-car lot, a tiny birthday apartment, the retro TVA office, a desert junkyard full of giant wrecks, a ruined theatre lair inside a giant skull, a broken-down rebel camp, a neon subway station). Cinematic camera and letterbox. No film footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every film line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** the English subtitle track in Pushpam's file (source/dw_transcript.txt).
**Tone (Pushpam 15 Nov 13:09 UTC):** adult jokes are OK, make it hella funny. Swearing and innuendo allowed; the hardest f-words are bleeped as a gag, and the first 30 seconds stay clean so YouTube ads still run. Gore is cartoon, off screen, or a pop of confetti.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s).
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, hotline, talk show, weather report...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen: "the Merc" in a red-and-black suit with our own mask shape (no film logo or belt emblem), "the Claw guy" in a yellow-and-blue suit with our own pointy cowl and three metal claws, a pale bald woman in a long black coat, a time-office boss in a beige suit and tie, a tiny chubby dog in a mini red suit, a girl with claws in her knuckles, a vampire hunter in a long leather coat, a card-throwing Cajun in a trench coat, a ninja woman in red, a flaming guy in a blue jumpsuit, a giant in a dome helmet. Names are said in VO only.

## 0:00 HOOK (cold open)

🎬 Night, snow falling on a lonely graveyard. A man in a red suit digs with a shovel, humming. Slow push in on a skeleton in a yellow suit at the bottom of the hole.
🎵 Soft, holy choir. Way too holy.

This man is digging up his favourite superhero. Not to say goodbye. Not to pay respects. He needs the skeleton. Because tonight, the skeleton is his weapon.

[SFX] Shovel clank. Record scratch.
[POP] "GRAVE ROBBING: TECHNICALLY A HOBBY"

🎬 The red-suited man holds up the skeleton like a puppet and waves its arm at the camera.

This is Deadpool and Wolverine. One dead hero, one hero who refuses to die, a hundred more Deadpools, and the meanest bald lady in the Marvel universe. I watched all of it so you don't have to. Let's get dumb.

## 0:40 THE GRAVE PARTY

🎬 Time-office soldiers in brown armour surround the grave. The Merc grabs the skeleton's arm and swings it like a weapon. Bones fly.
🎵 A cheesy 2000s boy-band pop song kicks in, very loud.

Meet Wade Wilson, aka Deadpool. Six seconds after he opens the coffin, a squad from the Time Variance Authority shows up to arrest him. So Wade does what any normal person would do. He beats all of them to death using his idol's body. Like a bone nunchuck.

[SFX] Bone crunches timed to the drums.
[POP] "THE SKELETON IS NOW CANONICALLY A WEAPON"

🃏 CARD: DEADPOOL. Type: MERC. Move: "Talk Until It Hurts". Weakness: boundaries.

Then he turns to us, covered in blood and snow, and says, look, I'm not proud of this. To understand why he's punching cops with a corpse, we need to go back a few years.

🤡 GAG 1: "TALES FROM THE CRYPT-O". A late-night investment ad. A sleazy host stands in a graveyard: "Why buy gold when you can dig up a legend? Bones are an appreciating asset!" The skeleton waves. Fine print: "Past heroes do not guarantee future returns."

## 1:50 THE WORST JOB INTERVIEW

🎬 A shiny tower office. The Merc in a suit and tie sits across from a nervous man in a security uniform holding a clipboard.
🎵 Soft corporate elevator music.

Six years ago, Wade walks into the Avengers tower to apply for a job. Not as a janitor. As an Avenger. His interviewer is Happy Hogan, a man who started his career as Iron Man's driver and still seems to be driving.

Wade's CV is a disaster. He leads with, he was once caught getting very personal with a toy Hulk hand in their lobby. His team experience? He started a team called X-Force. They all died on day one. The police say it was gravity.

[SFX] Pen stops writing. A single cricket chirps.

🎬 The interviewer stands up. The Merc stands up too, a little too fast.

And then Wade says the quiet part out loud. He doesn't want to help people. He needs to matter, so his girlfriend Vanessa will stay. Happy tells him, Avengers do the job because people need them, not because they need it. Aim for the middle, kid. Rejected.

[POP] "APPLICATION STATUS: LOL"

🤡 GAG 2: "LINKEDIN FOR MERCENARIES". A fake job site. The Merc's profile: "Skills: stabbing, talking, more talking. Endorsed by: nobody." A pop-up: "Your X-Force team members are no longer with the company."

## 3:20 USED CAR SALESMAN

🎬 A sad used-car lot under grey sky. The Merc in a polo shirt and a fake hair piece leans on a minivan, smiling at a family.
🎵 Cheap ukulele.

So Wade quits being a superhero. He sells used cars now with his best friend Peter, the most normal man in the multiverse. He pitches a minivan to a mom and tells her it doesn't suck. She asks if he has kids. He says no, and then explains exactly why, in detail. She does not buy the minivan.

Vanessa has left him. His roommate is Blind Al, an old blind lady who wants him dead and says so every morning. Honestly, the healthiest relationship he has.

🃏 CARD: PETER. Type: NORMAL GUY. Move: "Shows Up". Weakness: everything.

## 4:10 THE BIRTHDAY PARTY

🎬 A tiny apartment stuffed with balloons. Friends shout "SURPRISE!". The Merc stands in the doorway holding a grocery bag, stunned.
🎵 A wobbly kazoo version of Happy Birthday.

It's Wade's birthday, and his friends throw him a surprise party. Vanessa even shows up, which is awkward, because she has a new boyfriend. He likes hiking. He hasn't gotten her shot yet. Wade's toast is beautiful. He says he's the luckiest man alive, standing in a room with everyone he loves.

[SFX] Doorbell. Ding dong.

🎬 Soldiers in brown armour pour into the hallway. The Merc smiles, thinking they're party strippers.

Then the TVA kicks the door down. Wade thinks they're strippers Peter ordered. He tells them, take off everything, keep the helmets, and this is not Pretty Woman, we're kissing. They are not strippers. He fights them in the hallway anyway, because a birthday is a birthday.

[POP] "THIS IS NOT THE PARTY ENTERTAINMENT"

🤡 GAG 3: "RENT-A-COP STRIPPERS INC." A cheesy hotline ad with neon text. "Need a party? Our dancers come in uniform, and leave with your suspect!" The customer reviews pop up: "One star. They arrested my uncle."

## 5:30 WELCOME TO THE TVA

🎬 A retro office from the 1970s: orange carpet, tube monitors, beige filing cabinets. A man in a beige suit smiles too wide.
🎵 Funky 70s office jazz.

Wade wakes up at the TVA. The guy in charge is Mr. Paradox. Think of a middle manager who read one book on leadership and now thinks he's God. He tells Wade he's special. He's been chosen. He can leave his dying, low-budget universe and join the big one, the Sacred Timeline, the MCU itself.

🃏 CARD: MR. PARADOX. Type: MIDDLE MANAGER. Move: "Per My Last Email". Weakness: noses.

🎬 The Merc looks at a giant screen showing a heroic blond god crying in the future.

Wade sees a future clip of Thor crying in his arms, and loses his mind. He calls himself Marvel Jesus. He runs to a corner and yells, suck it, Fox, I'm going to Disneyland. Paradox gives him a brand new suit with fancy swords. It has a new car smell. Wade is ready to sign without reading. He doesn't read.

[SFX] Angels singing. Then a cash register.
[POP] "MARVEL JESUS (SELF-APPOINTED)"

## 7:00 THE ANCHOR BEING

🎬 A huge screen shows Wade's universe as a glowing ball, crumbling at the edges like a stale cookie.
🎵 Low, sad cello.

Then the catch. Wade's whole universe is dying. Every universe has an Anchor Being, one person so important that when they die, their world slowly falls apart. Wade's anchor was Logan, the Wolverine. And Logan died. Very nobly. In a movie that made everyone cry.

Normally, the universe would fade out over a few thousand years. But Paradox doesn't have time for that. He built a machine called the Time Ripper, a giant laser to wipe Wade's world out in three days. He calls it a mercy kill. Wade calls it Old Yeller-ing his friends.

[SFX] Machine hum rising.

🤡 GAG 4: "ANCHOR BEING DATING APP". A swipe-style screen. "Looking for someone so important the universe collapses without them? Swipe right!" Profile: a skeleton in yellow. Bio: "Dead. Still hot."

🎬 The Merc punches Paradox in the nose. Paradox's nose sprays blood like a cartoon sprinkler.

So Wade makes a choice. He'll find the Wolverine, and he'll break Paradox's nose. He does the nose part right away. Which brings us back to the grave. The skeleton was plan A. Plan A did not have a pulse.

[POP] "NOSE: BROKEN. PLAN: NONE."

## 9:10 SHOPPING FOR WOLVERINES

🎬 A fast montage: the Merc jumps through glowing portals into different worlds. A tiny short Claw guy, a muscly Claw guy with a moustache, a Claw guy in brown and tan, a very handsome Claw guy who looks like a famous Superman actor.
🎵 Montage rock, full blast.

Wade grabs the TVA's time remote and goes Wolverine shopping. He finds a tiny one, which he calls a comic-accurate short king. He finds one in the classic brown suit. He finds one so handsome he just bows and says, this feels right. None of them want to come.

[SFX] Portal whoosh, portal whoosh, punch, portal whoosh.

🤡 GAG 5: "WOLVERINE.COM". An online store. "Wolverines, now in Short, Tall, Hot, and Divorced!" A cart icon fills up. "Free shipping on orders of three or more claws." Checkout: "Out of stock: alive ones."

🎬 A dirty bar. A drunk Claw guy with a beard and messy hair is thrown out of the door into the street. The Merc catches him.

Then he finds this one. Drunk, angry, kicked out of a bar, wearing a yellow suit he clearly hates. Wade says, perfect, and drags him back by the collar.

🃏 CARD: WOLVERINE (THE WORST ONE). Type: BERSERKER. Move: "Snikt". Weakness: talking about feelings.

## 10:30 THE WORST WOLVERINE

🎬 Back at the TVA, Paradox laughs so hard he has to sit down.
🎵 A sad trombone.

Wade proudly presents his new Wolverine. Paradox laughs. Out of every Wolverine in the multiverse, Wade picked the worst one. This guy let down his whole world. Whatever he did, it's so bad that the TVA won't even say it out loud.

Wade begs. Paradox refuses. Wade threatens to tell Paradox's bosses about the illegal laser. Black belt in Karen. So Paradox opens a portal and throws both of them in the trash.

[SFX] Portal suck. A garbage truck beep.
[POP] "TAKE OUT THE TRASH: COMPLETED"

## 11:20 THE VOID

🎬 A huge desert junkyard under an orange sky. A giant wrecked Ant-Man helmet, a broken 20th Century logo shape in the sand, rusted cars.
🎵 Desert western guitar.

They land in the Void. It's the multiverse's dump. Anything useless goes here before it's eaten by Alioth, a giant purple storm cloud with teeth that you might remember from Loki. Wade looks around and says, looks kind of Mad Max-y. But that would be copyright infringement, right?

🎬 The two heroes fight in a wrecked car. Claws through the door, swords through the roof. A big wide shot of the car rolling.

Of course, they immediately fight. Logan is mad that Wade lied to him. Wade is mad that Logan exists. The fight goes on and on, through a car, through a sand dune, until someone threatens to cut off a head and see if it grows back. They only stop when Wade promises the TVA can fix Logan's world too. Spoiler, he has no idea.

[SFX] Clang, slash, groan, car horn.

🤡 GAG 6: "THE VOID: TRIP ADVISOR". A travel-review website. "One star. Ate by a cloud." "Two stars. Great sunsets, terrible bald lady." "Five stars. I'm a 20th Century logo and finally I'm at peace."

## 13:00 CASSANDRA NOVA

🎬 A giant skull-shaped lair built from the wreck of a giant hero's head. Inside, a pale bald woman in a black coat sits on a throne of junk. A big guy in a dome helmet stands behind her.
🎵 Creepy music box.

The Void has a queen. Her name is Cassandra Nova, and she is the evil twin of Professor X. She tried to strangle him in the womb. She was born, her own words, through the back door. She uses her powers by sticking her fingers inside your head. Literally. Into your skull. Like a bowling ball.

🃏 CARD: CASSANDRA NOVA. Type: PSYCHIC. Move: "Finger In Your Brain". Weakness: none, which is the problem.

🎬 A flaming guy in a blue jumpsuit kneels before her, sweating.

Also in the room is Johnny Storm, the Human Torch, played by the actor who was Captain America. Wade, trying to be cool, tells Cassandra a long, very creative, very rude speech that Johnny supposedly said about her. Johnny did not say it. Cassandra kills him anyway. Wade's response? Hey, we're all grieving. Also, do you know what he was doing to the budget?

[SFX] A pop, like a balloon. A sad kazoo.
[POP] "RIP JOHNNY. YOU WERE TOO HOT FOR THIS MOVIE."

🤡 GAG 7: "COMPLAINT HOTLINE FOR DEAD CAMEOS". A tired operator. "Hi, you've reached the Cameo Complaints Line. If you were killed in under five minutes, press one. If you came back from 2005 just for this, press two." Hold music: a sad kazoo.

## 14:40 A ROAD TRIP WITH FEELINGS

🎬 A beat-up minivan drives through the desert at sunset. The Merc at the wheel, the Claw guy in the passenger seat, a chubby little dog in a red suit in the back.
🎵 Soft road-trip guitar.

They escape with the help of Nicepool, a Deadpool so nice he is basically a golden retriever with guns. He has long hair, a baby, and a dog in a tiny Deadpool suit, called Dogpool. He lends them his minivan. Wade hates minivans. He says owning one is like a disease making a comeback.

🃏 CARD: DOGPOOL. Type: GOOD GIRL. Move: "Lick". Weakness: belly rubs.

🎬 Inside the van, the Claw guy holds up a photo of Wade's friends and goes quiet.

On the drive, the truth comes out. Wade never knew if the TVA could fix Logan's world. He just said so to get help. He calls it an educated wish. Logan calls him a liar, and the worst person he has ever met. They fight again, inside the minivan, while it drives itself. And honestly? The minivan handles great.

[SFX] Punch, crash, a soft car voice: "Lane assist activated."
[POP] "THIS IS COUPLES THERAPY NOW"

🤡 GAG 8: "HONDA ODYSSEY CRASH TEST". A serious car commercial. Two crash-test dummies in red and yellow fight inside the van. "Five stars in safety, even while your passenger stabs you." Fine print: "Do not stab your passenger."

## 16:20 THE FORGOTTEN HEROES

🎬 A rebel camp in an old ruined building. A vampire hunter in leather, a card-throwing Cajun in a trench coat, a ninja woman in red, and a girl with claws stand around a fire.
🎵 Heroic horns, a little rusty.

The minivan crash lands at the rebel camp. Here live the heroes the world forgot. Blade, the vampire hunter. Elektra, the ninja. Gambit, the card thrower nobody can understand, which is the joke. And Laura, aka X-23, the girl Logan died to save in his own universe.

🃏 CARD: LAURA. Type: CLAWS. Move: "Stabs Feelings". Weakness: none.

🎬 The Cajun throws a glowing playing card. It explodes. Everyone claps politely.

They all feel the same. Their movies got cancelled, their worlds forgot them, and they never got a proper ending. Wade tells them, this is your chance to get a legacy. Big slow-motion fights, sad music, who knows if you live or die. They all go, yes, let's do it.

[SFX] A card fizz, a small boom.
[POP] "CANCELLED, BUT FAMOUS"

🤡 GAG 9: "THE CANCELLED HEROES SUPPORT GROUP". Folding chairs in a circle. A sign: "WE STILL MATTER". A guy says, "I had a trilogy planned." Everyone nods. Gambit says something nobody understands. Everyone nods harder.

## 18:00 LOGAN'S SECRET

🎬 Night. The Claw guy sits alone on a broken wall. The girl with claws walks up to him.
🎵 Lonely piano.

That night, Laura talks to Logan. She says, you remind me of my Logan. Angry, drunk, mean. But he showed up when it mattered most.

And Logan finally tells the truth. In his world, the X-Men begged him to join the team, and he said no. One day, while he was off drinking, humans came and killed them all. He wears the suit they made for him to remember them. And the reason the TVA calls him the worst Wolverine is how he took his revenge. It was so bad the whole world turned against mutants.

[SFX] Wind. One soft guitar note.

## 19:10 THE BIG RAID

🎬 The rebels charge across the desert toward the skull lair. Explosions, sand, a slow-motion jump by the vampire hunter.
🎵 Epic heroic theme, huge drums.

The plan is to steal the helmet off Cassandra's giant guard, Juggernaut, since it blocks psychic powers. The heroes attack, and they give it everything. Blade kills a whole crowd. Gambit throws a burning deck of cards. Elektra cuts through an army. They get their ending. Most of them do not get a sequel.

🎬 The Claw guy jams the dome helmet onto Cassandra's head. She looks annoyed.

Logan shoves the helmet onto Cassandra. She can't use her powers. And then Logan does something surprising. He doesn't kill her. He says he's wearing the suit, which means he's an X-Man, and her brother would never want her to die. Cassandra is touched for a moment, and then opens a portal using Doctor Strange's ring, which she took off his dead body after wearing his skin. She is a lot.

[SFX] Portal fire sparks.
[POP] "THIS IS HOW YOU GET UNINVITED FROM FAMILY DINNER"

🤡 GAG 10: "SIBLING RIVALRY COURT". A judge show. "Your Honour, she tried to strangle me in the womb." "I was a baby!" "You were a FETUS!" The judge bangs the gavel: "Both of you need therapy."

## 21:00 RETURN TO EARTH

🎬 Back at the used-car lot. The two heroes stumble out of a portal next to a shiny new minivan.
🎵 Triumphant guitar riff.

Wade and Logan escape back to Earth. But Cassandra follows them. She walks into the TVA's secret base, sticks her fingers in Paradox's brain, and decides to use his giant laser not just on Wade's world, but on every world in the multiverse. All of them. Until only the Void is left. Ambitious.

🎬 A subway station filled with a hundred Deadpools of every shape: a kid, a cowboy, a lady, a baby, a giant head.
🎵 A very loud polka.

And she calls in backup. The Deadpool Corps. A hundred Deadpools, including a little kid Deadpool who swears like a sailor, a Ladypool, a Babypool, and a Headpool. Wade looks at them and says, they're dumb, but they can fight. Nicepool, who came along to get his dog back, gets shot in the first five seconds, because he doesn't regenerate. Nobody told him. Wade takes his golden guns, and then it's two guys against a hundred Wades.

🤡 GAG 11: "THE DEADPOOL CORPS RECRUITMENT AD". A proud army ad. "Do you talk too much? Do you have no boundaries? Do you regenerate? Join the Corps!" A tiny baby Deadpool salutes. "Uniform comes in red, red, and red."

## 22:50 THE TWO-MAN ARMY

🎬 A huge wide shot of a subway platform. The Merc and the Claw guy fight back to back through a hundred red figures. Slow-motion. A pop song plays.
🎵 A cheesy 2000s love song, slow and dramatic.

What follows is the greatest one-take fight in DumDown history. Two guys, a hundred Deadpools, nobody can die, so it lasts forever. Eventually, Peter, the used-car guy, walks in, and every Deadpool stops. Every Deadpool universe has a Peter. Peter is a legend. They let them through.

[SFX] A hundred swords drop at once.
[POP] "PETER: THE TRUE ANCHOR BEING"

## 24:00 THE SACRIFICE

🎬 Down in a glowing chamber under the station, two giant energy beams crackle. The Merc stands in the middle, arms out.
🎵 Sad violins, a heartbeat.

To stop the laser, someone has to stand between two energy beams and get turned into dust. Wade volunteers. He says, I'm not doing it because I need it, I'm doing it because they need me. Which is literally what Happy told him in the first scene. Character growth! Then Logan joins him, because two idiots are better than one.

🎬 The two heroes stand in a blinding white light, holding hands. The screen goes white.

And they live. Because if you put a Deadpool and a Wolverine together, holding hands, listening to Madonna, they are basically unkillable.

[SFX] A huge explosion. Then a soft bird chirp.

🤡 GAG 12: "THE HAND-HOLDING ENERGY DRINK". A sports drink ad. Two gym bros in red and yellow hold hands. "Survive anything with the power of friendship!" A scientist in a lab coat says, "This makes no sense," and then holds hands with his intern.

## 25:10 THE ENDING

🎬 The TVA office. A woman in a sharp suit arrests the man in the beige suit. The Merc and the Claw guy stand behind her, bruised.
🎵 Soft hopeful strings.

The TVA's boss arrests Paradox for his illegal laser. And the best news? Wade's world doesn't need Logan anymore. It's not dying. Wade asked if the TVA could fix Logan's past. They say, there's nothing to fix. His past made him who he is.

🎬 A small apartment. Wade, Logan, Blind Al, Peter, Vanessa and Dogpool sit around a table eating takeout.

Logan moves into Wade's world. Blind Al says hi. Dogpool licks everyone. And Wade finally understands that he doesn't need to be an Avenger to matter. He just needs to look around the table.

[POP] "LESSON LEARNED (FOR ONCE)"
