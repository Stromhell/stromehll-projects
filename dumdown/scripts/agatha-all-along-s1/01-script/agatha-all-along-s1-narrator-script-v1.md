# DumDown: Agatha All Along Season 1, Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 14 min of narration (~2,000 VO words), plus 12 cutaway gags (~4 min). Target full length about 19-20 min.
**Voice:** FREE local Kokoro am_michael (same as Loki, Deadpool 2 and Ironheart). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices. No ElevenLabs except the thumbnail.
**Visuals:** original parody animation in the DumDown anime cel style over highly detailed painted backgrounds (a foggy small town, a cosy cul-de-sac house, a dark forest road, a 70s beach house, a rock studio, a 80s cabin, a tarot tower, a basement garden). Cinematic camera and letterbox. No show footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every show line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** the English subtitle tracks in Pushpam's 9 episode files (source/Agatha_S01_all.txt).
**Tone:** PG-13. Deaths are off screen or "she's gone". Swearing is bleeped as a gag.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s).
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, hotline, talk show, weather report...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen: "the purple witch" in a long violet coat with a high collar and a silver brooch (our own shape), a goth teen in a hoodie and a sigil glowing on his face, a fortune-teller with layered scarves, a potions witch in a pink tracksuit, a security guard with a lightning tattoo, a cheerful suburban mom with a gardening hat, and a woman in a dark green leafy cloak with a sharp smile. Names are said in VO only.

## 0:45 DETECTIVE AGNES

🎬 A small-town police station. A cranky chief with a smoothie stain on his tie. Our detective stares at him.
🎵 Jazzy cop-show bass.

Meet Agnes. Detective in the town of Westview. She's been suspended for punching a suspect, she plays Candy Crush on the job, and she has just found a dead woman in a creek. The only clue is a library card from a book that was stolen three years ago.

Then the FBI sends a fancy agent who keeps asking Agnes weird questions. Like "do you remember why you hate me?" And "isn't it funny how the body just magically appeared?"

🃏 CARD: AGATHA HARKNESS. Type: WITCH. Move: "Steal Your Power". Weakness: having feelings.

Here's the thing. Agnes is Agatha Harkness. The villain from WandaVision. At the end of that show, Wanda trapped her in a spell and made her a nosy neighbour. Now the spell turned into a crime drama, because the brain does what it wants.

🤡 GAG 1: "LAW & ORDER: WITCH VICTIMS UNIT". A gritty TV title card with a "DUN DUN" sting. A grim voice: "In the world of magic, crimes are investigated by two groups. The witches who did it, and the witches who forgot they did it." Narrator: "This is a real genre now."
