# DumDown: Ironheart Season 1, Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 15 min of narration (~2,300 VO words), plus 12 cutaway gags (~4 min). Target full length about 19-20 min.
**Voice:** FREE local Kokoro am_michael (same as Loki and Deadpool 2). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices. No ElevenLabs except the thumbnail.
**Visuals:** original parody animation in the DumDown anime cel style over highly detailed painted backgrounds (Chicago South Side, a garage, a greenhouse farm, a mansion). Cinematic camera and letterbox. No show footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every show line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** the English subtitle track in Pushpam's Season 1 file (source/Ironheart_S01_all.txt, all 6 episodes, 4 h 46 min).
**Tone:** PG-13. Deaths are off screen or "he's gone". Swearing is bleeped as a gag.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s).
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, hotline, talk show, weather report...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen: "the girl genius" in a chunky red-and-gold home-made armour (our own shape, no arc-reactor logo), a blue hologram best friend, a man in a smoky red hooded cloak, a guy in khakis who later turns half-robot, a teen witch, and a smiling man in a suit who orders way too much pizza. Names are said in VO only.

🃏 CARD: RIRI WILLIAMS. Type: GENIUS. Move: "Because I Can". Weakness: asking for help.
