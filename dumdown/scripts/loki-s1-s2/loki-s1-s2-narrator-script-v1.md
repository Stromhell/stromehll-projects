# DumDown: Loki Seasons 1 + 2, Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 20 min of narration (~2,900 words), plus 12 cutaway gags (~5 min). Target full length about 25-26 min.
**Voice:** FREE local voice only (Pushpam 13 Nov: no ElevenLabs). Narrator = Kokoro TTS (open-source, runs on our machine). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices.
**Visuals:** original parody animation in the DumDown anime cel style over highly detailed painted backgrounds. Cinematic camera and letterbox. No show footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every show line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** the English subtitles inside Pushpam's S1 episode files (the .srt files in the folder are Malayalam) and a speech-to-text transcript of the S2 file.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s).
- `🃏 CARD` = Pokémon-style trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, helpline, talk show, nature doc...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change to match the scene. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody names on screen: TVA = "Time Variance Authority" is said in VO, but on-screen props use our own designs (no Marvel logos). Miss Minutes = an orange cartoon clock mascot of our own design.

## 0:00 HOOK (cold open, before anything else)

🎬 A golden throne floating in total darkness. Glowing green branches of time coil around it like a giant tree. A tiny figure in a horned helmet sits on it, alone, forever. Slow push in on his tired eyes.
🎵 Low, huge choir drone.

This man used to be the worst brother in the universe. He stabbed people, lied to everyone, and once threw a whole alien army at New York. And by the end of this story... he is literally holding the entire multiverse together. Forever. Alone. On a chair.

[SFX] Record scratch. 🎵 cuts.

How does a guy go from "kneel before me" to "I'll carry everything so you don't have to"? Two seasons, twelve episodes, about a hundred Lokis, one alligator, and a cartoon clock that is way scarier than it has any right to be.

I watched all of it so you don't have to. Grab a snack. Let's get dumb.

He grabs it. Zap. Gone.

🎬 A huge 1970s-style office building with no windows. Endless desks, beige walls, old computers, pneumatic tubes.

They drag him to the TVA headquarters, which looks like a tax office from 1974 that never closed. And Loki, a god, a prince, a would-be king of Earth, has to stand in line. Take a ticket. Fill in forms. Get his clothes "confiscated". The god of mischief has entered his final boss: paperwork.

[POP] "Now serving: ticket 000,000,001."

🎬 A small animated cartoon clock on an old TV, waving.
🃏 CARD: MISS MINUTES. Type: CLOCK? Move: "Orientation Video". Weakness: unknown. Danger: suspiciously high.

Then he gets the orientation video, hosted by a cheerful cartoon clock called Miss Minutes. She explains that long ago there was a huge multiversal war, and then three wise space lizards, the Time-Keepers, showed up and fixed everything into one single Sacred Timeline. 🎬 A courtroom with a judge high up on a tower desk.
🃏 CARD: JUDGE RAVONNA RENSLAYER. Type: BOSS. Move: "Guilty". Weakness: secrets.

Loki stands trial in front of Judge Ravonna Renslayer, and he tries the move that always works on Earth: he blames the Avengers. The judge does not care. Guilty. Reset him.

And right before he gets erased... a man in a grey suit walks in and says, wait. I want this one.

🎬 A tired man with a moustache, a coffee and a stack of files.
🃏 CARD: AGENT MOBIUS. Type: ANALYST. Move: "Jet Ski Dream". Favourite snack: salad from the cafeteria.

This is Mobius. He's an agent, he loves jet skis even though he has never seen one in real life, and he is the most "dad at a barbecue" person in the whole Marvel universe. Mobius sits Loki down in the Time Theatre and shows him his own life like a Netflix show.

🎬 A film screen. Loki watches his own greatest hits: the mother he lost, the brother he betrayed, and finally a huge purple hand around his throat.

He sees his mum die. He sees himself make up with Thor. And then he sees himself get killed by Thanos. Every bad thing he's done, every good thing he could have done, already written. He wasn't born a villain, he was born a *character*. And the script was written by somebody else.

Oh, and fun fact from the same screening: Loki was D.B. Cooper. The famous plane robber who jumped out of a plane with the money in 1971 and was never found? Yeah. That was him. He lost a bet with Thor.

🎬 In a drawer in the TVA, a clerk opens a desk. Inside: six glowing Infinity Stones, rolling around next to staplers.

And then Loki finds out the TVA has a drawer full of Infinity Stones. The stones that killed half the universe? Here, they're paperweights. Thanos spent ten years collecting them and some intern uses one to hold down a coffee cup.

[POP] "Six stones. Zero respect."

So Loki joins the TVA. Not because he's good, but because Mobius needs help catching a much more dangerous target. A Loki who is killing Minutemen across time.

Places that get destroyed anyway, like Pompeii or a hurricane, so nothing he does there can create a branch. Big brain move. Mobius takes him to Pompeii to test the theory, and Loki screams into a volcano town about the end of the world to see if it changes anything. It doesn't. Everyone's about to be lava anyway.

🎬 Pompeii street. Loki on a cart, yelling. A volcano erupting. A Roman guy shrugs.

[SFX] Volcano boom. Distant "AAAH".

The Variant is a woman. A female Loki. She calls herself Sylvie. She drops dozens of TVA bombs across time all at once, the timeline explodes into branches like a broken firework, and she escapes through a door. And Loki, instead of stopping her... jumps in after her.
