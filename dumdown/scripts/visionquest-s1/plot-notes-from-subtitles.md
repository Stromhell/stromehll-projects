Source: English subtitle track of Pushpam's season file (watched 12 Nov). Episode bounds from credit gaps: E1 0:00-0:48, E2 0:53-1:33, E3 1:38-2:25, E4 2:30-3:14, E5 3:18-3:59, E6 4:04-4:53, E7 ~4:58-5:55 (NO subtitles in file; frames only), E8 6:00-7:07.

## E1
- Cold open: white Vision monologue "Who am I? I am Vision. At least I was." Recap Ultron, Stark/Banner, AIs (Friday, DUM-E, U, Jarvis, Edith), Wanda, Thanos, Westview. "Same body, different mind."
- Edinburgh pub: Vision disguised as human barman "Sherwood Ellis". Boss/friend Fiona Guthrie (calls him "my odd wee duck", invites him to Sunday roast). He threw out his back ("iliocostalis lumborum is attempting to murder me"). Red-haired lass he used to come in with -> "crushed under the weight of a mountain".
- Home = mind space house with AIs: Jarvis (butler, "older sibling card, played 1,382 times this month"), Friday (social coach: smiled too much, customer thought he wanted to shag his wife), Edith (teen thrill-seeker, skydive Rubik's cube), DUM-E and U (bots). Ultron locked in the attic, delivers fake food as human avatar ("Sesame chicken calzone", "Zero stars, lousy tip"). Vision rewatches WandaVision memory. Ultron: "You're a ghost haunting her footsteps." "Not her footsteps, his." Ultron 50/50: visit the Louvre or burn them all.
- Ultron secretly sends data package "Ultron-five". Pub attacked by mercs; Fiona killed. Vision kills them ("Two down... fourteen down"). Edith: "Hey V, skylight exit?"
- Bounty hunter (later named Paladin) with drone helper "Snipey", tech sidekick (female voice) and a S.W.O.R.D.-encrypted client with weird voice: needs Vision intact. "Purple space sac" Thanos joke.
- Mind space: tea "Earl Grey with computational debuffs"; Edith "car 13 takes checkered flag", "you can't drive stick". Council with Ultron (IKEA psilocybin decor, Dorothy Parker/Oscar Wilde). Vision wants to go offline. Ultron confesses he leaked location to the hunter "the Paladin" via email ("carrier pigeon"). Punishment: boredom.
- Paladin's team traces "Sherwood Ellis" -> Ellis St x Sherwood Ave, Westview NJ -> S.W.O.R.D. files on WandaVision, twins Billy & Tommy.
- London market: Thomas Shepherd (white hair, grifter, super fast) and Lisa (pickpocket friend) con with a watch. TV news: Vision reappears. Tommy panic attack ("Your name is Thomas Shepherd. Is it?"). Paladin snatches Tommy. Broadcast to Vision: "This is not a test. I am the emergency... I have your son." "Mazel tov! It's a boy."
## E2 "Theseus"
- Police psych tape: pool incident, "someone else moved into my brain". Prison bus to Saint Mabyn's sanitarium, guard sings Christmas songs; Lisa is on the bus (arrested lifting a cop's wallet). Bus breaks (Tommy's power?), they jump from moving bus; "Jingle Bells"; Tommy says name "Thomas". Paladin catches Tommy, leaves him in Saint Mabyn's.
- Paladin lore: centuries-long line of hunters, unbreakable contract. Tommy's memories: haunted house with laugh track, meatloaves, red-faced father "with kind eyes", "Grief is just love persevering" (only Wanda + Vision knew that line).
- Paladin meets Vision at the Edinburgh pub (scene of Fiona's death). Vision computed quintillion simulations. Paladin theory: Wanda twitched her nose and threw the kid into a London teen. Threatens Saint Mabyn's shock therapy "control-alt-delete, goodbye Tommy". Vision: "I'm not Vision." Throws/strangles Paladin.
- Butcherbird killer news (wings etched on floors). Ultron true-crime junkie. Ship of Theseus: Ultron "it's bullshit... When a monster has your son, don't be the ship. Be the goddamn king."
- Hospital raid: Edith fashion clues (tac boots, military watches, earpieces). Calypso song. EMP cage trap; Adamantium rounds. Vision drained; Tommy refuses to leave him ("you're my... something"). Jarvis deletes himself to give power: "Family is forever, and it's worth the fight." Ultron: "Like father, like son."

## E5
