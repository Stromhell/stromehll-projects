# DumDown: Doctor Doom's Entire Life, Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 17 min of narration (~2,500 VO words), plus 10 cutaway gags (~3.5 min). Target full length about 20-21 min.
**Voice:** free local Kokoro am_michael (same as every DumDown video since Loki). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices. No ElevenLabs except the thumbnail.
**Look (Pushpam 20 Nov):** copy the viewing experience of the reference video (youtu.be/ZfffqOOlLAY): flat 2D cartoon with soft gradient shading, big round eyes, rich stylised backgrounds, drifting particles in every shot, glowing magic, lowercase name labels with an arrow. All designs are our own (see source/reference-style-study.md). Shot types from the reference (two-shot, big-face intro, wide establishing, back view toward a sky event, object hero shot, space wide, symbolic close-up) plus DumDown's cinematic camera. No film or comic art, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Story told in our own words.
**Why now:** Doom is the villain of Avengers: Doomsday (in cinemas 18 Dec 2026), so search interest will climb all December.
**Tone:** family-friendly and playful. Cartoon action only. The demon parts stay spooky-cute (red smoke, a grinning shadow with horns), never scary or gory.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene (new picture every 5-10 s), shot type in brackets.
- `🏷` = lowercase name label with arrow.
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = cutaway gag (fake ad, hotline, talk show...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen (original, no comic logos or emblems): "Doom" = tall, silver-grey armour, plain riveted metal mask with slit eyes, green hooded cloak, gold belt clasp; "young Victor" = messy dark hair, brown traveller's cloak; "the Healer" = his dad, grey beard, green apron; "the Witch" = his mum, dark hair, purple shawl, glowing hands; "the Baron" = fat little man, curly moustache, too many medals; "Valeria" = girl from the camp, red headscarf, braid; "Reed" = tall smiley student, brown hair with grey temples later, blue jumper, very long arms; "the Big Rock guy" = orange rocky body, blue shorts; "the Invisible Lady" = blonde bob, blue suit, sometimes see-through; "the Torch kid" = spiky hair, flames; "the Chrome Surfer" = smooth silver man on a silver board; "the Planet Eater" = giant purple helmet seen only as a shadow; "the Beyonder" = a glowing white silhouette with an afro of stars; "the Mind Guy" = purple-skinned man in a purple suit; "the Wizard" = cape with a tall collar, goatee, grey temples; "the Demon King" = red shadow with horns and a big grin; "the Keeper" = calm bald man in a green-black suit. Names are said in VO and shown only on the lowercase labels.

🎵 Low choir hum, slow.

There is a throne at the centre of the universe. Not a nice universe. A patchwork one, stitched together from leftovers, like a quilt your gran made out of old curtains.

🎬 [Slow push-in, low angle] Doom sits on the throne, cloak spread, fingers tapping the armrest.
🏷 "doctor doom"

The man on it is Doctor Doom. He made every rock, every ocean and every star here himself. He is, and I cannot stress this enough, a god.

🎬 [Extreme close-up] The metal mask. Eye slits. A tiny reflection of a star.
[SFX] Metal creak.

And he is still wearing the mask. Under it, his face is scarred. He could fix it with one thought. He hasn't. Why would a man who can do anything keep the one thing he hates most?

[POP] "PLOT HOLE? OR CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT?"

To find out, we have to leave the throne and go back a long way. To a tent. On a mountain. With a very small boy in it. I read all of it so you don't have to. Let's get dumb.

## 1:00 A TENT, NOT A CASTLE

🎬 [Wide establishing, slow pan] A camp of painted tents in snowy mountains at dusk. Lanterns, cooking fire, string of little flags. Fireflies.
🎵 Warm folk guitar.

Most kings are born in castles. Victor von Doom was born in a tent. His people were travellers, living in the mountains of a tiny country called Latveria. They were poor, and the man in charge hated them.

🎬 [Two-shot, push-in] Inside the tent: the Healer with a big grin, the Witch cradling a baby. Rugs, a violin on the wall.
🏷 "werner (dad)" · "cynthia (mum)"

His dad, Werner, was a healer. Kind, patient, the sort of guy who says "this won't hurt" and it actually doesn't. His mum, Cynthia, was a witch. Not kind. Not patient. Very tired of soldiers kicking her family around.

🃏 CARD: CYNTHIA. Type: WITCH. Move: "Bad Deal". Weakness: reading the small print.

🎬 [Low angle, red light] Cynthia alone in the snow, hands glowing. A red shadow with horns and a big grin rises out of the smoke.
[SFX] Deep whoosh, demon chuckle.

So she made a deal with a demon king called Mephisto. Power to protect her people. He said yes. Demons always say yes. The power was real, but so was the price. Cynthia didn't make it, and the demon kept her soul.

[POP] "ALWAYS READ THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS"

🤡 GAG 1: "MEPHISTO'S DEAL OF THE DAY". A shopping channel. The Demon King in a shiny red suit with a headset: "Unlimited power! Just three easy payments of... your soul! Operators are standing by, forever!" A tiny legal disclaimer scrolls so fast it smokes.

## 5:40 THE MATHS MISTAKE

🎬 [Close-up] Victor's eyes narrow. Then: Reed pushed out of the room, door slam.

Victor had two choices. Check the numbers, or throw Reed out. He threw Reed out.

Nobody knows how bad the scar is. Some say his face was wrecked. Some say it was one tiny scratch, and for Victor, one flaw was already too many. But he was sure of one thing. His maths was perfect. So someone must have changed it. And only one person had seen it.

🎬 [Close-up] Reed, innocent, holding a mug that says "WORLD'S OKAYEST STUDENT".

Reed. Victor has blamed Reed for this every day for the rest of his life. Reed was not involved.

## 7:30 THE HOT MASK

🎬 [Extreme wide] Snowy peaks of Tibet. A tiny figure collapses in the snow by a monastery gate.
🎵 Low throat-singing drone, wind.

Victor wandered the world looking for anyone who could teach him more. He ended up in the mountains of Tibet and passed out in the snow. Some monks found him, took him in and taught him their old secrets of magic and metal. Pretty soon he knew more than they did, so they started calling him master.

🎬 [Medium] A stone forge. A monk hammers a glowing orange mask on an anvil. Sparks fly.
[SFX] Clang, clang, sizzle.

Then he asked them for one thing: a suit of armour, and an iron mask. The monks made the mask and said: it's still hot, give it a minute.

🎬 [Close-up, slow push] Victor's hands lift the glowing mask toward his face. The monk's eyes go wide.

He did not give it a minute.

[SFX] Big hiss. Monk gasps.
[POP] "PATIENCE: 0. DRAMA: 100."

🎵 Big brass hit.

Whatever his face looked like before, it doesn't look like that now. That day, he dropped the name Victor and picked a name to scare the whole world. Doctor Doom.

🃏 CARD: DOCTOR DOOM. Type: GENIUS SORCERER KING. Move: "It Was Reed's Fault". Weakness: Reed.

Fun fact: Doom is his actual last name. He didn't even have to think of it. Also, he never finished university. He is not a doctor. Nobody has ever been brave enough to bring this up.

🤡 GAG 4: "IS HE A REAL DOCTOR?" A fake medical hotline. A nervous caller: "Hi, my arm hurts, can Doctor Doom help?" Doom on the line: "I can replace it with a robot arm." Caller: "...I'll see my regular doctor." Doom: "THERE IS NO OTHER DOCTOR."

## 22:20 THE ONE TRUE SENTENCE

🎬 [Wide] A tiny spaceship lands in a field. Reed Richards climbs out, older, grey temples.
🎵 Hopeful strings.

Then a small ship landed. A few survivors from the old worlds had slept inside it for eight years. One of them was Reed Richards.
