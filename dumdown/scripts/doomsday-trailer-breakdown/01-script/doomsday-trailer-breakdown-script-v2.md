Character entrances. Every new character arrives with a transition (portal flash, whip pan, door, smoke, lightning) and a 1-line recap card ("🃏 CARD") of who they are and where we last saw them.

Gags. Strong setup + punchline, act-outs, clean [CUT] in and out, 1.7 s hold after the laugh, no early cuts.

Voices. Narrator = DumDown narrator voice; every gag character a NATURAL human-sounding voice (no robotic pitch-shift). Voice QA on every stem.
