# DumDown: The Marvels, Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 15 min of narration (~1,800 VO words), plus 10 cutaway gags (~3.5 min). Target full length about 18-19 min.
**Voice:** FREE local Kokoro am_michael (same as every DumDown video since Loki). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices. No ElevenLabs except the thumbnail.
**Visuals:** the usual DumDown cel character style. Every scene mixes shot sizes: extreme wide establishing, medium, close-up, extreme close-up on eyes or hands, low-angle hero shots, over-the-shoulder, crash zooms and whip pans. Characters belong to the scene and use action poses (fly, punch, dodge, fall, run). Highly detailed painted backgrounds (a dusty moon with ruins, a teen bedroom full of fan art, a lonely spaceship cockpit, a space station ring above Earth, a cosy Jersey City living room, a green refugee planet, a dark throne hall on a dying planet with a red sun, a singing ocean planet with pink palaces, a space station corridor full of kittens, the surface of the Sun). Cinematic camera. No film footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every film line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** the English subtitle track in Pushpam's file (source/mv_transcript.txt). Source is research only.
**Tone:** family-friendly and playful (DumDown rule after Spider-Man: Brand New Day). No swearing, no adult jokes, cartoon action only. Monica's mum passing away is said gently.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s), with the shot type in brackets.
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, hotline, talk show, weather report...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen (original, no film logos or emblems, no star symbols): "the Captain" = short blonde hair, navy-and-red flight suit with a plain gold chest stripe, glowing fists; "the Light Lady" = curly black hair in a puff, white-and-navy suit, glowing blue eyes; "the Jersey Kid" = teen with dark ponytail, red tunic, blue leggings, red scarf, a gold bangle; "the Kree Queen" = tall woman, short black hair shaved at the sides, black-and-blue armour, a big glowing hammer, a gold bangle; "Eyepatch" = bald man, eyepatch, long black coat; "Goose" = fluffy ginger cat (tentacles come out of its mouth); "Ammi" = Kamala's mum, green dupatta, big hug energy; "Abu" = Kamala's dad, cardigan, glasses; "Aamir" = big brother, beard, hoodie; "the Prince" = tall man, flowing gold robes, shiny hair; "the Green Emperor" = green alien in robes with a high collar; "the Archer Girl" = purple jacket, bow on her back. Names are said in VO only.
