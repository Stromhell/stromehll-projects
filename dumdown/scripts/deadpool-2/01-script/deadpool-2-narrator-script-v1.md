# DumDown: Deadpool 2 (Super Duper Cut), Explained (Narrator Script v1)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 14 min of narration (~2,100 VO words), plus 12 cutaway gags (~4 min). Target full length about 18-20 min.
**Voice:** recommended FREE local voice (Kokoro am_michael, same as Loki); ElevenLabs David only if Pushpam picks it. Gag voices = other Kokoro voices.
**Visuals:** original parody animation in the DumDown anime cel style over highly detailed painted backgrounds. Cinematic camera and letterbox. No film footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every film line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** a speech-to-text transcript of the English audio track in Pushpam's Super Duper Cut file (source/dp2_whisper.txt).
**Tone:** the film is R-rated. The narration stays PG-13 (no f-words, bleeps used as a gag). The orphanage headmaster's abuse is handled as "he hurt the kids", never shown.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s).
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, hotline, talk show, weather report...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen: "the Merc" in a red-and-black suit with our own mask shape (no film logo or belt emblem), a chrome giant, a cyborg soldier with a glowing eye, a lucky woman with a ring around one eye, a kid with fire fists. Names are said in VO only.

## 0:00 HOOK (cold open)

🎬 A dark apartment. A man in a red suit lies on a pile of fuel barrels like it's a waterbed, smoking a cigar, staring at a photo. Slow push in. A lighter clicks.
🎵 Sad piano, way too sad.

This man cannot die. Shoot him, stab him, chop off his hand, he grows it back. So why is he lying on top of twelve hundred gallons of fuel with a lit cigar?

[SFX] Lighter flick. Pause. KABOOM.

🎬 The apartment explodes. Body parts spin past camera in slow motion. A hand flies by and gives a thumbs up.
[POP] "HE'S FINE. MOSTLY."

That, my friends, is a family film. No, really. The movie itself tells you that. A family film about a guy who keeps trying to die, a time-travelling soldier who keeps trying to kill a child, and the worst superhero team ever assembled.

This is Deadpool 2, the Super Duper Cut, the longer version with even more jokes. I watched all of it so you don't have to. Let's get dumb.

## 0:45 WHO IS THIS GUY?

🎬 Quick flashback montage in comic-panel frames: a handsome guy, a hospital bed, a scary lab, then the scarred face under a hoodie.
🎵 Funky bass recap music.

Quick recap. Wade Wilson was a mercenary with a big mouth. He got cancer, a shady lab "cured" him by torturing him until a mutant healing power kicked in, and it left his whole body looking like, in his words, an avocado that had sex with an older avocado.

Now he heals from anything. He wears a red suit so bad guys can't see him bleed. And he knows he is in a movie, so he talks to us. Constantly. He will not stop.

🃏 CARD: THE MERC WITH A MOUTH. Type: CHAOS. Move: "Fourth Wall Punch". Weakness: silence.

## 1:30 THE WORLD TOUR OF BAD GUYS

🎬 Hong Kong at night, neon signs. A crime boss in a shiny suit. The Merc drops through a glass roof, swords out.
🎵 Action guitar.

At the start, Wade is living his best life. He's gone international. Gangsters, crime bosses, the worst people on Earth, people nobody else will touch. He touches all of them. With swords.

🎬 Fast cuts: Tokyo rooftop, a yacht, a warehouse. Bad guys flying in every direction. Wade waves at the camera mid-fight.
[SFX] Sword shing, shing, shing.

He even jokes his way through every country. He doesn't speak the language, but he did take eighth-grade Spanish, so every threat comes out as "where is the library?"

🎬 His taxi, back in New York. A sweet, nervous driver with a turban and a big smile.
🃏 CARD: DOPINDER. Type: TAXI DRIVER. Move: "Meter Running". Weakness: wants to be a contract killer.

Back home, his favourite taxi driver Dopinder tells Wade he wants to be a hitman too. Wade says, sure, follow your dreams. This is terrible advice. Remember it.

🤡 GAG 1: "DOPINDER'S DRIVING SCHOOL". A cheap local TV ad. Dopinder points at the camera. "Learn to drive! Learn to drive angry! Learn to drive... THROUGH a man!" Car honk. "Dopinder. Lessons. Taxi. Murder. Please call."

## 3:00 HAPPY ANNIVERSARY

🎬 A cosy apartment with fairy lights and a skee-ball machine in the corner. Vanessa, a woman with curly dark hair, hands Wade a small box.
🎵 Warm soft guitar.

Then he goes home to Vanessa, the love of his life, for their anniversary. She gives him a skee-ball token from their first date. A tiny, worthless coin. Hold on to that coin, because this whole movie secretly runs on it.

[POP] "IMPORTANT COIN. PROBABLY."

🎬 Wade opens his gift box. Inside, a small plastic thing. He squints at it.

Wade's present? Vanessa shows him something that tells him she's ready to have a baby. They start picking names. Wade's terrified, because his own dad walked out on him, and he thinks he'll mess it up the same way.

Vanessa tells him, you are not your father. Kids give us a chance to be better than we used to be. Again. Remember that line. The movie will not let you forget it.

🎬 Night. Fairy lights. A door crashes open. A thug Wade failed to finish off earlier bursts in, gun raised.
🎵 Music cuts to a single low note.

And then, the same night, a gangster Wade let get away comes for him. He misses Wade. He doesn't miss Vanessa.

🎬 Slow motion. A skee-ball token rolls across the floor and stops.
[SFX] A coin spinning to a stop.

Yeah. That happens in the first ten minutes. Wade kills the guy, but it doesn't matter. Vanessa is gone. And it's his fault.

## 5:00 THE WORST SIX WEEKS

🎬 Back to the fuel barrels. Wade lies on them, cigar, photo of Vanessa. Same shot as the hook.
🎵 That too-sad piano again.

And that's where the hook comes in. Six weeks later, Wade tries to blow himself up, because his healing means he can't stay dead any other way.

🎬 The explosion again. Then a dark sky. Pieces of Wade float down like confetti. A giant chrome hand catches one.
🃏 CARD: COLOSSUS. Type: STEEL. Move: "Polite Punch". Weakness: swear words.

It doesn't work. He just ends up in a lot of pieces. And his chrome-skinned friend Colossus from the X-Men picks all the pieces up and puts them in a bag.

🎬 A dreamy, snowy, glowing place. Vanessa stands far away in a golden light. Wade reaches for her. She points at his chest.

While he's in pieces, Wade has a vision. He sees Vanessa, but he can't reach her. She just tells him one thing: "Your heart's not in the right place."

He thinks that's deep. It is also, partly, a literal joke about him being in pieces. Hold on. That line also comes back.

🤡 GAG 2: "INSURANCE FOR IMMORTALS". A calm insurance agent at a desk. "So you exploded." "Yep." "Again." "Yep." "Sir, this is your fourteenth claim this month." "I'm on a journey." Stamp: DENIED.

## 6:30 X-MEN TRAINEE

🎬 A huge stone mansion on a lawn, totally empty. Only Colossus, a moody teen girl with a buzz cut, and a cheerful girl in pink.
🃏 CARD: NEGASONIC TEENAGE WARHEAD. Type: ATOMIC. Move: "Eye Roll Blast". Weakness: enthusiasm.
🃏 CARD: YUKIO. Type: SUNSHINE. Move: "Hi Wade!". Weakness: none, she's perfect.

Colossus takes Wade to the X-Men mansion to heal and, maybe, become a hero. Negasonic is there, and she's now dating Yukio, the sweetest person in the movie, who says "Hi Wade!" every time she sees him. Every single time.

🎬 Wade walks the empty halls. A whole wall of portraits. Not one of the famous X-Men is home. A door at the end of a hallway closes very fast.

Wade notices the mansion is always empty. Every time they come here, it's the same three people. He jokes the studio can't afford the big-name X-Men. He is not wrong.

🎬 Colossus at a whiteboard. "RULE 1: NO KILLING. RULE 2: LABEL EVERYTHING IN THE FRIDGE."

Colossus gives him the rules. One: no killing anyone, ever. Two: label your food in the fridge. Wade gets one of these right.

🤡 GAG 3: "X-MANSION HOA MEETING". A big meeting room, fifty chairs, two people. A clipboard. "Okay, roll call. Professor? ... Storm? ... Anyone with a famous name?" Cricket noises. "Great. Motion to buy a new couch. All in favour?" One hand. "Passed."

## 8:00 THE FIRE KID

🎬 A grey orphanage with spikes on the gate. News vans. Police cars. A thin teenage boy in a hoodie, fists glowing like lava.
🎵 Tense drums.

Wade's first X-Men job. An angry kid is going wild at the Essex House, an orphanage for mutant kids. His name is Russell, but he calls himself Firefist.

🃏 CARD: FIREFIST (RUSSELL). Type: FIRE. Move: "Hot Hands". Weakness: everyone gave up on him.

[POP] "WHERE DOES THE FIRE STOP? THE WRIST? THE ELBOW?"

Wade is very interested in how far up the fire goes. Russell yells at him. Wade does the classic hero speech. Russell yells some more.

🎬 Russell points at a man in a suit by the gate. The Headmaster. Cold smile, glasses. Behind him, guards in orderly uniforms.

Then Wade sees it. Russell isn't angry for no reason. The headmaster and his staff have been hurting the kids in that place. Russell is scared out of his mind.

🎬 Wade turns around. A guard. Pop, pop.
[SFX] Gunshot. Gasp from the whole crowd.

So Wade does the most Wade thing possible. In front of the police, in front of the news, in front of Colossus, he shoots one of the guards. Rule number one, gone in about four minutes.

🎬 Two glowing collars snap around two necks. Wade and Russell sit in a police van.

Both of them get power-blocking collars and go straight to the Icebox, a super-prison for mutants. And Wade's collar means no healing. Which means his cancer is back.

🤡 GAG 4: "ICEBOX TRAVEL REVIEWS". A fake travel website. One star. "Room was cold. Staff were cold. Food was cold. My neighbour was a giant man in a basement. Would not recommend." Next review: "One star. Too much foreshadowing."

## 10:00 PRISON BUDDIES

🎬 A cold blue prison yard. Huge guards. Inmates with power collars. Wade in an orange jumpsuit, coughing, looking sick.
🎵 Sad prison harmonica.

In prison, Russell sticks to Wade like glue. Wade keeps telling him to go away. Russell wants a friend so badly, and Wade is too busy feeling sorry for himself to be one.

Russell has a plan of his own. Get out, then get revenge on the headmaster. Wade tells him to find the biggest guy in the prison and make friends.

🎬 A deep pit in the basement. Chains. Something huge breathing in the dark. Russell looks down.

Spoiler: the biggest guy in this prison is chained up in the basement, and Russell hears about him.

## 11:00 CABLE

🎬 A dark road at night. Lightning hits the ground. A crater. A huge soldier rises out of it, one robot arm, one glowing eye, and a big gun. He's holding a small, dirty teddy bear.
🎵 Massive synth stab.

Meanwhile, from the future, a soldier arrives. His name is Cable. He has a metal arm, a scary eye, and he only came here for one reason: to kill Russell.

🃏 CARD: CABLE. Type: TIME SOLDIER. Move: "Grumpy Stare". Weakness: jokes about his height.

🎬 Cable blows the prison wall open. Guards fly. Wade jumps between him and Russell.

Cable breaks into the Icebox. Wade, sick, no powers, still steps in front of the kid. He fights a cyborg from the future with basically just his attitude.

[POP] "IS DUBSTEP STILL A THING?"

Wade does get three important questions in. Is dubstep still a thing? Do people still make beer at home? And does Dopinder ever find love? Cable does not answer.

🎬 Cable and Wade crash through the wall together and fall out into the snow. Russell is left behind, staring at the basement.

In the end Wade and Cable go flying out of the prison together. And Russell? Russell is still inside, with the giant in the basement.

🤡 GAG 5: "TIME TRAVEL AIRLINES". A gate desk. "Welcome to Time Air. You have two charges. One to get here, one to get home." "What if I need a third?" "No refunds." Cable: "...That's just lazy writing."

## 13:00 ROCK BOTTOM, THEN A TEAM

🎬 Wade at the bar with his friend Weasel, dirty bar, flat beer.
🎵 Sad trombone, then upbeat heist music.

Every movie has a moment where the hero hits rock bottom. Wade even points it out. This is it. He's lost Vanessa, his job at the X-Men, and the kid.

Then he gets it. Vanessa said his heart wasn't in the right place. Maybe she meant this kid. Maybe if he can't save her, he can save Russell.

🎬 Weasel reading a phone. "Prison convoy, moving the mutants tomorrow, armoured trucks." Wade nods slowly.

Russell is being moved by armoured convoy. Wade's plan: ambush the convoy and grab him. Weasel's plan: you are going to need a team.

So they put out a job ad. On a website for jobs. Wade wants it gender neutral, tough, and morally flexible. Basically a team of very cool weirdos.

## 14:00 X-FORCE AUDITIONS

🎬 A plain office. A folding table. Wade and Weasel interview people one by one, like a talent show.
🎵 Game-show music.

🃏 CARD: BEDLAM. Move: "Messes with electricity". Weakness: wind.
🃏 CARD: SHATTERSTAR. Move: "Better at everything". Weakness: being better at everything.
🃏 CARD: ZEITGEIST. Move: "Very strong acid spit". Weakness: lawnmowers.
🃏 CARD: THE VANISHER. Move: "Invisible". Weakness: power lines. Possibly also a famous actor.
🃏 CARD: PETER. Move: none. He just saw the ad. Weakness: everything.

Meet the team. Bedlam messes with electricity. Shatterstar is an alien who says he's better than everyone at everything. Zeitgeist has acid spit. The Vanisher is invisible, so we don't know what he looks like. And Peter. Peter has no powers. He just saw the ad and thought it sounded fun. Wade loves him instantly.

🎬 A woman with a ring around one eye, arms crossed, casually calm.
🃏 CARD: DOMINO. Type: LUCK. Move: "It just works out". Weakness: Wade doesn't believe in her.

And Domino. Her power is luck. Wade says that's not a real superpower. They argue about it for an embarrassingly long time. It is. A very real superpower. Domino wins every argument, and every fight, by things just kind of happening to go her way.

Wade even names the team. Not X-Men, that's sexist. X-Force. Domino points out that's a little derivative. Wade ignores her.

🤡 GAG 6: "IS LUCK A SUPERPOWER? THE DEBATE". Two podiums, a debate moderator. "Yes it is." "No it isn't." "Yes it is." The moderator's microphone happens to fall on the other guy's head. "...Yes it is." Lights go out on only his side.

## 15:30 THE WIND ADVISORY

🎬 A plane door opens over a highway. Six heroes in matching suits. A weather warning on a little screen inside the plane: "HIGH WINDS".
🎵 Huge heroic music.

Team X-Force jumps out of a plane to land on the convoy. Everyone is hyped. One small issue: there is a high wind advisory. Somebody did point that out. Nobody listened.

🎬 One by one. Bedlam blown through a bus window. Shatterstar into a helicopter blade. Zeitgeist into a woodchipper. The Vanisher hits a power line, fizzles into view for one second and he's a famous handsome guy, then gone.
[SFX] Crash, clang, chomp, zap. Each one gets its own sad trumpet.

And they die. All of them. Almost immediately. In the dumbest ways possible. The Vanisher lands on power lines and becomes visible for exactly one second, and he looks like a very famous movie star. Then he's dead.

🎬 Peter, peacefully floating down, waving. He lands safely in a park. Then he walks over to help Zeitgeist, who's being chewed up by a woodchipper. Acid spit. Peter melts. Fade to black.

And Peter, sweet Peter, actually survives the jump. Then tries to help. And dies too. Within thirty seconds, X-Force is down to Wade and Domino.

🤡 GAG 7: "THE WEATHER WITH A FOREST RANGER". A cheerful weatherman with a map. "Today, high winds. Good day to fly a kite. Bad day to skydive with a superhero team you met yesterday." A tiny cartoon guy blows across the map. "Anyway, back to you."

## 17:00 DOMINO'S LUCKY CHASE

🎬 A huge chase on a highway. Domino walks down the middle of the road. Cars crash on both sides of her. A rolling truck goes right over her head. She doesn't even look.
🎵 Funky chase music.

Wade, Domino and Cable all crash the convoy at the same time. And here Domino proves Wade wrong in the coolest way. She just walks through the chaos. A car flips over her. A truck rolls past her. A panda balloon floats into a guy's face at exactly the right moment. Luck.

🎬 Cable stares at her, stunned. Wade gives the camera a slow look.

Even Cable stops for a second like, okay, that's actually pretty cinematic.

🎬 Inside the prison truck. Russell pulls the collar off the huge giant from the basement. Two red eyes open.

But while everyone is busy, Russell frees the giant in the back of the truck. The Juggernaut.

🃏 CARD: THE JUGGERNAUT. Type: UNSTOPPABLE. Move: "I'm the Juggernaut". Weakness: baby legs jokes.

🎬 Juggernaut grabs Wade by the top and bottom. Pull. Cut away right before it happens. A small sad sound like a cracker snapping.
[SFX] A loud rip, then a slide whistle.

Wade is a huge fan. He tells Juggernaut exactly which comic issues he loves. Juggernaut answers by ripping him in half.

## 18:00 BABY LEGS

🎬 The taxi. Wade's top half in the back seat, legs missing. Then Wade at the bar with tiny baby legs growing out of his waist.
🎵 Lullaby music.

Wade's healing is back, so he slowly regrows his whole bottom half. But it grows back like a baby's. So for a while, Wade walks around with tiny baby legs. Grown man, adult top half, baby bottom. You cannot unsee this.

🤡 GAG 8: "BABY LEGS FITNESS". A cheesy 90s workout tape. Wade on a mat, in a leotard, with tiny legs. "And kick! And kick! And... sit down, these legs are four days old." Neon text: "TONED IN TEN YEARS."

🎬 Wade's apartment. Cable is suddenly standing behind him. Weasel quietly backs out of the room.

Then Cable shows up. Not to fight. To ask for help. Because Wade's team let the Juggernaut loose, and Cable cannot stop Juggernaut and the kid on his own.

🎬 A soft blue memory. A cosy future home. A woman and a small girl. The little girl hugs the dirty teddy bear. Then the bear is on the ground in ash.
🎵 Tragic strings.

Here's the real story. In the future, grown-up Russell becomes a killer. And Cable's wife and his little daughter Hope are among the people he kills. The teddy bear was hers. Tonight, Russell will burn the headmaster at the orphanage. That's his first kill. After that, he never stops.

[POP] "SO HE'S... KIND OF RIGHT."

Wade and Cable are the same. Both lost the person they love. Both blame themselves. Wade even admits, if he could go back and stop the people who killed Vanessa, he would.

🎬 A handshake. Cable's metal hand, Wade's tiny-legged body standing on a chair.

So they make a deal. Wade gets one chance to save Russell's soul before Cable shoots. How long? Thirty seconds. That's the deal.

🤡 GAG 9: "THIRTY SECONDS TO SAVE A SOUL". A game-show studio with a giant ticking clock. "Your time starts... now!" Wade: "Kid, you're special, you matter, you..." Buzzer. "Time's up! Our prize is... a bullet."

🎬 Colossus' room. Wade in the doorway. Colossus doesn't look at him.

First, Wade goes to Colossus and apologises. Wade broke his trust. Colossus says no. He would be disgraced. Wade says sometimes doing the right thing is messy, and leaves.

🎬 Night. Rain. The orphanage on fire. Russell and Juggernaut tear through the gate. Orderlies run. Cable, Domino and Wade storm in.

Until Colossus shows up after all. Chrome vs Juggernaut. Wade even announces: big CGI fight coming up. Colossus doesn't fight fair this time either. Let's just say Juggernaut walks a little funny afterwards.

## 21:00 THE BULLET

🎬 The headmaster on his knees in the courtyard. Russell, hands glowing, about to burn him. Cable on a ledge, aiming.
🎵 Music slows down to a heartbeat.

Then it all comes down to one moment. Russell is about to kill the headmaster. Cable has the shot. Wade has his thirty seconds, and he's talking fast. You're just a kid. There are people who'll treat you right. Don't become what he said you are.

🎬 Wade unlocks his power collar... and clips it on his own neck. No healing. He looks at Cable.

Russell doesn't buy it. So Wade does the only thing left. He puts a power collar on himself, so he can't heal. And when Cable fires, Wade jumps in front of the bullet.

[SFX] A single gunshot. Slow motion. Silence.

🎬 Wade on the ground. Russell, crying, holding his hand. Domino, Colossus, Negasonic, Yukio and Cable gather around.

It works. Russell sees someone actually sacrifice something for him. For once. He doesn't kill the headmaster. Wade's heart, finally, is in the right place.

🎬 Wade, dying, keeps talking. And talking. He gives everyone a goodbye. He gives a speech. He sings a little. The others look at their watches.

And then Wade dies. Very slowly. He takes the longest death scene in history. Everyone gets a goodbye. He even asks if the Academy is watching.

🎬 A golden light. Vanessa. She's waiting for him in a soft, glowing place. She tells him he can't stay. Not yet.

He finally sees Vanessa again. But she tells him he can't stay. It's not his time.

🤡 GAG 11: "DEATH SCENE OSCAR CAMPAIGN". A movie ad in gold letters. "For your consideration. Best Actor in a Very Long Death. Forty minutes of dying. Four costume changes. One song." Fake quote: "Please just go." The New Pork Times.

## 22:30 THE COIN

🎬 Cable alone on a rooftop, staring at his time device. The last charge glowing. He looks at the teddy bear. Then he turns the dial.
🎵 Hopeful piano.

Here's the twist. Russell didn't kill anyone, so the future is already fixed. Cable's family is already safe. So he uses his very last time charge, the one he needed to go home, to go back a few minutes.

🎬 Rewind effect. Cable quietly slipping something into the belt of Wade's suit. Then the same gunshot.

He sneaks something into Wade's suit. When the bullet hits, it hits that skee-ball token. Vanessa's gift. The coin from their first date. It saves his life.

[POP] "TOLD YOU. IMPORTANT COIN."

Cable can't go home now. He chooses to stay here and keep the world from falling apart. Wade keeps asking, you did that for me? Cable keeps refusing to admit it.

🎬 A taxi bursts through the gate and runs over the headmaster. Dopinder leans out the window, beaming.

And the headmaster? Just as everyone agrees to let karma deal with him, Dopinder drives his taxi straight into him. Karma, apparently, has a taxi licence. Remember that terrible advice from the start? Yeah.

🎬 A sunset. Wade, Russell, Cable, Domino, Colossus, Negasonic, Yukio, and Dopinder, standing together.

So, what do you get when you take a chrome giant, a pinch of courage, a cup of luck, a splash of diabetes and a whole lot of cancer? A family. Wade wasn't lying. It is a family film.

🎬 Negasonic and Yukio fixing Cable's time device with a screwdriver. Wade grabs it.

And then the end-credits scenes. Negasonic fixes Cable's time machine. And Wade does what anyone would do. He travels through time to fix all his mistakes.

He saves Vanessa. He saves Peter, the guy with no powers. And he even goes back and fixes a couple of old, embarrassing movies from his own career. Let's say Hollywood owes him.
