# DumDown: Secret Invasion, Explained (Narrator Script v2)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Video 11.** **Format:** one funny narrator, about 15-16 min of narration (~2,100 VO words), plus 10 cutaway gags (~3.5 min). Target full length about 19 min.
**Voice (rule 4):** the Spider-Man: Brand New Day voice for everything: Chatterbox + Fenrir reference, exaggeration .75, cfg .35. Gag characters are pitch/pace variations of the same voice. No Kokoro.
**Visuals:** DumDown cel character style. Every verb a character does in the VO is acted (anticipation, action, settle) on the word (rule 1). Mixed shot sizes, characters fill the frame, no graphic overlaps a character, every pop label sits inside the safe frame on the empty side of the shot (rule 2). Before every 🤡 GAG the shot finishes and the line finishes, hard cut to the gag, fresh shot after (rule 3), marked `[CUT]`.
**Built from:** the English subtitle track in Pushpam's Secret Invasion S1 file (source/si_transcript.txt). Research only, every line paraphrased.
**Tone:** family-friendly and playful. Deaths are said gently, cartoon action only, no swearing.
**Running bits (callbacks):** the SKRULL-O-METER (a counter in the top-right safe zone that ticks up each time someone is revealed as a Skrull), Fury's "everybody gets one lie, nobody gets two", Fury's bad knee, the chess board.
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Parody designs (original, no Marvel logos or costumes): **Eyepatch** = Fury, older, grey stubble, eyepatch, long black coat, walks with a limp. **Talos** = green shapeshifter, grey swept hair, pointy ears, chin ridges, brown waistcoat. **G'iah** = young green shapeshifter, shaved sides, olive hoodie. **Gravik** = green rebel leader, scar on his cheek, black bomber jacket. **Agent Hill** = dark ponytail, black tactical jacket. **The Spymaster** (Sonya) = posh, short blonde bob, cream trench coat, teacup. **The Colonel** (Rhodey) = grey suit, tie, stiff smile. **The President** (Ritson) = white hair, blue suit, very red face. **Varra / Priscilla** = Fury's wife, elegant, glasses, silver cardigan. **Pagon** = Gravik's big sidekick, beanie. **Agent Ross** = glasses, rumpled suit. Shapeshifters in true form: green skin, ridged chins, pointy ears, big expressive eyes (cartoon, not scary).

Cue key: `🎬` shot (camera in brackets, acted verbs in CAPS), `🃏 CARD` trading-card intro, `🤡 GAG` cutaway, `🎵` music, `[SFX]`, `[POP]` caption (safe-frame, never over a character), `[CUT]` clean scene end before a gag, `[METER n]` SKRULL-O-METER ticks to n.

## 1:35 FURY IS BACK (SORT OF)

🎬 [Extreme wide, crane down] A gloomy Moscow safe house in the rain. A car door opens. A boot steps out. Then a second boot, very slowly.
🎵 Big hero brass that stops on a sad trumpet note.

And here he is. Nick Fury. The man who built the Avengers. The coolest spy on Earth. He's been living on a space station called S.A.B.E.R. for years.

🎬 [Low angle, then medium] Fury STRIDES toward camera like a legend. Halfway, his knee CLICKS. He WINCES, grabs the railing, keeps going.
[SFX] Knee crack.

Except, he's older now. His knee makes noises. His good eye is not so good. He climbs the stairs like every step owes him money.

🃏 CARD: EYEPATCH. Type: SPY. Move: "Stare". Weakness: stairs.

🎬 [Medium, two-shot] Talos, the old green Skrull, waits by a window with a small plant. He HUGS Fury. Fury pats his back twice, awkward.

Waiting for him is his old friend Talos. Talos is a Skrull, but a good one. Thirty years ago, Fury and Captain Marvel promised the Skrulls they'd find them a new home planet. Thirty years later, the Skrulls are still waiting. That's the longest "I'll get back to you" in history.

🃏 CARD: TALOS. Type: SPACE DAD. Move: "Disappointed Sigh". Weakness: believing in people.

🎬 [Close-up] Talos's face, sad. A photo of a Skrull woman on the shelf.

Talos tells Fury the bad news. A young, angry Skrull named Gravik has taken over. He's built a secret base, and he has an army of Skrulls who are fed up with waiting. And then Agent Maria Hill shows up with the worst news: Gravik is trying to start a war between Russia and America. Using a bomb.

## 3:05 MEET GRAVIK

🎬 [Extreme wide, slow push-in] A rusted, abandoned nuclear power plant in the Russian snow. Hidden inside, warm lights, green plants, hundreds of Skrulls in their true form.
🎵 Low drums.

Fresh shot. This is New Skrullos. Gravik's secret base inside an old nuclear power plant. Why a nuclear plant? Because Skrulls don't get hurt by radiation. And because the rent is very cheap.

🎬 [Medium, tracking] Gravik WALKS past rows of Skrulls, SHAKES hands, PATS a kid on the head, then turns to camera with a cold stare.

🃏 CARD: GRAVIK. Type: REBEL. Move: "Big Speech". Weakness: listening.

Gravik is the bad guy, but he's the kind of bad guy who has a point. His people were promised a home and never got one. His solution is less great. He wants to take Earth, and get rid of the humans. That's like being upset you didn't get a seat on the bus, so you steal the bus.

🎬 [Close-up] A young Skrull woman with shaved sides LEANS on a wall, arms crossed, watching Gravik.

And standing in the crowd is G'iah. Talos's daughter. She's working for the bad guys. Yes. Talos's own kid. Family dinners are going to be awkward.

🃏 CARD: G'IAH. Type: DOUBLE AGENT. Move: "Eye Roll". Weakness: her dad.

[METER 2]

🎬 [Medium, low angle] A human prisoner in a chair. A Skrull leans close and copies his face. The face WOBBLES and settles into the prisoner's face.
[SFX] Squelchy morph.

Here's how Skrulls copy people. They don't just copy faces. Gravik's team can copy your memories too. So the fake you knows your password, your birthday, and that embarrassing thing you did in Year 4. [CUT]

🤡 GAG 2: "SHELL-B-GONE". A cheesy late-night shopping channel. A smiling Skrull host holds a bottle. "Tired of your human face? Itchy? Too many eyebrows? Try SHELL-B-GONE! And this is where Fury says the best line of the show. He tells the bomb guy: everybody gets one lie. Nobody gets two. The guy lies twice in a row. Fury looks almost happy.
