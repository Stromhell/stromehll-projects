# Joke craft notes for DumDown (video 11, Secret Invasion)

Techniques used in the script, each tagged in the script margin as (J: ...):
- **Setup / punchline**: setup states a normal expectation, punchline flips it with the last word carrying the surprise ("He's been gone so long, his voicemail is full... of other voicemails").
- **Rule of three**: two normal items, third absurd ("bombs, blackouts, and someone stole the office stapler").
- **Misdirection**: lead the viewer to one reading, reveal another (the "Agent Ross" cold open; G'iah wearing Fury's face in the finale).
- **Escalation**: a running bit that gets bigger each time (the "SKRULL-O-METER" counter of who turned out to be a Skrull: 1, 2, 5, Rhodey?!).
- **Callback**: plant early, pay off late ("Everybody gets one lie, nobody gets two" returns with Ritson and with Fury himself; Fury's bad knee; the chess game).
- **Act-out**: the character physically acts the joke in the shot (Fury tries to run, his knee says no).
- **Understatement**: deadpan narrator under big drama ("Earth is about to go to war. Fury's plan: a nap.").
- **Specificity**: weirdly exact details are funnier than vague ones ("a 136-year-old space dad").

Rhythm targets (yt-retention): hook loses under 25 %, so the first 15 s states the question (who is secretly a Skrull?) plus a twist promise. A laugh or surprise every 20-30 s. Every topic change is signposted with a chapter beat. No talk-only stretch over 25 s without a visual change.

Family-friendly: deaths said gently (no gore), cartoon action only, no swearing, no adult jokes.
