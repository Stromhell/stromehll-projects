**Channel:** DumDown. **Video 12.** **Format:** one funny narrator, countdown of 12 mistakes, ~2,100 VO words (~14 min) plus 8 cutaway gags (~1.5 min). Target ~15 min.
**Release:** booked slot Mon 7 Dec 2026 16:00 UTC, 11 days before Avengers: Doomsday (18 Dec).
**Voice (rule 4):** Spider-Man: Brand New Day voice for everything (Chatterbox + Fenrir ref, exag .75, cfg .35). Gag characters are variations of it.
**Visuals:** DumDown cel style. Every verb acted on the word (rule 1). No graphic over a character, everything in the safe frame (rule 2). `[CUT]` = clean scene end before each gag, fresh shot after (rule 3). First gag before 1:30 (Secret Invasion lesson).

- **Callbacks:** Steve's "no do-overs" rule (planted at #2, paid off at #1); "the bill always comes due" (planted at #7, paid at outro); "a lot to hand a 16-year-old" style specificity.
