# DumDown: Thunderbolts*, Explained (Narrator Script v2)

**Channel:** DumDown. **Format:** one funny narrator, about 15 min of narration (~1,700 VO words), plus 10 cutaway gags (~3.5 min). Target full length about 18-19 min.
**Voice:** FREE local Kokoro am_michael (same as every DumDown video since Loki). Gag voices = other Kokoro voices. No ElevenLabs except the thumbnail.
**Visuals (Pushpam 19 Nov 18:40 UTC):** the usual DumDown cel character style (not Toon Lanky). Every scene mixes shot sizes: extreme wide establishing, medium, close-up, extreme close-up on eyes or hands, low angle hero shots, over-the-shoulder, crash zooms and whip pans. Characters belong to the scene: they lean on rails, grab ropes, kick crates, sit in car seats, climb a shaft, duck behind cover, punch through doors. Action poses (run, jump, punch, kick, dodge, fall, climb) instead of standing and talking. Highly detailed painted backgrounds (a neon Kuala Lumpur skyline, a glass tower lab, a congressional hearing room, a tiny sad apartment, a vault a mile under a mountain, an elevator shaft, a desert road, a limo interior, a skyscraper penthouse, Times Square at night, a shadowy room maze). Cinematic camera and letterbox. No film footage, no logos, no subtitles, no channel intro. Every film line is paraphrased.
**Built from:** the English subtitle track in Pushpam's file (source/tb_transcript.txt).
**Tone:** family-friendly and playful (DumDown rule after Spider-Man: Brand New Day). No swearing, no adult jokes, cartoon action only. Bob's dark past is said gently ("a really rough life"), never in detail.
**v2 (19 Nov):** eight extra narration beats for length and setup (soccer-team name, Mel calling Bucky).
**End screen:** last 5 s clean.

Cue key:
- `🎬` = animated scene recreation (about every 30 s), with the shot type in brackets.
- `🃏 CARD` = trading-card intro for a new character.
- `🤡 GAG` = Comedy Central-style cutaway (fake ad, hotline, talk show, weather report...). Own voices, laugh track, music.
- `🎵` = music change. `[SFX]` sound effect. `[POP]` pop-up caption.

Parody designs on screen (original, no film logos or emblems): "the Sister" = blonde braid, black tactical vest, green-grey trousers; "the Metal Arm guy" = shoulder-length dark hair, silver metal arm, black leather jacket; "the Dad" = big bearded man in a stretched red suit with a gold star-free chest plate and a red helmet; "the Ghost" = woman in a white-grey hooded suit that flickers; "the Shield guy" = buzz-cut soldier in a navy-white-red tactical suit with a plain round shield; "the Mask" = grey hooded suit with a mirrored visor; "Bob" = scruffy curly hair, grey pyjamas, later a glowing gold suit; "the Void" = a tall shadow with white eyes; "the Boss" = sharp black bob haircut, power suit, red lips; "the Assistant" = glasses, ponytail, cardigan, tablet. Names are said in VO only.

🎬 [Extreme wide, slow crane down] Night. The tallest tower in a neon city. A tiny figure stands on the very edge of the roof, braid blowing in the wind.
🎵 Low, lonely synth.

This woman is about to jump off the second tallest building in the world. Not because she has a parachute. She has a parachute. She just doesn't care if it opens.

🎬 [Extreme close-up] Her eyes. Bored. Then she steps off.
[SFX] Wind whoosh. Record scratch.
[POP] "WORK-LIFE BALANCE: 0%"

This is Thunderbolts. Six broken people, one very strange man in pyjamas, and a boss who would delete them all like an old email. I watched all of it so you don't have to. Let's get dumb.

## 0:40 THE SAD ASSASSIN

🎬 [Tracking, side on] The Sister freefalls past glowing windows, then crashes through a glass wall into a white lab. She rolls, stands, dusts off her vest.
🎵 Punchy drums.

Meet Yelena Belova. Super spy. Trained since she was a little kid in a creepy spy school called the Red Room. Her sister Natasha was an Avenger, and Natasha is gone. So Yelena does cleanup jobs now. Fly in, break stuff, fly out. Malaysia this week. Who knows next week.

🃏 CARD: YELENA. Type: SPY. Move: "Parachute Of Sadness". Weakness: feelings.

🎬 [Medium, then crash zoom] A scientist stands by a scanner. The Sister grabs his face and presses it to the scanner. Beep. Green light.

She talks to her targets like they're her therapist. She tells one guard about her empty heart, then knocks him out. She needs a scientist's face for a scanner, so she borrows it. Just the face. He's still attached. Then she sets the whole lab on fire, says job done, and asks: what's next?

Quick fact: Yelena is the funniest assassin in Marvel. Her jokes are dry. Her humour is dry. Her emotional life is basically a desert.

[SFX] Scanner beep. Fire whoosh.
[POP] "FACIAL RECOGNITION: VERY HANDS-ON"

🤡 GAG 1: "THERAPY FOR ASSASSINS". A late-night hotline ad. A calm host: "Feeling empty? Talking to your targets? Call 1-800-SAD-SPY! Our counsellors are trained, armed and have nowhere else to be." A phone rings. The host ducks a flying knife.

🎬 [Wide, low angle] A grand wooden hearing room. Politicians on a raised bench. The Boss sits alone at a small table, sunglasses on, sipping water.

Meet Valentina Allegra de Fontaine. Director of the CIA, and before that, the boss of a shady company called OXE. And yes, she will correct you if you forget the "de". 🎬 [Wide] A tiny, gloomy apartment. A huge bearded man opens the door in a fake chauffeur hat. Boxes of junk everywhere, a tiny TV, a sad cactus.
🎵 Soft accordion, slightly out of tune.

Yelena visits her dad, Alexei. He's a big Russian super soldier called Red Guardian. He used to be the Soviet answer to Captain America. Now he drives a limo and lives in a flat that is technically in Baltimore.

🃏 CARD: RED GUARDIAN. Type: DAD. Move: "Big Hug". Weakness: honesty.

Alexei also claims he once fought Captain America. There's no proof. He has a lot of medals, and some of them still have price tags.

[SFX] Stool creaks. Squeak.
[POP] "SO FULL. SO FILLED."

🤡 GAG 2: "RUSSIAN LIMO SERVICE". A fake TV ad. The Dad leans out of a stretch limo: "You want luxury? You want security? You want driver who was once super soldier? Call Alexei! Seat belts optional. Feelings guaranteed." The limo drives into a mailbox.

🎵 Tense drums.

Inside the vault, Yelena finds her target. Then another one. Then another one. First, the Ghost. Ava Starr. She can walk through walls. Then a masked woman, Taskmaster, who copies any fighting move she sees. Then John Walker, the man who was Captain America for about five minutes.

🃏 CARD: TASKMASTER. Type: COPYCAT. Move: "Copy Paste". Weakness: plot.
🃏 CARD: JOHN WALKER. Type: SOLDIER. Move: "Shield Bash". Weakness: being liked.

And here's the funny part. None of them know who the real target is. It's like four people showing up to the same blind date, and all of them brought weapons.
