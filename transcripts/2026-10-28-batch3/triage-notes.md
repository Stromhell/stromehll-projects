## Why
- **Spanish Civil War = 2D, and it becomes Spain part 3.** Spain part 1 (Al-Andalus to 1492) and part 2 (the Empire, 1492 to 1898) are already queued. This picks up right after 1898 and runs to Franco's death and democracy in 1977. Same maps, same art style, a ready-made trilogy.
- **Japan = 2D.** 40 years of economics: charts, maps, company logos, bullet trains, GDP lines. Fast cutout style fits.
- **Putin = 2D.** It is a list explainer (limo, four planes, ghost train, fake offices, body doubles, food tasters), not one story. 2D cutaways and diagrams do it faster. The armoured train and the replica office would also look great in 3D, so these sets could be reused later if it does well.
- **Bermuda Triangle = 3D.** Ships vanishing in storms, Flight 19's five bombers lost over the sea, the USS Cyclops, rogue waves, fog. It is atmosphere and suspense, which is what 3D is for. Ocean and ship sets overlap with the Antarctica (Shackleton) build, so assets can be reused.
- **Education Trap = 2D, optional.** It is half self-help, half history. The history part (Prussia after losing to Napoleon in 1806, the factory age, Macaulay's 1835 English Education Act) is strong for an Indian audience. The rest is a motivational talk with shaky claims. Only make it if reframed as a history video (see below).
- **Wild Heart of Spain = skip.** A wildlife film with no people and no story (ibex fights, cranes, otters). It only works with real nature footage, and it is off-brand for a history channel.
- **Inside ICE = skip.** A current-affairs documentary about named private people (a detainee and his family) and a 2026 political fight. Remaking it would mean retelling real living people's lives, it will date fast, and it is off-brand. If Pushpam wants the topic, it could become a 2D explainer on how US immigration detention works, without the family's story.

Cadence: Spain part 3 (Civil War), Japan and Putin make a 2D, 2D, 2D block, then Bermuda (3D). Education Trap goes after that if Pushpam wants it. All of these queue behind batch 1 and batch 2.
