## Why
- **Mongols, JPMorgan, Dark Web = 2D.** Each covers years or centuries, lots of people, maps, charts and money flows. The cutout style tells them fast. The Dark Web story has a great arrest scene, but most of it is explaining Tor, Bitcoin and an FBI hunt over two years, which suits 2D.
- **Antarctica = 3D.** One ship, one ice sheet, 28 men, one survival story. Ice crushing a wooden ship, a lifeboat in a storm and men walking over glaciers are exactly what 3D does best. The ship and ocean sets can be reused later.
- **Spain = two 2D videos, not three.** Transcripts 5, 6 and 7 cover the same history and overlap a lot. Recommended split:
  - **Spain part 1: Al-Andalus to 1492.** Muslim rule, the Reconquista and the fall of Granada. Built from transcript 6 plus the first half of transcript 5. Transcript 6 has garbled captions ("Siberia" for Iberia, etc.), so its script needs a full rewrite.
  - **Spain part 2: The Spanish Empire, 1492 to 1898.** Columbus to losing Cuba and the Philippines. Built from transcript 7 plus the second half of transcript 5.

Cadence: Mongols, JPMorgan and Dark Web are a 2D, 2D, 2D block before Antarctica (3D). Spain part 1 and part 2 start the next block. These all queue behind current work (3 x 2D + 2 x 3D at once).
