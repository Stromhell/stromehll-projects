If not the daughter, then who? The most powerful woman in the country is Kim's sister, Kim Yo Jong.

She runs propaganda, speaks for the regime on foreign policy, and stands at her brother's side at summits.

But the rule so far is direct bloodline: from father to child. A sister breaks that rule. A daughter doesn't.

North Korea is a deeply male-dominated society, and it has never had a woman in charge. But the Kim family is treated almost like a royal line.
