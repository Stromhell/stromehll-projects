The first hint came from an unlikely source: the American basketball star Dennis Rodman, who had become friends with Kim Jong Un.

In 2013, after a holiday with the Kim family on the coast, Rodman said he had held Kim's baby daughter, and named her as Ju Ae.

South Korea's spy agency later reported that Kim had three children, born in 2010, 2013 and 2017. Some researchers doubt there is a son at all.

Her mother is Ri Sol Ju, a former singer, now called the 'respected first lady'. The children are believed to be schooled at home in Pyongyang, not abroad like their father, who studied in Switzerland.

Reports say the girl loves horse riding and skiing. The family's coastal estate has a riding ring, and a giant ski resort was built nearby.
