After the 2022 missile test, she kept appearing. In February 2023, at a banquet for the army's anniversary, she sat at the centre of the top table, generals lined up behind her.

The next night, at a military parade in Kim Il Sung Square, a white horse trotted past. The announcer called it the horse most loved by the leader's beloved child.

In North Korean myth, white horses carry the Kim family. Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Un have both been shown riding them on Mount Paektu, the dynasty's sacred mountain.

Later in 2023, she stood in front of her father at the launch of a spy satellite, in a black leather coat like his. Analysts noticed he was in the background.

A study by Nikkei Asia counted her days on state TV. Her appearances rose sharply in 2024, more than half of them at military events.

In September 2025, she travelled with her father to Beijing, where he stood beside China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin.
