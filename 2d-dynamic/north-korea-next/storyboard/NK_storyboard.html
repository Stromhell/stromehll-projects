Look and device

Running device

The white horse. In Kim family myth, a white horse on Mount Paektu stands for the bloodline. It appears in the parade for 'the leader's beloved child', in a riding ring at the Wonsan estate, in the old propaganda paintings of Kim Il Sung and Kim Jong Un, and in the last shot riderless in the snow, waiting for whoever comes next.

Look

2D dynamic, locked Village Painterly look: 6-head characters from the village-cast rig. Kim Jong Un, Ri Sol Ju, Kim Yo Jong and the girl state media calls his beloved daughter, drawn as painterly characters, never photo-likenesses; generals in peaked caps and medals. Highly detailed painted backgrounds with warm light and dynamic cast shadows: a missile launch pad at dawn, the banquet hall, Kim Il Sung Square at night with parade lights, the Wonsan coast estate with a beach and riding ring, a satellite launch site, a Beijing review stand, snowy Mount Paektu. Moving cameras, 3 to 5 parallax layers, characters act in every shot. Neutral, evidence-first narration: everything about the girl is labelled as reported or believed. No repeats of the 3D NK film's beats (punishments for K-dramas, power cuts, defector escape stories). SFX under VO; music at combine.

Fact log

Fixes against the transcript

  • Her name 'Kim Ju Ae' comes from Dennis Rodman (2013) and NIS; not confirmed by North Korea. Age: NIS believes born ~2013. Script labels both.
  • First public appearance: Hwasong-17 ICBM launch, 18 Nov 2022 (white padded coat).
  • Banquet 7 Feb 2023 (75th anniversary of the KPA founding); parade the night of 8 Feb 2023; white horse and the 'beloved child' line from KCTV coverage.
  • Satellite: Malligyong-1 launch, 21 Nov 2023; NYT noted she stood in front of Kim in an official photo.
  • Nikkei Asia analysis of state TV appearances (2022-2025), as cited by the source; script keeps the trend only.
  • Beijing Victory Day parade, 3 Sept 2025: Kim stood with Xi and Putin; his daughter was reported to have travelled with him.
  • NIS 2017 report on three children (2010, 2013, 2017); a son has never been seen and some researchers doubt he exists.
  • Kim Jong Un studied in Bern, Switzerland in the 1990s. Kim Yo Jong also schooled in Bern.
  • State-media titles: 'respected daughter' and similar honorifics are documented; NIS has said she is 'most likely' successor (2023 assessment) while hedging. Script says South Korean intelligence 'reads' them as signs.
  • Source spells the name many ways ('Chouer', 'Jouer', 'Jer'); corrected to Ju Ae.
  • Overlap: no K-drama punishments, power cuts or defector stories (all in the 3D NK film). Purges kept to one generic line ('push aside generals and relatives') to avoid repeating that film.

Removed from the transcript

  • NordVPN read cut. Release after 24 Nov; end card points to the 3D North Korea film.
