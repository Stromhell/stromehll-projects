Title: How Muslims Lost Spain After 781 Years

Description:
For 781 years, from 711 to 1492, Muslims ruled Spain. Cordoba was the brightest city in Western Europe, with lamp-lit streets, libraries and one of the greatest mosques ever built. Then the Christian kingdoms pushed south, Granada fell, and the mosques of Al-Andalus became churches. The Great Mosque of Cordoba still stands today, with a cathedral built in its middle. This is how Muslim Spain rose, how it lasted almost eight centuries, and how it was lost.

This is Part 1 of our Spain series. A runaway prince swims a river to escape a massacre and founds the emirate of Cordoba. Cordoba grows into one of the biggest cities on Earth, with paved, lamp-lit streets, a huge library, new crops and a man who tried to fly. Then the caliphate breaks into rival kingdoms, the Christian north pushes south, and Granada holds out for 250 years before 1492 changes everything: the surrender, the broken promises, the expulsions, and the Arabic words still spoken in Spain today.
