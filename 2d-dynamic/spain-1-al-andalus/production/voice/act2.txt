Then, far away in Syria, everything changed. In 750 a rival family, the Abbasids, overthrew the Umayyads and hunted down almost every member of the royal family.

One young prince escaped. Abd al-Rahman, about twenty years old, ran with his little brother and jumped into the Euphrates river with soldiers behind them.

The soldiers promised safety if they came back. His brother turned around and was killed. Abd al-Rahman kept swimming.

He spent about five years on the run across North Africa, hiding with his mother's Berber relatives.

In 755 he landed in Al-Andalus. Within a year he took Cordoba and declared himself emir. Al-Andalus now answered to nobody in the east.

He started building the Great Mosque of Cordoba on the site of an old church. Its forest of red and white striped arches is still standing.
