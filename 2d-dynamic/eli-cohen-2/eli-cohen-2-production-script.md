# Why Syria Hanged Eli Cohen in Public

Working title: **Why Syria Hanged Eli Cohen in Public (Eli Cohen, Part 2)**

6 parts (cold open + 5 acts), 27 shots, 567 words, about 3:58 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** The radio key from Part 1. It opens Part 2 under the Damascus window, is tapping when the door is broken down, lies on the table as evidence at the trial, and the last shot is the empty tea tin on a windowsill. A wristwatch (returned to his family in 2018) bookends the act about his legacy.

**Look:** 2D dynamic, locked Village Painterly look, same cast and sets as Part 1 (Eli with thin moustache, Nadia, Damascus officers, Amin al-Hafiz) plus new sets: the Golan bunkers and eucalyptus trees, the Jordan headwaters works, a radio-detection van, the embassy street, a military courtroom, Marjeh Square at dawn, the Tel Aviv flat. Moving cameras, 3 to 5 parallax layers, characters act in every shot. Torture and the execution are shown only through shadows, a closed door, an empty square and a crowd's faces, never the body. Neutral tone on Israel and Syria. Morse tapping motif; SFX under VO; music at combine.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Eucalyptus trees: widely told but not confirmed in official records; script calls it 'the most famous story' and 'legend'.
- Deputy defence minister: rumour only; source's 'almost became president' dropped.
- Capture: 24 Jan 1965 by Syrian counter-intelligence (Col. Ahmad Suwaydani's service is often named) using Soviet direction-finding equipment; the Indian embassy's radio interference complaints are commonly cited as a trigger. Power-cut search method is from multiple accounts.
- Clemency appeals came from many governments and figures including Pope Paul VI. Israel's offers of exchange are reported, not officially detailed: script says 'reportedly'.
- Hanged 18 May 1965 in Marjeh Square, Damascus; body displayed for hours; broadcast on Syrian TV.
- Remains never returned. Family and Israeli reports say the burial site was moved; source's 'three times' kept as 'several times'.
- Wristwatch recovered by Mossad in 2018. In May 2025 Israel announced it had obtained about 2,500 documents, photos and belongings from Syria's Eli Cohen archive.
- Contribution to 1967 Golan capture is debated by historians; script says so.
- Source claim that the bin Laden family's company held a Golan contract and Eli 'reached' it: unverified, dropped. Girlfriends/hidden camera claims dropped (unsourced).
