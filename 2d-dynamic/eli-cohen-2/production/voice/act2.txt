By 1964 he was a regular at the top. Rumours later said he was even considered for deputy defence minister. There is no proof of that.

But he was transmitting too often, sometimes every day, at the same hours. His handlers warned him to slow down.

In November 1964 he made his last visit home. He told Nadia he was tired. He had a newborn son he had barely held.

His handlers asked him to go back one more time.
