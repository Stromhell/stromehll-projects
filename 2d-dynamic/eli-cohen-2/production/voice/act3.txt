Near his apartment was the Indian embassy. Its staff complained that their radio messages kept getting jammed.

Syrian counter-intelligence, using new Soviet direction-finding equipment, began hunting for a secret transmitter in the area.

They switched off the power to the neighbourhood, block by block, waiting for the signal to stop.

On the morning of the twenty-fourth of January 1965, they broke in while he was transmitting.

The man they had toasted at parties was an Israeli spy. For Syria's new rulers, it was a humiliation.
