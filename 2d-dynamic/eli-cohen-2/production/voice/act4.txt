He was interrogated for weeks. Under guard, he was made to send messages to Israel. Then Syria sent a message of its own, telling Israel its agent was in Syrian hands.

A military court tried him quickly. The verdict was death.

Israel asked for mercy. So did governments and figures around the world, including the Pope. Israel reportedly offered prisoners, money and medicine in exchange. Syria refused.

On his last night, he wrote to Nadia. He asked her not to spend her life mourning, to look after the children, and to marry again.
