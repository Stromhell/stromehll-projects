Description:
In 1945 Japanese newspapers printed advice on how to eat acorns, sawdust and rats. Sixty-six cities had burned and about nine million people had no home. Twenty-three years later Japan was the third-largest economy on Earth.

How did a country with no oil, no colonies and no army pull that off? Not with one trick. This video follows the whole comeback: the occupation and Article 9, land reform, the 360-yen dollar, the Korean War orders that saved Toyota, the stubborn founders of Toyota, Sony and Honda, the income-doubling plan, the piggy banks of ordinary families, and 1964, when the bullet train ran and a young man born in Hiroshima on the day of the bomb lit the Olympic flame.
