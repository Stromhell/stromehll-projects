So today, much of the world celebrates on January the first, thanks to Julius Caesar, Pope Gregory the Thirteenth, and many others.

Now we celebrate with fireworks, by staying up until midnight, and in America, with the famous ball drop in Times Square.

From eleven days of Akitu in March, to honey cakes for Janus, to counting down the very last seconds of the year.

Four thousand years later, we're still doing the same thing: saying goodbye to the old year, and hello to a new one.

So, together now: Ten. Nine. Eight. Seven. Six. Five. Four. Three. Two. One. Happy New Year!
