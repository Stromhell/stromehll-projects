Why does the whole world count down to midnight on 31 December? New Year has not always been on January 1st. In this animated history we go from an 11-day Babylonian festival in March, to the Nile flood in Egypt, to Rome's 10-month calendar, Julius Caesar's fix, the two-faced god Janus, the Church cancelling the party, the pope who deleted 10 days, the night Britain lost 11 days in 1752, and Russia finally switching in 1918. Then we count down together. Happy New Year!
