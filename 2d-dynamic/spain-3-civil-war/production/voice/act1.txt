In 1898 Spain lost the last pieces of its empire to the United States. The country was poor, mostly farmers, and deeply split between rich landowners and the church on one side, and workers on the other.

The army looked for new glory in Morocco. In 1921, at Annual, Berber fighters wiped out a Spanish army. Thousands of soldiers died in days.

In 1923 General Miguel Primo de Rivera took power in a coup, with the king's blessing. He ruled as a dictator for seven years, then lost support and quit in 1930.

The king was tainted by the dictatorship. In April 1931 republicans swept the big cities in local elections. Alfonso the Thirteenth left the country without abdicating.

Spain was a republic: the Second Spanish Republic. Its flag was red, yellow and purple.
