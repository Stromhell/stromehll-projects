# How One General Took Spain, and Why a King Gave It Back

Working title: **36 Years of Franco: How Spain Fell Apart and Came Back**

7 parts (cold open + 6 acts), 50 shots, 1288 words, about 8:47 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A cracked Spanish flag hangs behind every act. The crack opens wider through the Republic and the war, is painted over in grey under Franco, and in the last act is repaired with a gold seam (kintsugi style) as democracy returns. The parchment map from parts 1 and 2 returns for the front lines.

**Look:** 2D dynamic, locked Village Painterly look: 6-head characters from the village-cast rig (smooth tapered limbs, detailed 4-finger hands, nose below the eye line), in 1920s-1980s dress (army uniforms, workers' overalls, militia caps, priests, 1970s suits). Highly detailed painted backgrounds with warm light and dynamic cast shadows: Madrid streets, Moroccan desert outposts, Barcelona barricades, Guernica's market square, a Castilian village, 1960s Costa del Sol beaches, the Spanish parliament chamber. Moving cameras, 3 to 5 parallax layers, characters acting in every shot, match-cut transitions. Violence is shown through shadows, smoke and aftermath, never gore. SFX under the VO; no music until combine.
