Description:
On 23 February 1981 armed police stormed Spain's parliament and fired into the ceiling. Spain had been a democracy for less than four years. That night the king went on television in his uniform, and by morning the coup was over.

This is Part 3 of our Spain series. To understand that night, we go back to the disasters after 1898, the dictatorship of Primo de Rivera, the hopes and fights of the Second Republic, the military coup of July 1936, Guernica and the International Brigades, and a civil war that killed hundreds of thousands. Then 36 years of Franco: Hitler at Hendaye, the hunger years, the deal with America, the economic miracle, and the prince he chose to follow him. And finally the king who used Franco's power to give Spain back its democracy.
