# The Spy Who Fooled an Entire Country

Working title: **The Spy Who Fooled an Entire Country (Eli Cohen, Part 1)**

6 parts (cold open + 5 acts), 28 shots, 530 words, about 3:44 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** The radio key. A small Morse key hidden in a tea tin keeps returning: we hear its tapping under scene changes, see it in training in Israel, packed in a suitcase in Buenos Aires, and in the last shot under the window of a Damascus apartment across the street from army headquarters. Part 2 opens with the same key.

**Look:** 2D dynamic, locked Village Painterly look: 6-head characters from the village-cast rig in 1950s-60s dress (Alexandria linen suits, Israeli army khaki, Buenos Aires tailored suits and hats, Damascus officers in olive uniforms and berets, party guests in cocktail clothes). Eli Cohen keeps one face sheet with a thin moustache. Highly detailed painted backgrounds with warm light and dynamic cast shadows: Alexandria's corniche, a Cairo police cell, a Tel Aviv flat, a training safehouse with maps, a Buenos Aires café, Damascus rooftops, the Hamidiyah souk, an apartment opposite army HQ. Moving cameras, 3 to 5 parallax layers, characters act in every shot. Neutral tone on Israel and Syria. SFX under VO (Morse tapping is the motif); music at combine.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Born 26 Dec 1924, Alexandria; Syrian-Jewish family from Aleppo. Parents emigrated 1949.
- 1954: Operation Susannah (Lavon affair) exposure; Cohen was detained and questioned but released for lack of evidence. Expelled after Suez, reached Israel Feb 1957.
- Married Nadia Majald 1959. First rejected by Mossad (1955); recruited by military intelligence Unit 188 in 1960.
- Arrived Buenos Aires Feb 1961; arrived Damascus Jan/Feb 1962. Apartment in Abu Rummaneh opposite the General Staff HQ.
- Amin al-Hafiz meeting in Argentina: widely told but Hafiz denied it in later interviews. Script says 'one later account... Hafiz always denied'.
- Ba'ath coup 8 March 1963; Hafiz became head of state July 1963.
- Source title says he 'almost became Syria's president': a legend. The common, also unconfirmed, claim is he was considered for deputy defence minister; that goes in Part 2 as a rumour only.
- Dropped the source's unsourced claims about girlfriends and hidden cameras; sponsor reads cut.

## Removed from the transcript

- Part 1 ends at the 1963 coup and the Golan tour invitation. Part 2: the Golan, the eucalyptus trees, capture in Jan 1965, trial, hanging 18 May 1965, the missing remains.
