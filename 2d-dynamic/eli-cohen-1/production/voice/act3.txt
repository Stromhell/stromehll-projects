In 1961 he flew to Buenos Aires, home to a large Syrian and Lebanese community.

He spent months making friends: at cafés, clubs and the Arab-language press. He gave to Syrian causes and spoke passionately about the homeland.

He also met Syrian officials posted to Argentina. One later account says he got to know Amin al-Hafiz, a military attaché who would soon lead Syria, though Hafiz always denied meeting him there.

By the time he left, he had letters of introduction to important people in Damascus.
