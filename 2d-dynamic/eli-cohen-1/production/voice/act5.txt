Syria in those years was unstable. Governments came and went, often through military coups.

On the eighth of March 1963, officers of the Ba'ath Party seized power.

Many of the new men in power were people Kamel had met at his parties. Within months, Amin al-Hafiz became Syria's leader.

Suddenly, Israel's spy was a friend of the government.

Officers began to offer him something no spy could normally get: a tour of the Syrian defences on the Golan Heights.
