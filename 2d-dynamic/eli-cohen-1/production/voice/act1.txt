Eliyahu Ben-Shaul Cohen was born in 1924 in Alexandria, Egypt, into a Jewish family whose roots were in Aleppo, in Syria.

He grew up speaking Arabic like a native, along with French and Hebrew. Teachers said he had a strong memory for detail.

After Israel was founded in 1948, life for Egypt's Jews grew harder. In 1949 his parents and brothers left for Israel. Eli stayed behind.

In Egypt he helped Jews leave in secret. In 1954, after an Israeli sabotage plot in Egypt was uncovered, he was arrested and questioned, but released.

After the Suez war of 1956, Egypt expelled many of its Jews. Eli arrived in Israel in early 1957.
