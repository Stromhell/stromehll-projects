In early 1962 he arrived in Damascus.

He rented an apartment across the street from the army's general headquarters. From his window he could watch who came and went.

He opened an import-export business, and began to throw parties.

Officers drank and talked. He listened. Late at night, he tapped out what he had heard to Tel Aviv.

Every few months he flew back to Israel through Europe, to see Nadia and his children, who believed he was buying equipment abroad for the government.
