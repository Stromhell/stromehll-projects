Description:
In June 1812 Napoleon Bonaparte led the biggest army Europe had ever seen into Russia. Six months later almost none of it came back.

He started as a mocked boy from Corsica with a thick accent. The French Revolution opened the door, Toulon made him a general at 24, and in 1804 he took the crown from the Pope and put it on his own head. Austerlitz, the Continental System, Spain's guerrilla war, Moscow in flames, Leipzig, Elba, the Hundred Days and Waterloo: every time he won, he wanted more. He died on Saint Helena in 1821. His legal code still shapes the laws of dozens of countries, and his wars killed somewhere between three and a half and six million people.
