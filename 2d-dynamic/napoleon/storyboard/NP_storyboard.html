Look and device

Running device

Napoleon's black bicorne hat. It sits on a schoolboy's desk at Brienne, then on his head at Toulon, on a cushion at Notre-Dame, in the snow on the road from Moscow, on the mud at Waterloo, and in the last shot on a hook at Longwood on Saint Helena, with the sea in the window behind it. A map of Europe returns in every act, and French blue spreads over it, then drains away.

Look

2D dynamic, locked Village Painterly look: 6-head characters from the village-cast rig in period dress (Corsican village clothes, cadet uniforms, revolutionary tricolour sashes, French line infantry in blue, Austrian white, Russian green, British redcoats, Imperial Guard bearskins, Empire-style gowns). Napoleon keeps one face sheet through five ages (9, 24, 35, 43, 51). Highly detailed painted backgrounds with warm light and dynamic cast shadows: a Corsican hill town, a school dormitory, Toulon harbour at night, Alpine passes, the Pyramids, Notre-Dame with candles, the frozen Pratzen Heights, burning Moscow, the snow road, Fontainebleau, Waterloo's muddy ridge, foggy Longwood. Moving cameras, 3 to 5 parallax layers, characters act in every shot, match cuts. Battles are shown through movement, smoke and maps; no gore. SFX under the VO; music added at combine.

Fact log

Fixes against the transcript

  • Source: 'His brother sat on the thrones of Spain, Holland and Westphalia' by 1805. Wrong date: Joseph became King of Naples in 1806 and of Spain in 1808, Louis King of Holland in 1806, Jérôme King of Westphalia in 1807. Script puts it after Austerlitz and names Naples, Holland, Westphalia, with Spain in 1808.
  • Source: the Holy Roman Empire 'collapsed within weeks' of Austerlitz. It was dissolved on 6 Aug 1806, about eight months later. Script: 'within a year'.
  • Source: 'that same year (1808) he divorced Josephine'. The divorce was in Dec 1809 / Jan 1810; married Marie Louise April 1810; son born March 1811.
  • Source: in Russia 'the final battle never came'. Wrong: the Battle of Borodino, 7 Sept 1812, one of the bloodiest single days of the era (~70,000 casualties). Added.
  • Grande Armée crossed the Neman on 24 June 1812; total force including reinforcements was over 600,000. Survivors are disputed (often given as under 100,000, some say far fewer). Script says 'only a small fraction'.
  • Source: Waterloo was 'two days' after crossing into Belgium on 15 June. It was three days (Ligny and Quatre Bras on 16 June, Waterloo on 18 June).
  • Source: first abdication 'April 4th'. Napoleon signed a conditional abdication on 4 April and an unconditional one on 6 April 1814. Script: 'April 1814'.
  • Source: Saint Helena is 'the most remote inhabited island in the world'. Wrong (Tristan da Cunha is more remote). Script: nearly 2,000 km from Africa.
  • Source: his last word was 'Josephine'. Reported last words were 'France, armée, tête d'armée, Joséphine'. Script gives France, the army, Josephine.
  • Source: poisoning theory presented as open. Stomach cancer is the accepted cause; arsenic theories are widely rejected. Script: 'most likely of stomach cancer'.
  • Source: Napoleonic Wars killed '3 to 6 million soldiers'. Common estimates are about 3.5 to 6 million deaths in total, soldiers and civilians. Fixed.
  • Laffrey, 7 March 1815: the 5th Line Infantry; his words are usually given as 'If there is a soldier among you who wishes to kill his emperor, here I am.'
  • Egypt expedition took about 167 scholars (the savants); fleet destroyed at the Battle of the Nile, 1 Aug 1798. He left Egypt in Aug 1799.
  • Napoleon became an artillery lieutenant in 1785, aged 16. Promoted brigadier general after Toulon (Dec 1793).
  • Height: about 1.69 m (5 ft 7), average for his time; the 'short' myth comes from French vs English inches and British cartoons (reel only).

Removed from the transcript

  • Cut the language-learner pace, the repeated refrain (used once in the cold open idea only), and the 'Era Speak' channel outro. Merged the Josephine divorce into the Russia act as the lead-in.
