One year later, at Austerlitz, the armies of Austria and Russia faced him. They had more men and held the high ground.

Napoleon made his right side look weak. The enemy came down off the heights to attack it. Then he sent his army straight up the middle onto the empty heights, and cut them in two.

It became his most famous victory. Within a year, the thousand-year-old Holy Roman Empire was gone, and his brothers were kings of Naples, Holland and Westphalia.

But he could not beat Britain at sea. So in 1806 he banned all of Europe from trading with Britain. Smuggling boomed, his allies suffered, and Britain kept trading with the world.

In 1808 he made his brother Joseph king of Spain. The Spanish rose up in a new kind of war: the guerrilla, the little war. Ambushes in the hills, year after year.

He called it his Spanish ulcer. Six years of war that drained him of men.
