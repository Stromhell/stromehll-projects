In seventeen ninety-six he took over a hungry, ragged army in Italy. In about a year, he beat Austrian army after Austrian army.

In seventeen ninety-eight he sailed to Egypt with an army and over a hundred and fifty scientists. But the British navy destroyed his fleet, and his army was trapped.

So he left his army behind, slipped back to France, and told everyone it was a triumph.

In November seventeen ninety-nine, he seized power with soldiers. He was thirty and ran France.

On the second of December eighteen oh four, in Notre-Dame, the Pope came to crown him. Napoleon took the crown and put it on his own head.
