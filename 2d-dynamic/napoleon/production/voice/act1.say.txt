He was born in seventeen sixty-nine on Corsica, an island France had taken over just the year before. Napoleone di Buonaparte grew up speaking Italian.

At nine, he was sent to a military school in France. He had a thick accent and little money, and the other boys mocked him.

So he read. Maths, history, battles. He became an artillery officer at sixteen.

Then in seventeen eighty-nine the French Revolution broke the old order. Nobles fled or lost their heads. Suddenly, talent could matter more than birth.

In seventeen ninety-three, British and Spanish ships held the port of Toulon. Captain Bonaparte, aged twenty-four, saw where to put the cannons. His guns drove the ships from the harbour, and he was made a general.
