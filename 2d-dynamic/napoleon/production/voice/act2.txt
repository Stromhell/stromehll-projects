In 1796 he took over a hungry, ragged army in Italy. In about a year, he beat Austrian army after Austrian army.

In 1798 he sailed to Egypt with an army and over a hundred and fifty scientists. But the British navy destroyed his fleet, and his army was trapped.

So he left his army behind, slipped back to France, and told everyone it was a triumph.

In November 1799, he seized power with soldiers. He was thirty and ran France.

On the second of December 1804, in Notre-Dame, the Pope came to crown him. Napoleon took the crown and put it on his own head.
