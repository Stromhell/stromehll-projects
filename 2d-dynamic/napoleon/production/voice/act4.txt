He had no son with his wife Josephine, so in 1809 he divorced her, and married an Austrian princess, Marie Louise. She gave him an heir.

Then the Tsar of Russia began trading with Britain again. Napoleon's answer was the Grande Armée: men from France, Poland, Italy, Germany and more.

The Russians retreated and burned what they left behind. At Borodino they finally stood and fought. Tens of thousands died in one day, and the Russians pulled back again.

He reached Moscow in September. It was nearly empty. Then it burned.

He waited five weeks for a peace offer. None came. In October he turned back, and winter caught him.

Cold, hunger, Cossack raids, the river crossing at the Berezina. By December the Grande Armée was gone.
