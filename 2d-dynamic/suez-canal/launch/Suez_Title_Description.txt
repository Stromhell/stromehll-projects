Description:
In March 2021 one ship got stuck sideways in a ditch in the Egyptian desert and blocked around ten billion dollars of world trade every day. That ditch took 3,000 years to build.

Pharaohs started it. A Persian king finished it and carved his name in stone. Greek kings, Roman emperors and caliphs kept it alive until the desert swallowed it for a thousand years. Napoleon's engineers got the sea levels wrong. Then a retired French diplomat, tens of thousands of forced labourers and a fleet of steam dredgers finally cut through, and Egypt threw one of the most expensive parties in history. Six years later Egypt was broke, Britain owned the shares, and it took until 1956 for Egypt to take its canal back.
