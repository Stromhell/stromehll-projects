In March 2021, one ship got stuck sideways in a ditch in the Egyptian desert. For six days, it blocked around ten billion dollars of world trade every single day.

That ditch is the Suez Canal. It saves ships a trip of around eight thousand kilometres round Africa.

Pharaohs tried to dig it. Persian kings, Greek kings, Roman emperors, caliphs, sultans and Napoleon all wanted it. It took three thousand years and one stubborn Frenchman to finish.
