By 1260, the Mongols had built the largest land empire the world had ever seen. China, Persia, Russia, Baghdad: every army that stood in their way had been destroyed.

That summer, a Mongol embassy rode into Cairo with a letter for the sultan of Egypt: surrender, or be destroyed.

The sultan had the envoys executed. A few weeks later, his army met the Mongols at a place called the Spring of Goliath.
