Bukhara fell. Then Samarkand. Then Urgench, and Nishapur. Cities that refused to surrender were emptied.

The shah ran west and died on a small island in the Caspian Sea, a hunted man.

Genghis died in 1227, but his sons and grandsons kept going: China, Russia, Poland, Hungary.

In 1253, the Great Khan Möngke sent his brother Hulagu west with orders to bring Persia and the lands beyond under Mongol rule.
