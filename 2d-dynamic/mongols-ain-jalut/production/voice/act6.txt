Qutuz did not enjoy it for long. On the way home, Baybars and other officers killed him, and Baybars became sultan.

Baybars ruled for seventeen years and made Mamluk Egypt the strongest power in the Middle East. The Mamluks would beat the Mongols in Syria again and again.

Meanwhile, the Mongol Empire was splitting apart. Berke, the khan of the Golden Horde, had become a Muslim. He allied with Egypt and went to war with his cousin Hulagu.

Within a few generations, the Mongol rulers of Persia had become Muslims too. In 1295, Hulagu's great-grandson Ghazan converted, and his court followed.

The empire that destroyed Baghdad ended up adopting the faith of the people it had conquered. And the slaves the Mongols sold away from the steppe were the ones who stopped them.
