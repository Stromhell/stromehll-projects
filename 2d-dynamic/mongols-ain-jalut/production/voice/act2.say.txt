Bukhara fell. Then Samarkand. Then Oor-gench, and Nee-sha-poor. Cities that refused to surrender were emptied.

The shah ran west and died on a small island in the Caspian Sea, a hunted man.

Genghis died in twelve twenty-seven, but his sons and grandsons kept going: China, Russia, Poland, Hungary.

In twelve fifty-three, the Great Khan Mung-keh sent his brother Hoo-lah-goo west with orders to bring Persia and the lands beyond under Mongol rule.
