In twelve oh six, a steppe chief named Teh-moo-jin united the Mongol tribes and took the title Genghis Khan.

West of him lay the Kwa-raz-mee-an Empire, rich Muslim cities on the Silk Road. Genghis sent a trade caravan of about four hundred and fifty merchants.

At the border city of Oh-trar, the governor accused them of spying, and had them killed.

Genghis sent envoys to the shah to demand justice. The shah executed the lead envoy and sent the others back humiliated.

To the Mongols, killing an envoy was the worst crime there was. In twelve nineteen, Genghis Khan rode west with his whole army.
