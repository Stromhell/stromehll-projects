In the summer of 1260, Qutuz marched north out of Egypt. On the third of September, the two armies met in a valley in Galilee, at the Spring of Goliath.

Baybars led the vanguard. He attacked, then turned and pretended to run.

It was the Mongols' own favourite trick. Kitbuqa's army chased them into the hills, where the main Mamluk army was hiding.

The Mongols almost broke through. Qutuz threw off his helmet so his men could see him, and charged, shouting 'O Islam!'

Kitbuqa was captured and killed. The Mongol army was destroyed.

It was the first time a major Mongol army had been beaten in open battle and the defeat had stuck. The Mongol advance toward Egypt stopped here.
