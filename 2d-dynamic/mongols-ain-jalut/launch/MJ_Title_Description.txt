Description:
In 1260 the Mongols sent Egypt a letter: surrender, or be destroyed. Egypt's sultan had the envoys executed.

It started with a massacre of merchants at Otrar and an executed envoy, and Genghis Khan's army swept across the Khwarazmian Empire. His grandson Hulagu took Baghdad in 1258 and ended the Abbasid caliphate. Only Egypt was left, ruled by the Mamluks: slave soldiers, many sold off the steppe because of the Mongols themselves. At Ain Jalut, the Spring of Goliath, Qutuz and Baybars used the Mongols' own feigned retreat against them, and for the first time a major Mongol army was beaten and the defeat stuck.
