In the 1600s the decline came fast. Portugal broke away in 1640. Catalonia revolted. France beat Spain's famous infantry at Rocroi in 1643.

The royal family had married cousins for generations to keep power in the family. The last Habsburg king, Charles the Second, was sickly all his life and had no children.

When he died in 1700, Europe went to war over who would get Spain. The French Bourbon prince Philip won the crown, but Britain took Gibraltar and kept it.

In 1808 Napoleon tricked the Spanish royal family into giving up the throne and made his brother Joseph king. On the second of May the people of Madrid rose up.

The next day the French shot hundreds of prisoners. The painter Goya made it immortal: a man in a white shirt, arms raised, in front of the firing squad.

With Spain itself occupied, the colonies in the Americas began to rule themselves. Simon Bolivar in the north, Jose de San Martin in the south.

In 1824 the last big royalist army in South America surrendered after the battle of Ayacucho in Peru. Mexico was already gone. Almost the whole mainland empire was lost.
