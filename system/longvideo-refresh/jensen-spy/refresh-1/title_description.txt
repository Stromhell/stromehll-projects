Title: China Sent a Spy After Jensen Huang, US Intel Says

Description:
She bet a room full of executives she could get the most powerful man in AI to walk into a Taipei bar. He came. Two years later, US intelligence concluded she was working for China's Ministry of State Security, and the man she was sent to get close to was Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. This is how a bar bet turned into a spy file, a billion-dollar chip mystery and an apology email to every Nvidia employee.

In a Taipei bar in June 2024, a businesswoman named Alice Huang bet a table of executives she could get Nvidia's Jensen Huang to show up. He did. Two years later, according to the New York Times, US intelligence concluded she had been recruited by China's Ministry of State Security, and her assignment was him.

This is the story as reported by the New York Times, the Wall Street Journal and the Financial Times: Megaspeed, the Singapore company that bought billions of dollars of Nvidia chips, the US chip wall and its gaps, a year of surveillance, a briefing that went nowhere, and the email Jensen Huang sent to every employee. Alice Huang has not been charged with any crime and has not commented publicly; Megaspeed says investigators found no evidence of wrongdoing.
