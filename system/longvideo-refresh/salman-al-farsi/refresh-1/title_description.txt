Title: He Crossed 5,000 km to Find the Last Prophet | Salman al-Farsi

Description:
He was born the son of a Persian lord and kept a sacred fire that was never allowed to go out. Then he heard Christians praying in a church, and he walked away from everything. He crossed the Roman world from teacher to teacher, each one telling him to look for the last prophet, until he was betrayed, put in chains and sold as a slave in Arabia. In Medina he tested the three signs his last teacher had given him. Years later, his idea of digging a trench saved the city. This is the full journey of Salman al-Farsi, the man who searched the world for the truth.

This is the story of Salman al-Farsi, Salman the Persian, told from the earliest sources.

Out of respect, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is never shown in this video, and his companions are shown without faces.
