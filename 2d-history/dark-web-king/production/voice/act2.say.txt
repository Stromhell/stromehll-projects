Ross William Ulbricht was born in Austin, Texas, in March nineteen eighty-four.

He was an Eagle Scout, then a physics student at the University of Texas at Dallas, on a full scholarship.

He went on to a master's degree in materials science at Penn State, studying crystals.

But along the way he lost interest in science, and fell for an idea.

He became a libertarian: someone who believes the government should stay out of people's lives, including what they buy and what they put in their bodies.

Back in Austin he tried running small businesses, including a used-book company. None of them took off.

Then he had a bigger plan: a hidden online market where people could buy and sell almost anything, behind Tor, paid in Bitcoin.

He taught himself to code. His own journal says he grew illegal mushrooms in a cabin, so the site had something to sell on day one.

In early twenty eleven, at twenty-six, he launched it. He named it after the ancient trade route between China and the West.

Silk Road.
