Description:
In March 2014 a young Canadian drove to the US border to pick up a car bumper. The officer typed in his passport and a gamer tag came up: Xenomega. He was arrested on the spot.

Part 2 of The Kids Who Hacked the World follows the Xbox Underground: a group of teenage console modders who went from tinkering with dev kits to getting inside Microsoft, Epic Games, Valve and about 25 major companies, leaking Gears of War 3, downloading a US Army helicopter simulator and building the next Xbox at home before Microsoft had even revealed it. Then came the eBay auction, the stolen prototypes, Person A and the arrests.
