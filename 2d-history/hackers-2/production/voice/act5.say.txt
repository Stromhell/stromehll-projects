August twenty twelve. Inside Microsoft, they find a locked-down folder: designs for the next Xbox, codename Doo-rang-go. It will become the Xbox One.

The parts are available to buy. So they order them, and Nathan builds a working copy at home. Then they try to sell it to a buyer in the Say-shells for five thousand dollars.

Justin offers to post it. It never arrives. Then Dylan, without asking the others, puts photos of the prototype up on ee-Bay.

The auction goes viral. Microsoft gets it taken down, and Dylan starts giving interviews. The rest of the gang are furious.
