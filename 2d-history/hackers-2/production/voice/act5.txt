August 2012. Inside Microsoft, they find a locked-down folder: designs for the next Xbox, codename Durango. It will become the Xbox One.

The parts are available to buy. So they order them, and Nathan builds a working copy at home. Then they try to sell it to a buyer in the Seychelles for 5,000 dollars.

Justin offers to post it. It never arrives. Then Dylan, without asking the others, puts photos of the prototype up on eBay.

The auction goes viral. Microsoft gets it taken down, and Dylan starts giving interviews. The rest of the gang are furious.
