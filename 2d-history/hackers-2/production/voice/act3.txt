2011. Dylan gets a leaked list of usernames and passwords from a gaming forum. Some people reuse the same password everywhere. That's how he gets into Epic Games, the studio behind Gears of War.

David tells himself he has rules: no customer data, no credit cards. Just the games. Then they find an early version of Gears of War 3.

Sonic downloads hundreds of gigabytes overnight, burns it to eight Blu-ray discs, and posts them to David in a package marked 'wedding videos'.

Days after it's shared around, the game leaks to the public. It's news everywhere. Epic calls in the FBI.

The others panic and go quiet. Dylan doesn't. He keeps logging in, finds a list of the company's master passwords, and even watches staff through their office webcams.
