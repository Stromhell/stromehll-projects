The arrests come one by one. Nathan's house is raided. Then Sonic's. In February 2013, Australian police arrest Dylan. In 2014, Austin, and then David at the border.

The FBI's case is huge: chat logs, call recordings, years of evidence. Much of it, according to the charges, came from someone called 'Person A', from Wilmington, Delaware.

The gang only knew one person from Wilmington. Many came to believe it was Justin, who had been arrested at a games convention years before. It was never officially confirmed.
