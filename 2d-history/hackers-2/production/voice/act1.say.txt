two thousand one. Microsoft launches its first Xbox. A huge black box with a giant controller. A boy in Canada called David Puh-kor-ah falls in love with Halo.

But David cares more about how the machine works. He joins online forums where fans tweak their consoles, and he writes mods that change Halo's physics and weather.

The Xbox three-sixty is harder to tinker with. You need a developer kit, a special console only approved studios get. Then a batch of old ones turns up at a recycling yard, and they get resold online.

With a dev kit, David can see Microsoft's private network for game developers, where unreleased games are tested. He posts screenshots and becomes a star of the scene. His grades slide.

He sells Call of Duty cheats online for a hundred dollars a half hour, and says he made up to eight thousand dollars a day. The game's publisher sends legal letters. He ignores them.
