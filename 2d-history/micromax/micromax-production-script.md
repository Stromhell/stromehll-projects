# Who Killed Micromax?

Working title: **Who Killed Micromax? The Indian Phone That Beat Samsung, Then Vanished**

7 parts (cold open + 6 acts), 50 shots, 982 words, about 6:55 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A market-share bar chart pinned to a chai-stall wall that the story keeps cutting back to: Micromax's orange bar climbs past Nokia and Samsung, then gets eaten from the side by Jio blue and three Chinese brand colours. A crime-board 'suspects' wall in the cold open and ending ties the clues together.

**Look:** Punchy Indian business-history documentary in the house 2D style, Gumball/t-shirt character style (as in NVIDIA). Painted plates: a 1990s Delhi middle-class flat with a beige PC, a yellow STD/PCO booth on a dusty village road, a Gaffar Market-style phone shop, a Karva Chauth evening, a 2016 Jio launch stage, rows of Chinese brand billboards. Warm saffron and orange with Jio blue as the threat colour. SFX under the VO (ringtones, keypad beeps, crowd, cash), music only at combine.
