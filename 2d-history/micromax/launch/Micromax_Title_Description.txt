Description:
In 2014 an Indian phone brand sold more phones in India than Samsung. It had Hugh Jackman in its ads and its name on cricket's Asia Cup. Five years later it had almost no market left. So who killed Micromax?

It started with a boy in Delhi, a 56,000-rupee computer and a small IT company. Then came booth phones for India's yellow STD-PCO booths, a phone with a battery that lasted up to 30 days on standby, the Bling, the Canvas, and the top spot. Then three suspects arrived: Chinese brands with deep pockets, Jio's free 4G, and Micromax itself.
