In 2020, after the India-China border clash, Micromax tried a comeback with a new 'In' brand pitched as Indian. It got headlines, but not the market back.

The founders moved on. The companies around them, including electronics manufacturing businesses, kept going, but the Micromax that beat Samsung was gone.

So who killed Micromax? Jio pulled the trigger, Chinese rivals were already waiting, and a business built on other people's technology had nothing to fight back with.

It's a lesson for every Indian start-up: being cheap gets you in the door. Owning the technology keeps you in the room.
