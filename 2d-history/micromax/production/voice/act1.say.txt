The story starts with Rahul Sharma, a Delhi boy from a middle-class family who studied engineering.

In the nineteen nineties he asked his father for a computer that cost about 56,000 rupees, a huge amount for a family like his. His father bought it.

With no internet at home, he learned programming from books, then started writing software and supplying computer hardware.

In two thousand, he and three friends, Vikas Jain, Rajesh Agarwal and Sumeet Kumar, set up a company called Micromax.

At first Micromax was an IT company, selling software and computer parts. Small, steady, unglamorous.
