# The Kids Who Hacked the World, Part 1: The 17-Year-Old Who Hacked Twitter

Working title: **The 17-Year-Old Who Hacked Obama's Twitter (The Kids Who Hacked the World, Part 1)**

9 parts (cold open + 8 acts), 40 shots, 829 words, about 5:50 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A 'blue bird' wall of famous accounts that light up red one by one as they fall, with a running count and a crypto wallet meter that fills with each scam tweet. Each phase of the hack opens with a big PHASE 1 / 2 / 3 card.

**Look:** Internet-crime caper in the house 2D style, Gumball/t-shirt character style (as in NVIDIA). Painted plates: a teenager's dark bedroom with RGB lights in Tampa, a Minecraft-ish blocky game world, a shady forum on a giant screen, a phone-shop counter, Twitter's open-plan office at night, a crisis room with red alerts, a courtroom on a video call. Neon blue, purple and alert red. Famous people never drawn: only their names on tweet cards. Methods kept high level (no how-to). SFX under the VO (keyboard clicks, Discord pings, phone rings, alarm beeps), music only at combine.
