Description:
On 15 July 2020 the Twitter accounts of Elon Musk, Barack Obama, Joe Biden, Bill Gates, Apple and Uber all posted the same Bitcoin scam. Behind it was a 17-year-old in Florida with a phone and a fake login page.

Part 1 of The Kids Who Hacked the World follows Graham Ivan Clark from Minecraft scams and SIM swapping to a fake help-desk call that got him inside Twitter, the middlemen who sold stolen usernames, the scam that hit 130 accounts, the night Twitter silenced every verified account, the zoom-bombed court hearing and the sentence.
