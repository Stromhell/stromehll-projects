On the forum he learns about a scam called sim swapping. Criminals trick a phone company into moving your number to their sim card.

Many accounts send login codes by text. So whoever controls your number can reset your passwords, from email to bank to crypto.

In twenty nineteen a Seattle investor, Greg Bennett, lost over a hundred Bitcoin this way. The New York Times later linked the attack to Graham's online alias. He was a minor, and was never charged for it.

Soon afterwards, Graham is living large: his own apartment, a white B M W, a Rolex covered in gems. He's still seventeen.
