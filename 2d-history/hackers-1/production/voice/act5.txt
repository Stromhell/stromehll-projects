Graham wants one last, bigger payday. Phase three: a crypto giveaway scam. Send Bitcoin, and you'll get double back.

15 July 2020. The first scam tweets go out from crypto accounts and exchanges, one after another.

Then he goes big. Elon Musk's account posts it. Then Bill Gates, Jeff Bezos, Kanye West, MrBeast, Apple, Uber, Barack Obama and Joe Biden.

In total, 130 accounts are targeted, and scam tweets go out from 45 of them. Millions of people see them.

Most people see through it. Some don't. Money starts landing in the Bitcoin wallet, and Twitter deletes the tweets as fast as it can.
