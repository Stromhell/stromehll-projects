2016, Tampa, Florida. Graham Ivan Clark is 13, home-schooled, and plays Minecraft for hours. His parents split up when he was seven.

Online he sells rare game items and cool usernames. The catch: buyers often never get them. Other players make videos calling him a scammer.

By his own account, at 14 he's making about 5,000 dollars a month.

Then he finds OGUsers, a shady forum where people trade stolen accounts and rare usernames. He's banned within days, for taking money and not delivering.
