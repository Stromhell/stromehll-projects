July 2020. His new plan: forget hacking stars one by one. Get inside Twitter itself, and take any account he wants.

He tells a few people online that he works at Twitter. A small crew forms on Discord.

Using LinkedIn, he finds Twitter employees and phones them, pretending to be the internal help desk. Most hang up. A few report him.

But one employee believes him, and signs in on a fake login page. Graham uses those details, and the code from the employee's phone, to get onto Twitter's network.

That account can't reach the controls he wants. So he reads through internal messages until he finds login details for someone with far more access.

Now Graham can change the email and password on almost any Twitter account in the world.
