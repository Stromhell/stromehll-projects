A Twitter employee gets a phone call from the company's help desk. There's a problem with his work login. Could he sign in again, please?

He does. The caller thanks him and hangs up. He isn't the help desk. He's a 17-year-old in Florida with a jewelled Rolex.

Within a day he takes over the Twitter accounts of Obama, Biden, Bill Gates, Jeff Bezos and Elon Musk, and uses them to run a Bitcoin scam.
