So when Twilight wrapped in 2012, he did the opposite of what a heartthrob is supposed to do. He went looking for the strangest directors he could find.

David Cronenberg, twice. James Gray. Claire Denis. Small films, odd films, films that most of his fans never saw.

The turning point was a film called Good Time in 2017. He played a desperate small-time crook running through New York for one terrible night, and critics finally said it out loud. This man can act.

Then came The Lighthouse, where he and Willem Dafoe slowly lose their minds on a rock in the sea. Then Christopher Nolan cast him in Tenet, in a sharp suit, doing spy work. Sound familiar?
