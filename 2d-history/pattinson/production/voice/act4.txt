Which brings us back to 2019, and that petition. Matt Reeves wanted him for Batman. A big part of the internet wanted anyone else.

The Batman came out in March 2022. Rain-soaked, slow, gloomy, and very good. It made about seven hundred and seventy million dollars worldwide.

The petition crowd went quiet. Some of them even started calling him Battinson. The doubters had been proven wrong in front of the whole world.

A sequel, The Batman Part Two, has been delayed again and again, and is now due in February 2028. Remember that date, because it becomes a problem.
