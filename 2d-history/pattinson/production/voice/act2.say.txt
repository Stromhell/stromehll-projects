Then in two thousand eight came the part that changed everything. Edward Cullen, the brooding vampire in Twilight.

Five films. More than three billion dollars at the box office. Screaming crowds at every premiere, and paparazzi outside his door.

And here's the strange part. He never really pretended to love it. In interviews he joked about the films so often that it became his trademark.

Critics, meanwhile, had him down as a pretty face who'd fade away when the franchise ended. Hollywood has seen that story a hundred times.
