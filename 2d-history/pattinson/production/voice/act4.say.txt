Which brings us back to twenty nineteen, and that petition. Matt Reeves wanted him for Batman. A big part of the internet wanted anyone else.

The Batman came out in March twenty twenty-two. Rain soaked. Slow, gloomy, and very good. It made about seven hundred and seventy million dollars worldwide.

The petition crowd went quiet. Some of them even started calling him Bat-tinson. The doubters had been proven wrong in front of the whole world.

A sequel, The Batman Part Two, has been delayed again and again, and is now due in February twenty twenty-eight. Remember that date, because it becomes a problem.
