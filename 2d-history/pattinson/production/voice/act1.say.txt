Robert Pattinson was born in London in May nineteen eighty-six and grew up in Barnes, by the Thames. His dad imported vintage cars from America. His mum worked at a modelling agency.

He wasn't a stage-school kid. He joined a local amateur drama club as a teenager, by his own account partly because that's where the girls were.

In two thousand five that hobby turned into Harry Potter. He played Cedric Diggory, the golden Hogwarts champion in The Goblet of Fire, the one who doesn't make it out.

Remember the producer on that film, David Hay-man. He'll come back at the very end of this story.
