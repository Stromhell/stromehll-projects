So why does Hollywood keep calling? Reason one: the directors. Nolan twice, Villeneuve, Reeves, Bong Joon Ho, Robert Eggers, the Safdies. The best filmmakers keep picking the same man.

Reason two: he works in both lanes. A small film he can turn into a hit. A giant blockbuster where he steals scenes. Most stars only manage one.

Reason three: he's a producer now, finding young directors himself. And reason four: the internet loves him. Every chaotic interview and every meme is free advertising.

And the biggest reason is trust. You might not love a Robert Pattinson film. But it is almost never boring.
