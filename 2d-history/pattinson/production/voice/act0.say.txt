May twenty nineteen. Warner Bros. names its new Batman, and within hours thousands of fans sign a petition to get him fired.

The complaint was simple. This was the sparkly vampire from Twilight. The boy who girls screamed at. Not a superhero.

Seven years later, on the sixteenth of November twenty twenty-six, Amazon made an announcement. Robert Pattinson will be the next James Bond.

Same man. Same face. So what changed? Because Hollywood didn't hand him this. He spent fifteen years making sure they couldn't say no.

This is how a Twilight punchline became the most wanted actor in Hollywood.
