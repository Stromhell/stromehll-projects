## What you write

Shot lengths will change by +-30%.

## Rules (Pushpam's, all are hard fails)
- Real people stylised, no likeness, no real logos, posters or film title treatments (film names only as plain caption text). The bat cowl has NO emblem. The 007 card is plain text.
- Every VO line has a visible action showing what is said. Nothing static > 2-3 s: camera drift/push on every shot, idle breathing, ambient motion (rain, mist, flicker, crowd flashes, sand, waves).
- No overlaps; nothing floats (feet on the plate floor line, seated people on a real chair/sofa, cards held in hands); nobody pops in (walk in/out of frame, or a deliberate camera reveal); complete bodies; varied poses; consistent scale; real-world object sizes vs people.
- Cards/text never over faces and stay above the subtitle band (bottom ~200 px of 1080 reserved).
- SFX on actions (names must exist in shared/sfx: alarm arrow birds_loop breath camera_shutter chime city_loop coins coins_cascade creak crowd_cheer crowd_loop door_slam drone drum fire_loop footsteps hit hum_loop night_loop office_loop paper paper_tear phone_ring pop rain_loop riser run_steps scrape stamp swell tension thud tick trading_loop typing whoosh whoosh_soft wind_loop; check `ls shared/sfx`) and a `bed` ambience per shot.
- Rich detail: live props and light (glows, reflections, rain, mist, crowds) on top of plates so no frame looks plain.
