Description:
Before dawn, Russian tanks cross into the Baltics, and NATO invokes Article 5. This is a made-up scenario, built from real war games and NATO assessments. It shows the race that would decide everything: how fast can NATO get to the front?

We follow it step by step: Russia's fait accompli plan and RAND's 36 to 60 hours, the Baltic borders turned into obstacle courses, the 45 days it can take to move troops across Europe, a cargo ship from Texas through the submarine-patrolled Atlantic, a cyber attack on a port, jets above and cheap drones below, the 8.5 cm rail gauge gap, saboteurs, the 65 km Suwałki Gap, and why Gotland is called an unsinkable aircraft carrier.
