On paper NATO is huge: about three point four million active troops, more than twice Russia's. Most of them are American, Turkish or French.

For a war like this, NATO's commander might need to move hundreds of thousands of them to the eastern flank.

Right now, moving troops from Europe's Atlantic coast to its eastern edge could take up to 45 days. NATO wants that under 10.

About eighty thousand to a hundred thousand American troops are already in Europe. More would come from the United States: tanks from Texas, soldiers from Georgia.

At the port of Boh-mont in Texas, vehicles roll into a giant cargo ship built to carry an army's gear.

It heads out through the Gulf of Mexico and across the Atlantic. The crossing takes about two weeks.
