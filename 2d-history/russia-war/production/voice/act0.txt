Before dawn, Russian tanks cross into Estonia and Latvia. Swarms of drones fly ahead of them. A second force pushes into Lithuania from Belarus.

Within hours, NATO invokes Article 5. An attack on one member is an attack on all of them. Now the whole alliance is at war.

This is a made-up scenario, built from real war games and NATO assessments. But it shows the race that would decide everything: how fast can NATO get to the front?
