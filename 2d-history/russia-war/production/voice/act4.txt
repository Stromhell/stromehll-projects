Russia has one of the world's biggest submarine fleets. Many patrol the North Atlantic, near the gap between Greenland, Iceland and Britain.

Some subs can cut undersea data cables. Others hunt ships. And sea mines can close a whole shipping lane.

Our ship makes it to the North Sea, nearly at Rotterdam. Then the port's computer systems go dark. A cyber attack.

The captain can't wait. He turns for Bremerhaven in Germany instead. US forces have practised using ports all over Europe for exactly this reason.

Meanwhile soldiers from Fort Stewart, Georgia, board ordinary airliners. The US military can borrow commercial jets in a crisis.
