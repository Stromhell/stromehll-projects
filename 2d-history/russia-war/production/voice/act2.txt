That is why the Baltic borders are being turned into obstacle courses. Rows of concrete 'dragon's teeth' are built to stop tanks.

In 2025 all three countries said they would leave the treaty that bans anti-personnel landmines. In this scenario, the borders are mined.

Lithuania is wiring its border with cameras and sound sensors, so it knows the moment anything crosses.

It is even bringing back old swamps and forests, to push enemy columns into a few narrow roads.

And here NATO had warning. Like Ukraine in 2022, the alliance saw troops massing and started moving before the first shot.

But warning only helps if you can move. And moving an army across Europe is much harder than it sounds.
