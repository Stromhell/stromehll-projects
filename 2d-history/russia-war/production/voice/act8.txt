How the war goes from here is impossible to predict. This scenario is fiction. But Russia's pressure on Europe is not.

For years Europe has faced sabotage, assassination plots, cyber attacks and mystery drones. Since 2022, defence spending has jumped, especially in Germany.

Our scenario assumed America shows up in full. That is not guaranteed. The US president has openly questioned how far America would go to defend some allies.

Meanwhile Ukraine has held Russia's army back for years. In doing so, it is buying the rest of Europe something it badly needs: time.
