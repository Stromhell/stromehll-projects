# How a Russia-NATO War Would Actually Unfold

Working title: **How a Russia-NATO War Would Actually Unfold (a War-Game Scenario)**

9 parts (cold open + 8 acts), 48 shots, 1038 words, about 7:15 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A big NATO war-room table map of Europe. A 'race clock' in the corner counts the days NATO needs to reach the front against the hours Russia needs to grab the Baltics. Blue NATO arrows crawl east; red arrows push west. Every obstacle (a port hack, a burning signal box, the 8.5 cm rail gap) drops a red 'DELAY' tag on the clock.

**Look:** Map-driven war-game explainer in the house 2D style, Alium character style (as in BlackRock). Painted plates: a NATO headquarters war room, a foggy Baltic border with dragon's-teeth concrete blocks, a Texas cargo port at dawn, a stormy North Atlantic, a German rail yard at night, a narrow Polish-Lithuanian farm corridor, a Gotland harbour. Steel blue, olive and warning red. All soldiers generic with no real insignia. Putin and the US president never shown, named only in narration. SFX under the VO (radar pings, ship horns, jet roar, drone buzz, train clatter), music only at combine.
