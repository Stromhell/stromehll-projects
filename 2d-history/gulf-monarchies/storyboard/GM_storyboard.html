Why Gulf Kings Never Fall (GM) storyboard

Device: A running 'CROWNS STANDING' tracker in the top-left corner (six small gold crowns, one per Gulf state, that wobble when threatened and set themselves upright), plus a pearl that appears in every act (in a diver's hand, on a desk, in a bowl, on the Pearl Roundabout) as the symbol of the old Gulf.

Style: Stormi 2D house style (Gumball-style rig characters on painted plates, same engine as Prime/Pattinson). Plates: night Gulf skyline with interceptor streaks, pearl-diving dhow coast, old mud-brick fort town, British treaty tent, Baghdad palace 1958, desert border with tanks, Dhofar mountains, Salalah-style palace, 1970s oil field, modern ministry office, construction site with cranes, labour camp, generic grand mosque courtyard (no real likeness, respectful), GCC summit hall, Kuwait City 1990 night, US air base, Pearl Roundabout square, Riyadh luxury hotel lobby, parliament chamber, climate summit hall, sovereign wealth vault. Real people stylised, no likeness, no real logos. Official-style maps only (no disputed borders drawn). SFX under VO, music at combine.
