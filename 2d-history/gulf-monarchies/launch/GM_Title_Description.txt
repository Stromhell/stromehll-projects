TITLE
Why Gulf Kings Never Fall

(Other options: Iran Fired Thousands of Missiles at Them. Not One Crown Fell / How Six Gulf Royal Families Survived Everything)

DESCRIPTION
This year, Iran fired thousands of missiles and drones at the Gulf. Airports, refineries and US bases were targeted in all six Gulf kingdoms. Yet every single king, emir and sultan is still in charge. In the last century, the kings of Egypt, Iraq, Libya and Iran all fell. So why do the Gulf monarchies never fall?

This is the full story: the pearl-diving sheikhdoms of the 1800s, the British treaties that froze them in place, the oil that turned up in 1932, the palace coups where sons replaced fathers without a shot fired, the deal of no tax and no vote, the foreign workers tied to their sponsors, the 1979 Grand Mosque siege, Saddam's invasion of Kuwait, the Arab Spring, Mohammed bin Salman's Ritz-Carlton purge, and the biggest danger of all: what happens when the oil money runs out.

Sources: IMF "The Future of Oil and Fiscal Sustainability in the GCC Region" (2020), Al Jazeera reporting on the 2026 attacks (18 Nov 2026) and standard histories of the Gulf states. Commentary and analysis; all people are stylised cartoons, no real footage.
