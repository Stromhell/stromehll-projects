This year, Iran fired thousands of missiles and drones at six small Gulf kingdoms. Airports, refineries, American bases. All of them were targeted.

And every one of those six royal families is still in charge. Not a single crown dropped.

Which is odd, because the last century was a royal massacre round here. Egypt's king, gone. Iraq's, gone. Libya's, gone. The Shah of Iran, gone.

And no, it isn't just oil. Iraq, Libya and Iran had oil too, and their kings fell anyway. These Gulf families nearly lost everything, more than once. They survived by rebuilding the game around themselves.

So how do the Gulf kings never fall?
