In October twenty twenty-six, a casino company did something no sponsor had ever done. It paid a YouTuber with fifteen million subscribers... to stop posting.

The YouTuber was Jack Doh-er-tee. And when the casino's boss was asked if the deal was real, he answered in public. Yes. Six months. Money well spent to get this off the internet.

That reply got around two hundred and forty thousand likes. Roughly twenty-three times more than Jack's own post.

So how does someone become so disliked that paying him to disappear looks like good marketing? It starts, of all places, in a supermarket.

This is the story of how getting kicked out became a business. And then a bill.
