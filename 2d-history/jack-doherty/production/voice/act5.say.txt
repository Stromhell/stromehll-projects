At the W M Phoenix Open, golf's rowdiest week, Jack offered a fan a hundred dollars to shout an insult in the middle of a player's backswing. The fan did it. Jack paid up.

Security and police walked him out. An official told him, on camera, that he was trespassed from every future P G A Tour event. Jack asked: forever? For the foreseeable future, came the answer.

Jack called it the most harmless prank he could have done. The golfer, Mackenzie Hughes, still made the cut, and said he didn't lose much sleep over it.

But look at the bill. A supermarket stunt costs you a supermarket. This one cost him an entire sport. Same pattern as twenty seventeen. The difference is that the places kicking him out had started keeping a list.
