## Facts (as reported; allegations stay allegations)
- Jun 2017: "Floor is Lava Challenge at Walmart" video, passed 15M views. Started channel with friend Ricky getting kicked out of Walmart.
- 7 of his 15 biggest videos have Walmart / kicked out in the title; 48 "kicked out" uploads ~90M views total.
- Confrontations: "your kids can't read" lines before taking a punch. Clip appeared to show him saying the n-word; he claimed he said "hater". Then fired an ableist slur at fans.
- Around Halloween a fight ended in a knockout; he posted that his security knocked a guy out. Lawsuit alleged his security guard was involved; his lawyers pushed back; outcome not reported.
- Island Boys stream: told security if they try to touch me they can't leave the property breathing.
- Jun 2024: brawl with streamer N3on at a Florida fight night; Kick banned both. N3on: "Everyone just hates him".
- Dec 2025: his crew brawled with ex-UFC heavyweight champ Andrei Arlovski at a Jake Paul fight; both sides blamed each other; he posted "Arlovski versus my security guard".
- Kick launched Oct 2022 promising fewer rules (Tubefilter). Less than 2 weeks before the crash Kick acted after reports of Kick streamers harassing people at TwitchCon; CEO Ed Craven said a few individuals don't reflect the platform; accounts suspended.
- 5 Oct 2024: streaming from driver's seat of a ~$200K McLaren (570S) on a wet highway near Miami. NBC: clip appeared to show him glancing at phone before car veered into guardrail. Posted "Crashed my McLaren. Glad we're both okay." Viewers criticised him for caring more about the car than the cameraman, who needed stitches in his head. Later tweeted apology to cameraman (Car Throttle). Kick spokesperson: does not condone illegal activity; banned him. Kick guideline: prioritise your safety and safety of those around you.
- Days later on TikTok live asking for gifts ("Galaxy"); said he hydroplaned. Florida Highway Patrol crash report (Miami New Times via Car Throttle) gave him a citation for using phone while driving; resolution not reported.
- 9 Jan 2025: Kick channel back, no clear reason (Dexerto); had been reported permanent; lasted ~3 months. Crash parodied on Saturday Night Live.
- 9 days after return (Jan 2025) streaming on a Kick... (in Miami street) a stranger slapped him; FearedBuck posted clip 40K+ likes; Dexerto: he and security chased the man and beat him up. Kick banned him again same day. "I'm defending myself and I get banned."
- Feb 2025: says he emailed Kick, appeal rejected. "All 200,000 followers I built on your platform."
- 6 Feb 2026 WM Phoenix Open: offered crowd $100 to yell "jackass" on a golfer's backswing (golfer Mackenzie Hughes). Escorted out; official trespassed him from all future PGA Tour tournaments "for the foreseeable future" (Golf Magic first said lifetime, then clarified). He: "most harmless prank". Hughes still made the cut, didn't lose much sleep. Reply post: 548 likes, 3,000+ replies.
- Late Sep 2026: asked on X if he should stream on Kick and trade meme coins; Shuffle's account replied it would pay him to stop talking. IBTimes UK (citing NDTV): he went looking for sponsors after a memecoin project linked to him fell short; "needed to get my money up somehow".
- ~9 Oct 2026: posted he signed a contract with casino shuffle.com, didn't read it fully; a term says he can't post online anymore; working with his lawyer. ~1 h later Shuffle CEO (Noah Dummett): "Yes, it's real. 6-month term. Money well spent to get this off the internet." That reply got 241K likes, ~23x his post. Contract not public; scope/enforceability unclear; lawyers pressing to scrap it.
- A week later won a boxing match in Miami (Brand Risk 16) by first-round stoppage in ~30 s (MyMMANews).
- Newest video "Addressing My Past" (7 Oct 2026): "You got to admit I'm pretty chill... Everyone comes up and speaks to me." ~125K views; previous 20 uploads median ~85K; Island Boys era ~1M. 2023 Island Boys video 8.4M, nothing since topped it. Subs 15.1M.
- As of 20 Nov 2026 no new upload since 7 Oct (no reported link to the contract). In the newest upload he went back to Walmart, and walked past a spot he says he is banned from: "They just hate when I film here."
