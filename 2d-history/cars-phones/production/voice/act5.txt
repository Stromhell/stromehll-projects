And then, in May 2026, Ferrari showed its first fully electric car, the Luce, in Rome. The interior was designed with Jony Ive's studio. Yes, the man behind the iPhone.

The reaction was brutal. Ferrari's shares fell about eight percent in Milan, billions wiped off in a day. One former Ferrari boss reportedly called it a historic mistake.

Enzo Ferrari liked to say he sold engines, and the car came free. The Luce has no engine at all. Somewhere, Enzo is spinning.

Porsche learnt the hard way too. After betting big on electric, its operating profit for 2025 fell by about ninety-three percent.

And what did Porsche show next? The Mission S concept. A mid-engined supercar with a real key, real buttons, round dials with a proper needle, and no central screen. Need sat nav? There's a holder for your phone.

So maybe there's hope. Let carmakers build phones on wheels if they want. But we already have phones. When buyers stop paying for them, watch how fast the buttons come back.
