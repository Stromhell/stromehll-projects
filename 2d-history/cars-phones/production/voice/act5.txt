So is there a way back? Volkswagen has already promised the buttons are returning. And Porsche just went one step further.

And what did Porsche show next? The Mission S concept. A mid-engined supercar with a real key, real buttons, round dials with a proper needle, and no central screen. Need sat nav? There's a holder for your phone.

So maybe there's hope. Let carmakers build phones on wheels if they want. But we already have phones. When buyers stop paying for them, watch how fast the buttons come back.
