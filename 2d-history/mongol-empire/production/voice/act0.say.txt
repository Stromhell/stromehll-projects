About a million herders, living in felt tents on a cold grassland, built the largest connected land empire the world has ever seen.

At its height it stretched from Korea to Hungary, about twenty-four million square kilometres.

They beat Chinese dynasties, Persian sultans, Russian princes and European knights.

And the word Mughal, the dynasty that ruled much of India for three centuries, comes from the Persian word for Mongol.

It all started with a boy whose own clan left him and his family to starve.
