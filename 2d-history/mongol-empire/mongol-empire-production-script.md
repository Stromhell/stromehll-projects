# 1 Million Nomads Conquered the World. Here's How.

Working title: **How About a Million Herders Built the Largest Land Empire in History**

10 parts (cold open + 9 acts), 91 shots, 1915 words, about 13:18 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A red ink stain that spreads across a parchment world map, act by act, from one valley in Mongolia to the edge of Europe, then splits into four colours and fades. A small 'POPULATION ~1,000,000' tag stays pinned to the steppe while the stain grows.

**Look:** Calm, serious history documentary in the house 2D style. Painted plates: open steppe under huge skies, felt gers (yurts), frozen rivers, walled Silk Road cities in ochre brick, Baghdad's domes on the Tigris, a Japanese coast in a storm, Mughal Agra. Horses and composite bows drawn as detailed props, never simple shapes. SFX under the VO (hooves, wind, bowstrings, crowds); no music until combine.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Temujin's birth year is uncertain (sources give 1155 to 1167); script says 'around 1162'.
- Population 'about a million' and army size are estimates; the 1206 army was organised into about 95 units of a thousand.
- Empire area ~24 million km² at its height (late 13th century) as the largest contiguous land empire.
- Khwarazm: caravan massacred at Otrar in 1218 by governor Inalchuq; Shah Muhammad II executed one envoy and shaved the others. Invasion 1219; the Shah died on a Caspian island in late 1220 / early 1221. Battle of the Indus 1221.
- Army size for Khwarazm given as a range (estimates vary widely); death tolls stated as unknown or disputed throughout.
- Yam relay: begun under Genghis, formalised under Ogedei; script says 'a network of relay stations' without a founding date.
- Europe: Kyiv fell December 1240; Legnica 9 April 1241; Mohi 11 April 1241; Ogedei died December 1241. Reasons for withdrawal presented as debated (succession, grazing).
- Baghdad surrendered 10 February 1258; carpet story labelled as a story; 'river black with ink' attributed to chroniclers.
- Ain Jalut, 3 September 1260; Hulagu withdrew most forces after Mongke died in 1259 (news reached him in 1260).
- Kublai: Great Khan 1260 (war with Ariq Boke to 1264); Yuan proclaimed 1271; Song defeated 1279; Japan invasions 1274 and 1281.
- Caffa 1346 corpse-catapult story comes from Gabriele de' Mussi and is disputed; script says so.
- Black Death: 'perhaps a third or more' of Europe's population.
- Timur was not a Genghisid; used the title guregen (son-in-law). Sacked Delhi 1398.
- Babur descended from Timur (father's side) and Genghis Khan (mother's side). First Battle of Panipat 21 April 1526; Mughal rule ended 1857.
- Mongolia: Khalkha submitted to the Qing in 1691; independence declared 1911; Mongolian People's Republic 1924; democratic revolution 1990; Genghis Khan Equestrian Statue (40 m) completed 2008.
- DNA: Zerjal et al. 2003 — ~8% of men in a large region of Asia (~0.5% of men worldwide) share a lineage possibly from Genghis or his male-line relatives; hedged as 'may'.
- Death toll of the conquests stated as 'tens of millions, numbers debated', not a single figure.
