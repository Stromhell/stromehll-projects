To understand Prime, you have to remember that Logan Paul and K S I were supposed to hate each other. In twenty eighteen they fought twice, in front of millions.

So in January twenty twenty-two, when the two rivals turned up together with a sports drink, the internet's reaction was simple. Wait, what?

Prime Hydration was made with Congo Brands, a company in Kentucky that already sold energy drinks. Logan and K S I were the faces. Their fans were the marketing.

And in Britain, it went nuclear. Early twenty twenty-three, shoppers queued outside Al-dee before opening time. Bottles sold out in minutes.

Kids traded it in the playground like Poe-kay-mon cards. Some sellers asked silly money for a single bottle. Some schools banned it outright.

The numbers were real too. Bloomberg reported that Prime was heading for more than a billion dollars in yearly sales. At Walmart, it said it was outselling Gatorade.

Asked what it would be worth in a year, K S I said over a billion. Then he said: way beyond that.
