Remember those queues outside Al-dee? By twenty twenty-four, shoppers were posting photos of Prime on reduced-to-clear shelves.

The clearest numbers come from Britain. Prime's UK company filed its accounts at Companies House. In twenty twenty-three it took in about one hundred and twelve million pounds.

In twenty twenty-four, about thirty-three million. Sales down around seventy per cent. Profit down more than ninety.

The directors said the days of hyper-growth were over, and announced a strategic review. Which is corporate English for: we need a new plan.

So what happened? Hype is like a firework. Spectacular, loud, everyone looks up. Then it's over, and you need a product people buy again on a normal Tuesday.
