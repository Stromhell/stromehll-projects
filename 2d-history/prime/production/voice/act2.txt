On that Sydney radio show, they bragged about something else. Prime, they said, had a marketing budget of zero.

Which is a funny thing to say when your bottle is the official drink of the UFC. And Arsenal. And Barcelona. And, from 2023, the LA Dodgers.

Logan said he could cry when the UFC deal was signed. Zero pounds has never looked so expensive.

And here's the trick. Fans didn't just drink Prime. They filmed themselves drinking it. Every unboxing, every taste test, every reaction was free advertising.

The problem with a drink that sells on hype is simple. Sooner or later, somebody actually drinks it.
