Remember those queues outside Aldi? By 2024, shoppers were posting photos of Prime on reduced-to-clear shelves.

The clearest numbers come from Britain. Prime's UK company filed its accounts at Companies House. In 2023 it took in about one hundred and twelve million pounds.

In 2024, about thirty-three million. Sales down around seventy per cent. Profit down more than ninety.

The directors said the days of hyper-growth were over, and announced a strategic review. Which is corporate English for: we need a new plan.

So what happened? Hype is like a firework. Spectacular, loud, everyone looks up. Then it's over, and you need a product people buy again on a normal Tuesday.
