Then came the lawsuits. Lots of them. Some Prime started, some it didn't. Let's go through the big ones.

In 2024, Lionel Messi launched his own drink, called Más Plus. Prime said the bottles looked too much like theirs. Messi's side went to court first, and Prime counter-sued.

Logan joked he'd drop it if Messi fought him in the ring. Messi didn't. In January 2026, both sides agreed to drop their claims, and the case was dismissed.

Boxer Ryan Garcia posted that Prime was full of dangerous chemicals. In May 2024, Prime sued him for defamation. By the end of the month, Garcia had apologised.

A US class action claimed Prime contained so-called forever chemicals. Prime said that wasn't true, and a judge threw out most of the claims in September 2024.

The US Olympic committee said special Kevin Durant bottles used Olympic marks without paying for them. That one was settled.

And the biggest one came from its own supplier. Refresco, a bottling giant, says it built a whole production line just for Prime, and Prime walked away before ordering a single bottle. It's suing for nearly sixty-eight million dollars. Prime disputes it.

Think about that. A brand that said it was booming, and a bottling line built for it, standing silent.
