To understand Prime, you have to remember that Logan Paul and KSI were supposed to hate each other. In 2018 they fought twice, in front of millions.

So in January 2022, when the two rivals turned up together with a sports drink, the internet's reaction was simple. Wait, what?

Prime Hydration was made with Congo Brands, a company in Kentucky that already sold energy drinks. Logan and KSI were the faces. Their fans were the marketing.

And in Britain, it went nuclear. Early 2023, shoppers queued outside Aldi before opening time. Bottles sold out in minutes.

Kids traded it in the playground like Pokémon cards. Some sellers asked silly money for a single bottle. Some schools banned it outright.

The numbers were real too. Bloomberg reported that Prime was heading for more than a billion dollars in yearly sales. At Walmart, it said it was outselling Gatorade.

Asked what it would be worth in a year, KSI said over a billion. Then he said: way beyond that.
