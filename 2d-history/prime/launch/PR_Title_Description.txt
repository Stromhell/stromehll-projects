Remember Prime? Now Logan Paul Blames Everyone Else

DESCRIPTION
In 2023, Logan Paul and KSI said they'd probably buy Coca-Cola. Britain queued round the block outside Aldi, kids traded bottles in the playground, and Prime was reported to be heading for a billion dollars a year. Three years later, Prime's UK sales are down about 70%, the lawsuits have piled up, and Logan Paul says not selling at the top was a "massive" $100 million mistake... that wasn't his fault.

This is what really happened to Prime: the zero marketing budget, the taste test, the 200 mg caffeine can, Messi's lookalike drink, the bottling line that never bottled, and the line-up of everyone Logan blames.
