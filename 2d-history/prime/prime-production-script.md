# What Really Happened to Prime? Logan Paul, KSI and the $1 Billion Drink

Working title: **Prime Was Going to Buy Coca-Cola. Now Logan Paul Blames Everyone Else**

9 parts (cold open + 8 acts), 51 shots, 1232 words, about 8:23 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A 'hype meter' gauge in the corner that swings from MAX to EMPTY as the story moves, plus a 'CLAIM / COURT SAID' tag that stamps on every lawsuit.

**Look:** Business rise-and-fall comedy in the house 2D style, Gumball/t-shirt character style (big white eyes, nose, ears), painterly detailed plates: a Sydney radio studio, a British supermarket aisle with a queue, a school playground, a football stadium, a podcast studio, a Senate podium, a courthouse, a bottling plant, a clearance shelf, a boardroom. Real people stylised, no likeness, no real logos on bottles (generic bright bottles called PRIME only as text). Every lawsuit tagged CLAIM unless a court ruled. SFX under VO (crowd, till beeps, can cracks, gavel, stamps, phone buzz), music at combine.

## Hooks (yt-script hookscore)

- (9) In 2023 they said they'd probably buy Coca-Cola. In 2026, Logan Paul is putting a price on his regret: a hundred million dollars.
