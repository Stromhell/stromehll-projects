In 2008 Efraim, David, Alex and Merrill are charged with fraud. Efraim, David and Alex plead guilty to conspiracy to defraud the United States. No government official is charged.

David and Alex get house arrest. Stuck at home, David invents a drum-machine pedal for guitarists, and builds a business on it.

Merrill pleads not guilty, is convicted, and gets four years. The Swiss broker Thomet is never charged, and denies any wrongdoing.

While awaiting sentencing, Efraim is caught in an undercover sting: as a convicted felon, he picks up a gun an agent shows him. He's arrested on the spot.

In 2011 he's sentenced to four years in prison.
