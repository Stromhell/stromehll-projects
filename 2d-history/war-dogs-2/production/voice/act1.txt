Neither of the bosses wants to go anywhere near Albania. So David sends his friend Alex Podrizki, a broke graduate who once served in the French army.

The ammunition is decades old, in rusty metal tins on rotting pallets. The workers open crates with an axe and smoke cigarettes next to gunpowder.

Then he notices the writing on the tins. He calls David in Miami: you know this ammo is Chinese, right?

Efraim doesn't believe it. Maybe it's Korean? Send pictures. Alex emails photos. They'll later call that their first big mistake: written evidence.

Of course it's Chinese. For decades China was Albania's only real ally, and sold it weapons. But the contract banned Chinese-made ammunition.
