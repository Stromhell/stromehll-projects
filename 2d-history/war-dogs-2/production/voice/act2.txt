They ask the State Department for an exception. The answer: only the President can approve one. No time for that. Finding other suppliers would take months.

The safe choice was to call a lawyer and maybe lose the contract. Tens of millions of dollars were at stake. They chose to hide it.

Merrill suggests by email removing anything that shows where the ammo came from. The plan: repack it all into plain cardboard boxes.

In Tirana, Alex finds a businessman with a box factory, Kosta Trebicka. He tells him the repacking is to cut weight on the flights.

The job: repack around 100 million rounds by hand, tin by tin, into new boxes.
