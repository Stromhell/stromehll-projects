Back in twenty twenty-two, the whole hobby was hunting for one card. A one-of-one Luh-Bron James card carrying logo patches from three of his teams. Collectors called it the golden ticket.

Boxes were ripped open all over the world. Even celebrities joined in, opening boxes worth six figures live online, just to find it.

And Whatnot made a promise. If that card was pulled in a break on its platform, the seller would win a brand new Lamborghini.

Then it actually happened. Three complete strangers, each holding a random team in a Whatnot break, watched the seller pull it. The card carried all three of their teams.

It later sold at auction for around two point four million dollars, split three ways. Life-changing money, from spots that cost a tiny fraction of that.

A win like that is the best advert a platform could dream of. But in a documentary about the hunt, the presenter said it plainly: box breaks are the hobby's form of gambling.
