Description + chapters 

One evening in February 2025, a man spent $25,000 on a single purchase. Not a car. A spot in a live stream, for a chance at some trading cards. The app was Whatnot: launched in 2019, now valued at $20 billion, and promoted by thousands of creators.

So is Whatnot a shopping app, or, as a wave of legal claims alleges, a casino nobody licensed? We explain box breaks and mystery repacks, the 2022 hunt for the 1-of-1 "golden ticket" card, the "pay, prize, chance" lottery argument, the gaps in Whatnot's spending limits, dark patterns, and the people who say the hobby turned into a gambling problem. Plus how China regulates live shopping, and why the UK, US and EU are still catching up.

Whatnot says gambling is not allowed on its platform and that the policy is strictly enforced. The legal claims described here are allegations and have not been proven in court.

Sources: The Wall Street Journal and The New York Times reporting on Whatnot (2026), and the documentary "The Worst New YouTube Sponsor" by Fern (interviews with Paul Lesko, Dr Mark Leiser and a former user). Commentary and analysis; all people are stylised cartoons, no real footage.
