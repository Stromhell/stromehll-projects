1989, a suburb in South Florida. Jonathan is six, and already can't be pulled away from the family computer. His dad is a programmer.

His parents add parental controls. He gets around them. As a young teen he teaches himself programming from books, and one day wipes Windows off the family PC and installs Linux.

When his parents take his computer away, he runs away from home until he gets it back.

By 15 he's one of the most skilled young hackers around. Online, he's 'c0mrade'.
