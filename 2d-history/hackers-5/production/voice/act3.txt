January 2000. He's just turned 16. His mum wakes him: the FBI is at the door.

They take five computers, his Palm Pilot, and even his Star Trek book. He admits everything.

He becomes the first juvenile in the US sentenced to detention for computer crimes: house arrest, probation, apology letters to NASA and the Pentagon.

After breaking his probation rules, he spends six months in a juvenile facility. He comes out saying he's done with hacking for good.
