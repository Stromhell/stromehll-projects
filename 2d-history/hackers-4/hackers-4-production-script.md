Working title: **The Boy Who Hacked People, Not Computers (The Kids Who Hacked the World, Part 4)**

8 parts (cold open + 7 acts), 34 shots, 751 words, about 5:16 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A magician's stage: every con opens with a curtain and a playing card that flips to reveal the trick ('the bus ticket', 'the phone call', 'the guard'), and a WANTED poster that gets bigger every act.

**Look:** Retro crime caper in the house 2D style, Gumball/t-shirt character style (same as Parts 1-3). Warm 70s browns and 80s neon green screens. Real people stylised with no likeness. Social engineering described only as 'pretending to be staff' and 'asking nicely'; no technical how-to. SFX under the VO (payphone coins, dial tones, modem screech, tape reels, gavel), music at combine.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Personal anecdotes (bus tickets, Springsteen number, DEC game) are from Mitnick's memoir Ghost in the Wires and flagged as his account.
- DEC arrest December 1988; sentenced 1989 to 1 year (plus halfway house).
- Fugitive from late 1992; arrested Raleigh 15 Feb 1995.
- 1999 plea; total ~5 years in custody including pre-trial; restitution $4,125; released 21 Jan 2000.
- 'Whistle into a phone and launch nukes' claim is Mitnick's account of prosecutors' arguments; worded as such.
- Shimomura voicemails' authorship disputed (Littman); not stated as Mitnick's.
- Mitnick died 16 July 2023, aged 59.
- All technical steps (spawn commands, IP spoofing, backdoors) removed; methods described as phone pretexting only.
