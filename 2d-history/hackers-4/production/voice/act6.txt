Christmas 1994. Someone breaks into the computers of Tsutomu Shimomura, a top security researcher in San Diego, and steals his files.

Then come mocking voicemails in a fake kung-fu-movie voice. Shimomura takes it personally, and teams up with the FBI.

With help from an internet provider, they trace the intruder's connections to Raleigh, North Carolina, and watch him online in real time.

That brings agents to the apartment door, and the pay stub in the ski jacket. In court, Kevin passes Shimomura and says, 'I respect your skills.'
