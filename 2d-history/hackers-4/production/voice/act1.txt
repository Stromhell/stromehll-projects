Los Angeles, 1975. Kevin is 12 and loves magic tricks. What fascinates him most is how easily people believe what they're shown.

He rides buses all summer. By his own account, he chats up a driver about a 'school project' and learns where the ticket punch is sold.

Unused transfer tickets get thrown away at the depot. With a punch and the spares, he rides for free all summer.

In high school a friend shows him how one phone call to the phone company can get an unlisted number. Kevin is hooked.
