Out of prison, he can't find work, and gets bored. Then a hacker calling himself Eric Heinz gets in touch, bragging about secret phone-company systems.

Kevin gets into a phone-company testing system that can listen in on calls. But something about Heinz feels wrong.

Digging into his phone records, Kevin finds Heinz has been calling the FBI. He's an informant.

In 1992, with a new arrest warrant out, Kevin vanishes. He lives under false names in different cities for more than two years.
