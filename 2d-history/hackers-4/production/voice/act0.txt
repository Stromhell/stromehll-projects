February 1995, Raleigh, North Carolina. FBI agents knock on an apartment door. Is he Kevin Mitnick? He says no.

In the pocket of an old ski jacket, they find a pay stub with his real name on it. After more than two years on the run, America's most wanted hacker is caught.

His secret weapon was rarely code. It was people. A friendly voice, a confident story, and staff who wanted to help.
