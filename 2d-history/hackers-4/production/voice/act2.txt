He joins the 'phone phreaks', teens who explore the phone network. He calls phone-company offices pretending to be an employee, and learns how everything works.

Soon he can get almost any unlisted number. In his memoir he says he got Bruce Springsteen's, just for fun.

At 17 he and two friends talk their way past a guard into a phone-company building, and walk out with operating manuals.

A friend brags about it. Kevin is arrested and tried as a juvenile: a psychological evaluation and a year of probation.
