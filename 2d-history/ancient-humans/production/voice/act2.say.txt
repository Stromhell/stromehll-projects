You wake at first light, on your own, because you slept enough.

Last night's fire is still alive. People sit around it, eating leftover meat and nuts.

The talk is simple: where the water is good, where someone saw fresh tracks. That is the whole plan for the day, and everyone has a say in it.

Today, nobody needs to hunt. The last kill was two days ago, and there is still food.

A few people walk out to gather roots, nuts and berries.

You stay and work on your gear. You sharpen a spear point. You work a stiff hide until it turns soft.

Not because a boss told you to. Here, skill earns respect, and respect is worth more than anything you could own.

By early afternoon, the jobs that needed doing are done.
