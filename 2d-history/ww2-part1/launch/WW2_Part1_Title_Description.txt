Description:
June 1940. In less than a year Germany had taken seven countries, and Britain stood almost alone. How did one treaty signed in 1919 lead here?

Part 1 of Why World War 2 Happened follows the road from Versailles to 1940: the war guilt clause and a bill Germany only finished paying in 2010, bread that cost 200 billion marks, a failed coup that made Hitler famous, Austria and the Sudetenland handed over for "peace for our time", a secret deal with Stalin, Blitzkrieg on meth, the Phony War, Finland's stand, the Ardennes trick and the boats of Dunkirk.
