Working title: **How Hitler Took Europe in One Year: Why World War 2 Happened, Part 1**

9 parts (cold open + 8 acts), 51 shots, 970 words, about 6:51 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** One big parchment map of Europe that the story keeps returning to. Germany is a grey blob with a frowning face; every country it swallows turns grey, and a running counter in the corner ticks up the countries taken. A ticking 'deal clock' marks every broken promise.

**Look:** Map-driven history explainer in the house 2D style, Leo sheet character style (as in Ancient Humans and Rothschilds). Painted plates: a Versailles hall of mirrors, a 1923 Berlin bakery queue with wheelbarrows of cash, a Munich conference table, a Polish plain with horses and tanks, a dark Ardennes forest road, the beach at Dunkirk with little boats, a London radio studio. Muted parchment, navy and brick red; German forces in plain grey with no insignia. SFX under the VO (radio crackle, marching, tank rumble, aircraft, waves), music only at combine.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Reparations: London Schedule 1921, 132 billion gold marks (about $33 billion then); final bond interest payment 3 Oct 2010.
- Bread prices: Berlin, about 163 marks (1922) to about 200 billion marks (Nov 1923).
- Hitler appointed chancellor 30 Jan 1933; dictatorship via Enabling Act (Mar 1933) and one-party state (Jul 1933). Transcript's 'declared himself dictator' corrected.
- Luftwaffe revealed and conscription restored March 1935; Anglo-German Naval Agreement June 1935; Rhineland remilitarised March 1936 (added).
- Maginot Line built from 1929, before Hitler took power; transcript implied it was a response to him. Length figure dropped.
- Anschluss: troops crossed 12 Mar 1938, Austria annexed 13 Mar; Schuschnigg resigned 11 Mar. Transcript's Fischböck detail dropped.
- Munich Agreement 30 Sep 1938 (Germany, Britain, France, Italy; Czechoslovakia excluded).
- Poland: invaded 1 Sep 1939 with about 1.5 million troops (transcript said 1 million); Soviets invaded 17 Sep; last resistance ended 6 Oct. Transcript's 'occupied 8 Sept / under a week' corrected.
- Polish cavalry charging tanks is a myth; script says only that the army relied heavily on horses.
- Winter War 30 Nov 1939 to 13 Mar 1940 (over 3 months); Leningrad about 32 km from the border (transcript said 50 km); Finland ceded about 11% of territory.
- Norway campaign 9 Apr to 10 Jun 1940; Denmark fell in hours.
- Dunkirk: Operation Dynamo 26 May to 4 Jun 1940, about 338,000 evacuated (transcript said 350,000).
- Pact of Steel signed May 1939; Italy declared war 10 Jun 1940; French armistice 22 Jun 1940.
- Churchill's 'fight on the beaches' speech 4 Jun 1940 (paraphrased, not quoted at length).
