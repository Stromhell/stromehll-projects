To pay, Germany printed money. By 1923 prices were doubling every few days.

In Berlin, a loaf of bread cost about 160 marks in 1922. By November 1923 it cost around 200 billion.

People carried cash in wheelbarrows. Savings built over a lifetime were worth nothing.

In this chaos, a young agitator named Adolf Hitler tried to seize power in Munich in 1923. The coup failed and he went to prison, but it made him famous.

He blamed Germany's humiliation on Jews and on communists. It was a lie, but angry, frightened people listened.
