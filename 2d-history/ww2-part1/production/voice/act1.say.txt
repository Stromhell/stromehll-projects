nineteen nineteen. World War One is over, and the winners meet at Versailles, near Paris, to write the peace.

One line, Article 231, put the blame for the war's losses on Germany. Germans called it the war guilt clause.

France and Britain wanted Germany to pay. In nineteen twenty-one the bill was set at 132 billion gold marks, about 33 billion dollars at the time.

Germany didn't finish paying off the last of those debts until twenty ten.

The army was cut to 100,000 men. No air force. Almost no navy.
