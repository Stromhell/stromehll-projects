In February 1938 Hitler summoned Austria's chancellor, Kurt Schuschnigg, and bullied him into putting Nazis in his government.

Schuschnigg called a vote on independence. Before it could happen, German troops crossed the border. On 13 March 1938 Austria was declared part of Germany.

Next was the Sudetenland, a border region of Czechoslovakia where about three million German speakers lived. Hitler demanded it.

In September 1938 Britain's prime minister, Neville Chamberlain, met Hitler in Munich with France and Italy. Czechoslovakia wasn't even in the room.

They handed him the Sudetenland in return for a promise of no more war. Chamberlain flew home waving the paper and promised 'peace for our time'.

Six months later, in March 1939, German troops took the rest of Czechoslovakia. The promise was worthless.
