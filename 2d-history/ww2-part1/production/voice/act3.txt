Then came the Great Depression in 1929. By early 1933 about six million Germans were out of work.

In January 1933 Hitler became chancellor. Within months he had banned other parties and made himself a dictator.

His plan was an empire for a so-called master race, with 'living space' taken from the east.

In 1935 he revealed an air force and brought back conscription, breaking the treaty. Britain even signed a deal letting Germany build a navy.

In 1936 his troops marched into the Rhineland, next to France. France and Britain did nothing.

France was already relying on the Maginot Line, a huge chain of forts along its border with Germany.
