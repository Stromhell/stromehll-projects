Then came the Great Depression in nineteen twenty-nine. By early nineteen thirty-three about six million Germans were out of work.

In January nineteen thirty-three Hitler became chancellor. Within months he had banned other parties and made himself a dictator.

His plan was an empire for a so-called master race, with 'living space' taken from the east.

In nineteen thirty-five he revealed an air force and brought back conscription, breaking the treaty. Britain even signed a deal letting Germany build a navy.

In nineteen thirty-six his troops marched into the Rhineland, next to France. France and Britain did nothing.

France was already relying on the Maginot Line, a huge chain of forts along its border with Germany.
