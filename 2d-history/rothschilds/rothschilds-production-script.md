# How One Family Became Richer Than Kings

Working title: **The Rothschilds: Five Brothers, Five Capitals, and the Loans That Built the 1800s**

8 parts (cold open + 7 acts), 58 shots, 1290 words, about 8:57 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** Five arrows (the family crest) that fly out of Frankfurt to London, Paris, Vienna and Naples in Act 2 and stay pinned to a parchment map of Europe. Each later loan is a gold line drawn from one of those arrows to a war, a railway or a canal. A small 'LEGEND' or 'FACT' tag marks every famous story so viewers can tell which is which.

**Look:** Calm, serious history documentary in the house 2D style, Leo sheet character style (as in Ancient Humans): clean outlined figures in period dress, frock coats and top hats, embedded in detailed painted plates. Plates: the cramped Frankfurt Judengasse at dusk, a candle-lit counting house, the London Royal Exchange in fog, a Paris salon, Bordeaux vineyards, a 1931 Vienna bank run, a 1938 Vienna street. Ledger-paper maps with gold ink. SFX under the VO (quill scratch, coins, carriage wheels, crowds); music only at combine.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Names fixed from transcript: Mayer Amschel (not 'Maya Amshel'), Hesse-Kassel ('Hessa Castle'), Simon Wolf Oppenheimer, Leopold I, Creditanstalt, Lehman.
- Suez 1875: £4m from N M Rothschild & Sons to Disraeli's government for 176,602 Suez Canal Company shares (~44%). Grape/'security' exchange is an anecdote; labelled 'the story goes'.
- Waterloo fortune story: debunked by historians (e.g. Niall Ferguson; Brian Cathcart, 'The News from Waterloo', 2015); originated in an 1846 pamphlet by 'Satan' (Georges Dairnvaell). Labelled LEGEND/MYTH.
- Five sons: Amschel (Frankfurt), Salomon (Vienna), Nathan (London), Carl (Naples), James (Paris). Austrian barony 1822.
- 1835 slavery compensation: £15m loan arranged by Nathan Rothschild and Moses Montefiore; total compensation £20m; enslaved people received nothing.
- British Relief Association 1847, co-founded by Lionel de Rothschild. Lionel first practising Jewish MP 1858 after elections from 1847.
- Mouton bought 1853 by Nathaniel; Lafite bought 1868 by James. Phylloxera hit France from the 1860s.
- De Beers Consolidated 1888 financed with N M Rothschild backing.
- Creditanstalt failure May 1931; Rothschilds main shareholders.
- Louis Rothschild arrested March 1938, released 1939 after holdings handed over. Elisabeth de Rothschild died in Ravensbrück 1945.
- Banque Rothschild nationalised 1981 under Mitterrand. Dropped: unsourced family wealth estimates; 'insider trading' framing; Zionist land-purchase detail (out of scope, avoid political framing).
