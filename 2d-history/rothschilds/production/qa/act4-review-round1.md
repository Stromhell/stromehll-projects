## Issues

1. **≈15.5–17.6 s (4.02), MEDIUM: characters walk through James.**
   - The two passengers walking right pass straight across James. At 16.8 s the blue-suited walker's head is in front of James's raised arm, and his body covers James's torso.
   - Earlier, from about 12 to 14 s, James's pointing hand sits on the front of the moving locomotive while the train slides past under it.
   - **Fix:** route the walkers behind James (or stop them before x≈1150), or have James step back. Lower his arm before the locomotive reaches his hand.

2. **≈16–17.6 s (4.02), MEDIUM: walker's feet behind the subtitle.** The brown-coated woman's feet (x≈920–980, y≈950) are hidden behind the subtitle box ("bank had paid for."). **Fix:** move the walk line about 50 px higher, or time the walkers so they cross outside x 770–1150 while that line is on screen.

3. **18–25 s (4.03), MEDIUM: crowd feet behind the subtitle.** Whenever a subtitle is up, the feet of the centre crowd are behind the box. The top-hat man at x≈690–750 and the man in the brown coat at x≈1000–1080 have their feet at y≈990–1005. The feet show in the gaps between lines (22.5 s), so the problem comes and goes. **Fix:** raise the front row about 70 px or push them further back.

4. **44–50.7 s (4.06), MEDIUM: porters' feet hidden by labels, and the porters are clones.**
   - The third porter's feet (x≈1520–1600, y≈975–1010) are behind the "IRELAND · 1847" tag. The first porter's shoes clip the subtitle box.
   - All three porters have the same face style, clothes, sack and walk phase.
   - **Fix:** move the tag to the top-right, or move the walk line up about 60 px. Vary the porters: different jackets and caps, one with a sack on his shoulder, one with a barrel, and offset walk phases.

5. **≈54.0–54.4 s and ≈55.2–55.6 s (4.07), MEDIUM: the stamp covers Lionel's face.** The second red "REFUSED" stamp is placed over the door at x≈830–1090, y≈510–610. As Lionel walks to the door and back, his head passes under it, and his face is covered by the red stamp. **Fix:** put that stamp on the door panel lower down (y≈640–700, below his head height), or keep it hidden until he has turned away.

6. **50.7–59 s (4.07), MEDIUM, rule 11: Parliament has low detail.**
   - The Commons is a plain dark block with one arched door, plain grey steps and two tower silhouettes (a clock tower and a striped block) in fog.
   - There is no Gothic tracery, no windows, no railings and no doorkeepers. It is the kind of flat backdrop Pushpam flagged in 4.02.
   - **Fix:** paint a Gothic Westminster frontage with pointed windows, pinnacles and a carved doorway, and add a doorkeeper or two MPs on the steps.

7. **33–43.5 s (4.05), LOW: identical figures in the inset.** In the "THE ENSLAVED GOT NOTHING" inset, two of the four figures are exact copies (same clothes, face and pose), and all four make the same gesture. Vary the poses (arms folded, hands open, a woman, a child).

8. **70.5–74 s (4.09), LOW: coins fall across the train.** The gold coins fall out of the sky into the chest, passing in front of the locomotive and the carriages. One also shows behind the "PROPPING UP" card. Start the coins below the track line, or have them pour from a sack or hand.

9. **19–25 s (4.03), LOW: sentries tangled with the gate bars.** The two sentries stand inside the open gate leaves, so gate bars run across their arms. There is also a bar at each sentry's side that could be a rifle, but neither holds it. Stand them in front of the posts, each holding a musket.

10. **4.02, LOW:** the round object on wheels on the right of the platform (x≈1600–1720) is hard to read. It looks like a dropped pan. Replace it with a clear porter's barrow.

11. **84–91.5 s (4.11), LOW:** the Naples arcade is clean and empty. A few passers-by or shuttered windows would help, but the sign-lowering action carries the shot.
