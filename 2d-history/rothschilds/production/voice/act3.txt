In London, Nathan became the star. In the last years of the Napoleonic Wars he helped move huge sums of gold to pay British armies on the continent.

June 1815: the Battle of Waterloo. Napoleon is beaten.

A famous story says Nathan got the news first by courier, pretended to sell British bonds to start a panic, then secretly bought them cheap and made a fortune.

Historians say that version is a myth, spread later by pamphlets, some of them openly antisemitic. He did get early news. There's no good evidence of the great swindle.

The real money came later, and more slowly: lending to governments after the war, and trading their bonds as Europe rebuilt.

In 1822, the Austrian emperor made all five brothers barons. Sons of the Frankfurt ghetto were now nobility.
