A Jewish family this visible became a target. Through the late eighteen hundreds, antisemitic writers turned the Rothschild name into a symbol of imagined Jewish control of the world.

Those myths were false then and they are false now. The family was rich and powerful. It did not secretly run Europe.

In nineteen thirty-one, Austria's biggest bank, the Creditanstalt, collapsed. The Vienna Rothschilds were its leading shareholders. The crash spread banking panic across Europe.

The Nazis used the name in their propaganda, including a nineteen forty film. When Germany took Austria in nineteen thirty-eight, Baron Louis Rothschild was arrested and held for about a year.

He was released only after the family handed over their Austrian holdings. Across occupied Europe, Rothschild banks, homes and art were seized.

Many family members escaped to Britain, the US or Switzerland. Some, like Elisabeth de Rothschild, died in the camps.
