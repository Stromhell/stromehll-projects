After the war the family rebuilt, but the world had moved. New York, not London or Paris, was the centre of money. In 1981, France's socialist government nationalised the Paris bank.

Today Rothschild & Co is still an independent advisory bank in Paris and London, and the family still owns famous vineyards. But no single Rothschild sits anywhere near the world's richest lists.

The fortune was split among many heirs over two centuries. Online claims of 'trillions' are fantasy.

What's real is enough: a boy from a locked street in Frankfurt, five sons in five capitals, and the loans that paid for the railways, wars and canals of the 1800s.
