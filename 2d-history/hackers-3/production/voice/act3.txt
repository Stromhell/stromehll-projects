Top of their list: the director of the CIA. Cracka finds his phone company, then calls its support line pretending to be one of its own technicians.

The support worker believes him and reads out private account details. With enough personal details, Cracka persuades the email company to reset the password.

He's now inside the CIA director's personal email account. Then come the prank calls to his phone, and to his family.

Nobody knows who's behind it, or what they want. The director's security has to treat it as a real threat.
