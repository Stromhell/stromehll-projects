2008, Virginia. An American teenager who goes by 'Default' likes soccer, video games and his dog. His father works for the government.

It starts with cheating in video games. Then he wants to know how computers really work, and how to make them do things they shouldn't.

Online, the hacker collective Anonymous is everywhere, in the news, in chat rooms. He hangs around their channels and starts breaking into websites just to see if he can.

He gets paranoid about being caught. He connects through a satellite dish to a free Wi-Fi network half a mile away, and keeps every device encrypted.

He also buys Bitcoin early. By his own account, more than a thousand coins, kept on a hard drive in his room.
