October 2015. The head of the CIA gets a call on his private phone from a number he doesn't know. The caller laughs at him, and says he's already inside his email.

On the other end: a 15-year-old in his bedroom on a housing estate in England. He calls himself Cracka.

His crew, Crackas With Attitude, would embarrass the US intelligence world for months, and land its members in prison.
