## Prologue
- Night road: a guy and a girl fight over a car ("it's my mum's car"). Police arrive. She whispers: it's stolen, follow my lead; tells cops he's her boyfriend, arguing over who drives. Cops check plates, they drive off. She stole it for her mum, who wants to be an estate agent and must look rich. Names: Jason and Lucia. Story starts with a stolen car and a lie that becomes true.
- Jump: living together; Jason goes on jobs, returns with cash. Lucia wants in ("I can handle a gun"). Jason: "this life isn't for you." Lucia: fine, I'll do it alone. Hard cut: prison call, Lucia Caminos, inmate at Hamlet Correctional. Opening credits.
- Counsellor Stephanie Gates: overcrowding, supervised release with electronic tag. "How did you get here?" Flashback: broke in a coffee shop, says money's in the car, robs a payday-loan shop, robbery goes wrong, escapes, then comes back and is caught. In the ambulance: "I left something at the coffee shop." It was her baby son Tomás. Child welfare takes him.
- Gates: custody is a separate case, prove a normal life. Address = the father's home, Jason Duval, who has a record. Lucia: it was a wake-up call, he's on his best behaviour. Cut to Jason on a boat surrounded by drug packets.

## Chapter 1: "Just Like Paradise"
- Leonida Keys (game's Florida Keys). Jason works for old smuggler Brian, lives free at his place. Cal (tech guy, sees conspiracies), Donny (seaplane pilot).
- Donny skimmed a gang's delivery; gang (King Fishers) grabs him; Jason rescues him and takes their cash too. Donny lies to Brian that the count was right; Jason covers.
- Lucia calls: they might let me out. Jason hesitates; "I already lost you once." Jason ex-army, left on bad terms (game never explains).
- Brian's broker Arnold (15 years) goes silent: "you started a turf war; your 40-50 kilos don't matter, the real players move tonnes on cargo ships." Brian: shut him up. Jason: "taken care of." No broker, no supply.
- Cal asks why Lucia left. Jason: she wanted in, I said no, came back and she was gone; she was pregnant, he didn't know; "maybe I made her feel I didn't want a family... scared I'd pass on whatever's wrong with me."
- Release day flashback: full prologue call. "You ghosted me." "That's what a break-up is." "I read in the news you have a kid." "Tomás. You're his dad. Are you angry?" "I think this is the happiest I've ever been."
- Lucia out with ankle monitor, must stay within half a mile of Jason's, 9 months. Tommy is with Lucia's aunt in Liberty State (GTA IV's Liberty City state).
- Core argument: cellmate Cat walked out and was on a yacht popping bottles, "like she had a secret key; I want that." Jason: working for rich people and being rich are different. Lucia: I don't want Tommy growing up like we did.
- Welcome party: Cat arrives pregnant with Italian boyfriend Mattia from Vice City. Jason asks for work; Mattia: "my boss is a very private person"; brags "if you knew what Cat and I had in our hands before coming here..."
- Bank refuses an account (bad credit). Phone app with a family savings account, money goes in but can't come out; they start saving for Tommy. REMEMBER THIS ACCOUNT.
- Brian finds a new broker; simple run; Lucia comes (Cal: last time you said no she left). Deal goes bad; Lucia saves them. 30 missed calls from parole officer; monitor says she was a mile out at sea; he threatens custody ("my word carries weight"); lets her go this time.
- Lucia refuses to video-call Tommy until everything is set and real. As far as the game shows, Jason has never met his son.
- Donny keeps selling stolen product behind Brian's back; Jason saves him again; "I'm going to fix this."
- Mattia returns with Burim, who removes the monitor and sits outside with it. First trip to Vice City together: yacht, supercars, VIP table. Mattia: how did cops catch you? Lucia: "They're not catching me again."
- Mattia: Cat and I dropped a bag (a payment), boss changed his mind, pick it up from a locker at Grassrivers visitor centre, deliver it, $ (amount unclear). Jason: why not do it yourself? No answer. Lucia: we'll take it.
- Donny sets up peace meeting with King Fishers at a slaughterhouse. Jason to Brian: I need the work. "Where's Donny?" Pig pen: they fed Donny to the pigs. Shootout; they kidnap Brian's wife; Jason and Brian rescue her. Brian: "I'm out, retiring." Income gone in the same hour Jason asked for big work. Need money for a custody lawyer. Lucia: not something steady, "something that's worth it."
- Mission "Tourist Trap": wig for Lucia, Mattia-style shirt for Jason ("they expect me and Cat"). Rule: if it's cops, don't pull a gun. Open locker, bag out: "You're under arrest." Back room, a sergeant asks who sent them. Detective Mendez arrives with his team, friendly, asks for the bag; sergeant refuses; Mendez leaves his men, who shoot the sergeant's team, then radio "officer down, suspect had a weapon... clean it up, 60 seconds." Cops killed cops for the bag, blame on Jason and Lucia. Jason shot: "whoever comes through that door isn't here to arrest us." Lucia fights them out (the girl told "this life isn't for you").
- Lucia gives Jason + bag to Cal for hospital, goes home for cash; men with broken English: "you have bag, give me now." House burns; "I think they died." Cops ask where the girlfriend is. She reaches the Vice City hospital in a friend's car. Two teams at the hospital: the bag's owners and Mendez's cops. Cop corners Jason: "Mendez, I think I got him." Radio: "check the package first, then light him up." Escape in a stolen ambulance; Jason: "hey, thanks for visiting."
- End Ch1: house burnt, boss retired, cops think they're cop killers, two sides hunting them, and they don't know what's in the bag.

## Chapter 2: "Paradise Lost"
- Cheap motel near Port Gellhorn, west of Vice City. News: officers lost; faces not in footage.
- Bag: small heavy ancient-looking statue with a note "From my collection. She reminded me of someone." Plus an unlabelled hard drive.
- First half of chapter = Lucia alone: suspicious manager raises rent; neighbour Vicky (dancer, has a kid) helps; Lucia tells her the truth. Fights the town's pill gang for real painkillers; steals cars to sell for rent.
- Drive encrypted. "A ticket to wherever we want to go." Waffles, then she hands him a mask: first robbery together, they're good at it. That night, one word: "Trust." REMEMBER: almost everyone will break their trust; question is whether they break each other's.
- Plan: back to Vice City, hide in plain sight, sell the drive, get their son. Only Mattia knows their faces (dead or in Italy). School friend Dre'Quan runs a club, can find them a place.
- Vicky: "I know who you are"; knows her club's safe code. Ladies' night: dancer passes out, Jason sent on stage as stripper so Lucia can reach the safe. Lucia caught, they fight out with the money.
- Cal sends article: couple shot, put in a car boot, "thought it was you two." Checkout day: gunmen ask for room six; motel shootout; escape on a bike. Who they were / how found: game doesn't say.
- Vice City, club Jack of Hearts. Dre'Quan says "my club"; really owned by Boobie Ike, who owns flats across the city; they get one. The man whose roof they sleep under is the one to watch.

## Chapter 3: "Money" (Ike's line)
- Ike explains: the club pays for his music studio, money pays for everything. Makes Jason sell his product. First deal ends in a shootout; Dre'Quan warns Jason about Ike.
- Cal visits, gives up: military-grade encryption. But Brian did time with someone who handles this level.
- Statue: antiques area, Benedict Halifax: "a bronze Venus, extremely valuable if it's real." Keeps it to check; then voicemail forever.
- Lucia's mum Mia plays a voicemail from the prison board: daughter missing work, ignoring calls. With Mia, Lucia breaks into an M7 gang house (they don't forget).
- Brian at dinner names Raul: bank robber big enough to sell such a drive. Test: Lucia steals a car from the port belonging to the city's biggest cartel; Raul sends her a photo of herself doing it (leverage). First meeting: police chase; they were decoys while Raul's real crew scored elsewhere. Crew: Andrea (drives?), Karim (tech), Felipe (driver), Valentina (launders money).
- Raul's plan: rob cartel cash so the cartel moves all its money into one place, its own bank's vault. Needs money and practice: three small banks in one day in old-people masks. Jason: let's take this and disappear with Tommy. Lucia: not enough. Their cut 15%, about $1m on paper.
- Kidnap vault designer Nicole Park; Raul turns her. Explosives via a pilot chased by the IAA (in-game spy agency). Retired Brian drives through gunfire as a favour. Cartel money man dangled over a pool for a box number; asks for plane ticket + new identity; "yeah we can do that", then "put him down".
- Lucia: smart move for Raul would be to frame us and sell the drive. No better option, so they trust Raul and tell him about the drive ("cops shot other cops to get this"). Raul: bring it, Valentina can do identities.
- Ike in their bed: "you all owe me"; sends them to collect from a building; police task force raid; they see Mendez; residents say Ike knew about the raid. Flat unsafe; drive into a storage locker.
- "The Cartel Heist": blow cell towers; Jason runs the lobby; Lucia drills the vault and holds a cable by hand while Nicole cracks the code (one slip = hard lock). Diamonds and bearer bonds. Valentina moves them to a beach five-star hotel as Mr and Mrs Walker, sets up meeting with the best-connected lawyer, Abraham "Abe" Gonzalez. Warning: "It's a dream, and you pay for it by the night." Balcony: "I want him back, J." "I know. Me too."

## Chapter 4: "Vice City, Baby"
- Abe: "I am Vice City", you need to "alter reality", new identities, too expensive. Sends Jason out; tells Lucia: give up your dude, say he scared you; no identity needed and half a million for your TV story. Lucia: "What? No." FIRST time someone tells Lucia to drop Jason.
- Abe's price: security for a TV-network owner's son-in-law; party attacked; "I can make the Walkers real, passports... maybe the Walkers have a kid." Family's child kidnapped; Abe: "how many kids have you lost so far? bring this one back or forget yours." They rescue the child; passports move forward.
- Recover the statue from Halifax. Drive from locker to Raul. Karim can't open it but finds the owner: Giorgio Gallo, Mattia's boss, shipping magnate; drugs found on his ships repeatedly; government seized his fleet (Arnold's "tonnes on cargo ships"). Why Mattia didn't collect: the bag was a payoff for someone (never named; note meaning unknown); police found out and watched the locker; recipient backed off; so they sent Jason and Lucia to take the fall. The cop chasing it: Aldo Mendez.
- Next score: Cordell Grant "KG", bodybuilder crew, old score with Raul. KG thinks Jason is a cop; Lucia goes undercover alone for weeks; "always doing one thing to get out of another: Mattia, then Dre, now Raul; when do we call the shots?" Nate befriends her, nickname "Juicy". KG: saw you in the news, payday-loan place; betray me and you're dead.
- Airport vault: ~$10m cash monthly from Latin America plus fine wine. Lucia poses as wine expert; crew takes cash to fly to Colombia; Raul's plan: swap the bags, KG finds fake money.
- Karim opens one clip: a party, "now Senator, you know you're not supposed to start without Big Daddy." Senator Camila Fuentes(?), ex-singer. Raul: two very rich parties, auction it (Gallo and the senator), via Valentina and Abe. Lucia guesses Mendez works for the senator (game doesn't say).
- Passports arrive, one with Tommy's photo. Raul shows Jason a photo of the son he stays away from: "some people aren't built for it." Lucia: that's an excuse; "you show up... you could be a great dad, J."
- Airport day: swap works; on the runway KG checks; Nate: "no money bro... Juicy, no." Nate shot: "it wasn't you, was it Juicy? cold here, good we're going to Cartagena?" Lucia: "it'll be beautiful, hang in there." Nate doesn't return. KG hijacks the plane with Raul; third crew member steals the cash truck, Karim killed. Jason chases truck; Lucia climbs into the landing gear on take-off. Lucia: KG "had to cancel his flight". Plane lands on water; Raul: "you did good, I owe you."
- Weeks waiting at the pool. Note: Raul's boat at Ocean Beach marina. Boat wrecked, Raul hanging bloody: "Mendez, the cops. I gave them nothing. It was Valentina, she gave me up. Andrea has the drive. Don't leave me like this. Do it." On-screen prompt SHOOT: the player fires. Trap: police outside, "This is Captain Mendez." Escape by water.
- Accounts frozen, citywide alert with names, mugshots online. Abe: cops hit me, I don't know you, "you're already dead." Andrea returns the drive: Valentina sold us all, delete my number. Valentina's bill arrives.
- Left: statue + drive. Lucia: let's just get Tommy, "I'm his mom." Jason repeats Raul: stay away from what we love. Option: fast boat from the Keys to Cuba, 90 miles, no return. Messages from "Mattia": "did you miss me?" "Rooftop bar, bring my things and I'll help." Lucia goes with the statue; Jason holds the drive.
- Not Mattia: Giorgio Gallo. Statue "2,000 years old", "you stole from me". Helicopter to his penthouse; Mattia alive, apologises "nothing personal". Gallo: Italian ships, Albanian product, perfect partnership until the feds seized everything; the drive has the senator's dirt, so he can get his ships back and call off the cops; the Albanians will kill him over 20 tonnes lost; the men at Jason's house were theirs. "You two are my secret weapons." Locked in. "Are we going to be OK?" "Of course. Just keep rolling, you and me."

## Chapter 5: "Top of the World, Ma" (nod to an old gangster-film line; it ends badly for its speaker)
- Prisoners as guests. Coast Guard holds Gallo's 20 tonnes at a base in Vice City. Threat: Albanians kill me, then you, your mum, your aunt, the child. Promise: no new identity needed, "a pardon from the top". His office computer holds something more dangerous than the drive.
- Someone in Gallo's office, feet on desk: "Niko Bellic, my brother." GTA IV hero (Liberty City, 2008), came to America on a cargo ship for a new life; now greying, speaking for the group whose product rides Gallo's ships; original voice actor. "Only one reason I'd be here: you really messed up." $2bn of product lost; cartels working with others. "I haven't killed anybody since 2012." (GTA V 2013 story: Lester mentions an Eastern European guy from Liberty City who went quiet; Rockstar never confirmed, dates fit.) Gun to Gallo's head; "one more chance, Giorgio". Job doubled: recover the 20 tonnes and blow up a rival's shipment at sea.
- Gallo confronts Lucia with Cat (who planted the bag with Mattia): "no choice, the baby, not personal." Lucia: "I looked after you inside." Cat goes into labour; Gallo sends her to hospital, keeps Mattia; Mattia breaks his own leg to get to hospital. Neither returns.
- Double heist: steal Coast Guard sniffer dog, find and blow rival containers, lift 20 tonnes in a net under a military helicopter. Niko: "Time to go home. I was beginning to like it down here."
- Gallo's deal with the senator: drive + all copies for his ships, cops off them. Back to Abe ("I don't know you"): full pardon, every crime, Tommy back; put all your money in the family savings account; if something happens to you, Abe gives it to the boy. Pardon only covers what you write yourself: they sign a full confession (King Fishers, Donny, all robberies, bank, airport).
- Gallo sends Jason alone to deliver the drive; police storm the penthouse. "Someone has to pay." "My pardon?" "Unfortunately that was all a lie." Jason grabbed too. "They're not getting me again": she's in custody again.
- Statewide prosecutor: Mendez's people will kill you within 6-7 hours, your boyfriend won't survive tonight; give me the drive, immunity, new life for you and your child; boyfriend probably goes to prison, "someone has to." SECOND time. Lucia: Jason handed it over, a copy is in the penthouse. Prosecutor can't search it (Gallo's lawyers); "good luck." Lucia later: wouldn't have taken it anyway.
- Jason in Hamlet town police station; Mendez asks about copies, tries to take him, stopped: "stay safe tonight." M7 attacks the station; police outside don't stop them (Jason's guess: M7 on Mendez's payroll). Lucia's transport rammed (accident or plan, unknown); she escapes, reaches the station; together reach Mendez on his knees: "you win, you win." SHOOT prompt again.
- Gallo's computer: Cal's gadget downloads and opens files; fast boat ready in the Keys, 90 miles. Penthouse assault; Gallo escapes by helicopter, never returns. Files secured. "Just you and me. We came out on top. We always do. Top of the world."
- Keys: something's wrong; police radio says suspects in area; "someone tipped off the cops" (never revealed). New plan: Brian's seaplane repair airfield at South Point. Bridge blocked, helicopter above. Jason: you can still use the files, the deal was only for you, "at least one of us can make it out." THIRD time, from Jason himself. Lucia: "We're in this together, all the way." "We're just going to keep on rolling, right?" Choice: SWIM FOR IT / NO SURRENDER.

## Endings
- **1 No Surrender:** "He's going to be OK, J." Kiss, drive at the police, gunfire: "yeah, they're gone." Credits: quiet street, a woman hugging a small child (identity not given). Recall the savings account: if anything happens to you, the money goes to the kid.
- **2 Swim for It (bad):** Jason steps out with a gun, police shoot; they never reach the water.
- **3 Swim for It (other):** Jason's first line differs: "Don't draw your weapon." Hands up, walk to the bridge wall, "3, 2, 1, jump". Lucia jumps; Jason tackled. Credits: Jason in prison, long hair, beard, orange; "Hey, Duval, visitor"; behind the glass Lucia with a small boy who puts his hand on the glass; "Surprise." Unnamed, but if Tommy, the first time Jason ever sees his son.
- Which Swim for It ending you get is decided earlier by how you played: relationship strength, savings account balance, needless violence (game doesn't give exact weights). At least one more ending not yet found (per source; unverified).
- Post-credits (all endings): Cal waits all day for files to open; at night: "No freaking way." Never shown how files reached him or what he sees. "Story complete." Game drops you back before the finale, both alive and together.

## Source's closing themes (ideas only)
- Starts with a lie ("he's my boyfriend"), ends on her line "we're in this together all the way".
- Everyone sells them: Mattia, Valentina, Gallo. Three times Lucia is offered "drop Jason and walk free": Abe, the prosecutor, Jason himself. She never takes it. "Trust" broken by everyone outside, never between them.
- Open questions: who tipped off the cops, what Cal saw, who Mendez works for, what happened to Jason in the army.
