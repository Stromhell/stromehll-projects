Then Raul's tech guy traces the drive. It belongs to Giorgio Gallo, Mattia's boss: a shipping billionaire whose ships kept getting caught with drugs, until the government seized his whole fleet. Remember the broker's line? Tonnes, on cargo ships. This is that man.

And now the bag makes sense. It was a payoff for somebody. The police found out and watched the locker, so Gallo's people sent two strangers to collect it. If anyone got caught, it would be them. Jason's question finally has an answer.

Next, Raul's revenge on KG, who runs a crew of bodybuilders. Lucia goes undercover in their gym for weeks. Same food, same lingo. A crew member, Nate, becomes her friend and nicknames her Juicy.

The target is an airport vault where about ten million dollars in cash arrives every month. KG's crew grabs the money to fly it to Colombia, and Raul's team quietly swaps the bags for fake cash.

On the runway, KG gets suspicious. Nate opens a bag. No money. He's shot in the chaos, and the last thing he says is, it wasn't you, was it, Juicy? Lucia tells him to hang in there. He doesn't come back.

KG hijacks the plane with Raul on board, so Lucia climbs into the landing gear as it takes off. Yes, a moving plane. It ends with a landing on water and Raul saying: I owe you.

Weeks later, a note sends them to Raul's boat. He's tied up and beaten. Mendez, he says. Valentina gave me up. Then he asks Jason to end it, and the game puts the trigger in your hands. Outside, police are waiting. It was a trap.
