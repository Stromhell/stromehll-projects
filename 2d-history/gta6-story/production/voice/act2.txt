Prison is overcrowded, so a counsellor offers Lucia early release with an electronic tag. She'll live at the father's address. The father is Jason Duval, a man with a record of his own.

Jason, meanwhile, is down in the Leonida Keys, the game's version of the Florida Keys, sitting on a boat in the middle of the sea surrounded by packages of drugs.

He works for Brian, an old-school smuggler, and lives at his place rent-free. With him are Cal, a tech guy who sees a conspiracy in everything, and Donny, who flies the seaplane.

Donny skims some product off a delivery for a gang called the King Fishers. They grab him. Jason gets him out, and takes the gang's cash on the way. Now they have the gang's drugs and the gang's money.

That's the start of a turf war. Brian's broker of fifteen years stops answering, and when they find him he laughs: your forty kilos mean nothing. The real players move tonnes, on cargo ships.

Remember that line about cargo ships. Brian tells Jason to shut the broker up. Later Jason just says: it's taken care of. Brian now has no broker and no supply.

Cal asks why Lucia left him. Jason admits she wanted in, he said no, and when he came back from a job she'd gone. She was pregnant. He never knew. He thinks he made her feel he didn't want a family.

Then the full prison phone call. You ghosted me, he says. That's what a break-up is, she says. Then: you're his dad. Are you angry? And Jason says: I think this is the happiest I've ever been.

Lucia walks out with an ankle tag. For nine months she can't go more than half a mile from Jason's place. Tommy is far away with her aunt, up in Liberty State, home of GTA 4's Liberty City.
