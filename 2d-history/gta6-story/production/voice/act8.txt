In custody, the state prosecutor makes Lucia the second offer. Mendez's people will kill you within hours. Give me the drive, and you and your son get immunity and a new life. Your boyfriend probably goes to prison. Someone has to.

But Jason already handed the drive over, and the copy is in Gallo's penthouse, which the prosecutor can't search. Good luck, she says. Lucia says she'd never have taken that deal anyway.

That night a gang attacks the police station where Jason is held, and the officers outside don't lift a finger. Lucia's prison van gets rammed, accident or plan, the game won't say. She breaks Jason out, and they corner Mendez. You win, he says. The game hands you the trigger again.

Last move: Gallo's computer. Cal gives them a gadget that copies and unlocks the files. They storm the penthouse, Gallo escapes by helicopter and vanishes from the story, but they get the files. Just you and me, they say. Top of the world.

Cal has a fast boat waiting in the Keys. Cuba is ninety miles away. But somebody has tipped off the police, and the game never says who. They're trapped on a bridge, police at both ends, a helicopter overhead.
