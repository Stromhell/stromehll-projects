In Vice City, Jason's school friend Dre'Quan says he runs a club. He doesn't. The real owner is Boobie Ike, who owns flats all over the city, lends them one, and then makes Jason sell for him.

Cal flies in to crack the drive, and gives up. Military-grade encryption, he says, who did you get this from? An antiques dealer looks at the statue and says: a bronze Venus, extremely valuable, if it's real. He keeps it, and stops answering his phone.

Brian, retired, gives them one name: Raul. A bank robber big enough to sell a drive like this. Raul tests them, uses them as decoys while his real crew scores elsewhere, and keeps a photo of Lucia mid-theft, just in case.

His plan is clever. Rob the cartel's cash around town until the cartel panics and moves all its money into one place: the vault of its own bank. Then rob the vault.

To get ready, they rob three small banks in one day, wearing old people masks. That night Jason says, let's take this cash, get Tommy and vanish. Lucia says: it's not enough.

The Cartel Heist. Cell towers go down so nobody can call for help. Jason holds the lobby. Down in the vault, Lucia drills in and holds a cable by hand while the vault's designer cracks the code. One slip, and it locks for good.

The door swings open: diamonds and bearer bonds, paper that belongs to whoever is holding it. Raul's money woman, Valentina, moves them into a five-star beach hotel as Mr and Mrs Walker.

Valentina also gets Lucia a meeting with the best-connected lawyer in the city. But she adds a warning. It's a dream, and you pay for it by the night. Hold on to that line.
