The bank won't even open them an account, their credit is that bad. All they get is a phone app with a family savings account: money goes in, and can't come out. They start putting money aside for Tommy. Remember that account.

At Lucia's welcome party, Cat turns up pregnant, with her Italian boyfriend Mattia from Vice City. Mattia says his boss is a very private person, and brags about what he and Cat were carrying just before they came.

Lucia joins Jason on a run, they nearly die, and her parole officer is waiting with thirty missed calls. Your tag says you were a mile out at sea, he says. I can put in a good word on custody. Or a bad one.

Then Brian takes Jason to a peace meeting with the King Fishers at a slaughterhouse. Brian asks where Donny is. And then he looks at the pig pen. Donny isn't coming.

After the shoot-out, Brian quits: I'm out, I'm retiring. In the same hour Jason asked for bigger work, his only income disappears. And they still need money for a custody lawyer.

So when Mattia offers a job, they take it. A bag he and Cat left in a locker at a visitor centre needs moving. Jason asks the right question: why aren't you doing it yourself? Mattia has no answer.

The locker opens, and so does the trap. You're under arrest. Then a friendly detective called Mendez arrives and asks the sergeant to hand him the bag. The sergeant refuses.

Mendez leaves, but his men stay. And they shoot the sergeant's team, then radio in: officer down, suspect had a weapon. Police killed police for that bag, and pinned it on Jason and Lucia.
