**Running device:** A 'TRUST' tally board in the corner that gets a new red X every time someone sells Jason and Lucia out (Mattia, Cat, Valentina, Abe, Gallo...), plus Tommy's savings jar that fills and empties through the video, and a 'WALK AWAY?' card that stamps in each of the three times Lucia is offered freedom without Jason.

**Look:** Crime-drama story recap in Stormi's own 2D house style (Prime / Jensen kit: rig characters on painted 2560x1440 plates). Original character designs only: no game screenshots, no Rockstar logos, no copied character art or box art. Neon Vice City nights, pastel beach days, swampy Keys, a cheap motel, a prison visiting room, a bank vault, an airport runway, a penthouse, a bridge at dusk. Violence shown through light, sound and aftermath only, never gore. British spelling on screen. SFX under VO (sirens, radios, phone buzz, cash counters, vault drill, engines, waves), music at combine.

## Hooks (yt-script hookscore)

- (9) GTA 6's whole story starts with one lie: 'he's my boyfriend'. By the end, almost everyone they trust has sold them out.
