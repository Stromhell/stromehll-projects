GTA 6 Story Explained: Every Chapter, Every Betrayal and All the Endings

DESCRIPTION
SPOILER WARNING: this is the whole story of GTA 6, endings included.

It starts with a lie on a dark road in the Keys: "he's my boyfriend". By the end, almost everyone Jason and Lucia trust has sold them out: a smuggler's broker, a friend, a lawyer, a billionaire. Three times Lucia is offered the same deal: drop him and walk away free. The last person to offer it is Jason.

In this video: Lucia's prison call, the bag that made police kill police, the motel, the Cartel Heist, Abe Gonzalez's offer, the airport vault and the moving plane, Valentina's betrayal, Giorgio Gallo, Niko Bellic's return, the pardon that was a lie, the bridge, and every ending: No Surrender, Swim for It (both versions) and Cal's final scene.

Story details are as reported from the game's release (19 November 2026) and players' playthroughs; where the game leaves something unexplained, we say so.
