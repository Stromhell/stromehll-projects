From here the years fly. 1996: Nike signs a young golfer, Tiger Woods. 2003: it buys bankrupt Converse for about 305 million dollars, and signs an 18-year-old LeBron James.

2015: LeBron gets a lifetime deal, reportedly worth over a billion dollars. 2016: Knight's memoir, Shoe Dog, becomes a bestseller.

2017: Vaporfly shoes help runners break records so often that track officials later write new rules about shoe technology.

2018: an anniversary Just Do It ad with Colin Kaepernick. Some customers burned their shoes. Others bought more, and the ad won major awards.

2019: Eliud Kipchoge, in Nike prototypes, runs a marathon distance in under two hours, in a special event that didn't count as an official record.
