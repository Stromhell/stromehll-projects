From here the years fly. nineteen ninety-six: Nike signs a young golfer, Tiger Woods. two thousand three: it buys bankrupt Converse for about 305 million dollars, and signs an 18-year-old LeBron James.

twenty fifteen: LeBron gets a lifetime deal, reportedly worth over a billion dollars. twenty sixteen: Knight's memoir, Shoe Dog, becomes a bestseller.

twenty seventeen: Vaporfly shoes help runners break records so often that track officials later write new rules about shoe technology.

twenty eighteen: an anniversary Just Do It ad with Colin Kaepernick. Some customers burned their shoes. Others bought more, and the ad won major awards.

twenty nineteen: Eliud Kipchoge, in Nike prototypes, runs a marathon distance in under two hours, in a special event that didn't count as an official record.
