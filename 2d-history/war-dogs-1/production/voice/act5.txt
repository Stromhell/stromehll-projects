Efraim promises David a quarter of the profit on everything he finds. David starts calling state-owned arms suppliers across the old Soviet bloc.

Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Romania, Hungary. All over Eastern Europe, Cold War ammunition is sitting in old depots, gathering dust.

The richest pile is in Albania. Its old dictator turned the country into a giant arsenal. The UN estimated over 100,000 tons of weapons and munitions were stored there.

Through Thomet's contacts, AEY strikes a deal with Albania's state arms exporter. Now they just have to win the contract.
