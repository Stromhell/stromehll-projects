Efraim Diveroli grows up in a strict Orthodox Jewish family in Miami. Guns run in the family: an uncle sells weapons to police departments.

He's a wild kid. He drinks, skips school and gets expelled at 14. His parents send him to Los Angeles to live with that uncle.

He starts in the warehouse, stacking boxes. Then September 11th, 2001. America goes to war in Afghanistan, then Iraq, and the arms business booms.

At 16 he's selling guns over the phone to police departments. Nobody on the line knows he's a teenager.

He fights with his uncle over money he says he's owed, about 75,000 dollars. So he flies home to Miami, taking a pile of client files with him.
