Miami, 2007. A man in his early twenties eats sea bass in a fancy restaurant. He and his best friend have just won a huge contract from the US Army.

Their cargo planes are flying grenades, rockets and bullets from Eastern Europe to Afghanistan. Then his phone rings. A plane full of ammunition has been seized in Kyrgyzstan.

He's a massage therapist with a chemistry degree. His partner is barely 21. And they're now supplying the Afghan army for the Pentagon.
