2006, Paris. One of the world's biggest arms fairs. David brings a metal briefcase, because he thinks that's what an arms dealer carries.

There they meet Heinrich Thomet, a Swiss arms broker with contacts all over the world, and a name on a US State Department watch list.

Meanwhile America is fighting two wars, and outsourcing a lot of the work to private companies. Now it needs to arm the Afghan army against the Taliban.

The Army posts a giant order: a hundred million rounds for AK-47 rifles, about a million grenades, rockets and more. Worth hundreds of millions of dollars.
