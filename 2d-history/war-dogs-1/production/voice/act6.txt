The whole contract is worth almost 300 million dollars. Efraim adds a margin of about nine percent: roughly 30 million in profit. David works out his share at around eight million.

With minutes to the deadline, they race to the post office to send the bid. They make it with seconds to spare.

Months later, the phone rings. Good news and bad news. They've won. But the first order is just 600,000 dollars.

A week and a half later, a 20 million dollar order arrives. And they learn they'd bid 52 million dollars lower than the next company. Efraim is furious he left money on the table.
