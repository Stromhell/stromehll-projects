Then, in two thousand nine, while most of Wall Street was still on its knees, BlackRock made the biggest bet in its history.

It bought Barclays Global Investors from the British bank Barclays for about thirteen and a half billion dollars.

Inside that deal was a business called eye-Shares, the biggest maker of exchange-traded funds, or E T Fs, in the world.

Overnight, BlackRock went from managing about one point three trillion dollars to more than three trillion.

To see why that mattered so much, you need to understand what an E T F actually is.
