Description:
BlackRock owns about 8% of Nvidia, more than Jensen Huang does, and it has never designed a chip. It has no factory and no product you can hold, yet by mid-2026 it managed $15.3 trillion of other people's money. Here is how a company that makes nothing ended up owning a piece of everything.

It starts in the 1980s with Larry Fink, a young bond trader at First Boston who helped turn home loans into bonds, then lost about $100 million in 1986 when rates fell and homeowners paid back early. He left, co-founded BlackRock inside Blackstone in 1988, and built Aladdin, a risk machine that asks "what if?" across every holding. In 2008 Washington hired BlackRock to value the wreckage. In 2009 it bought Barclays Global Investors and iShares for about $13.5 billion, right as index funds took over investing. That brought the Big Three, shareholder votes, the climate letters, the backlash, and the Texas coal lawsuit.
