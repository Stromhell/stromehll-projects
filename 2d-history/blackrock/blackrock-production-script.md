## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Added (not in transcript): 2020 Fed corporate bond programme run by BlackRock; Fink's 2020 climate letter; Florida pulling ~$2B in Dec 2022; BlackRock voting choice; leaving Net Zero Asset Managers in Jan 2025.
- Auto-caption name errors fixed: Larry Fink (not Frink/Fin/Fing/Fank), BlackRock, Aladdin, iShares, ChatGPT reference dropped.
- AUM: $15.3 trillion as of 30 June 2026 (BlackRock Q2 2026 results). Transcript said 'over $15 trillion'.
- Ownership: BlackRock holds about 1.9 billion Nvidia shares (~7.8%); Jensen Huang about 3.5%. Script says 'about eight percent' and 'about three and a half percent'. Apple/Microsoft given as 'roughly seven to eight percent' because the exact figures move every quarter.
- 'Steve Jobs owns less than 1% of Apple' dropped: Jobs died in 2011.
- Mortgage example payment: $200,000 at 9% over 30 years is $1,609 a month (transcript had '$1,69').
- Tranche example labelled 'simplified': real deals have servicing fees and costs, so the $2.3M gap is not pure profit.
- Fink 'got fired': he left First Boston in 1988 after the 1986 loss of about $100M. Script says 'he left'.
- Founding: 1988, Fink plus seven partners, inside Blackstone, as Blackstone Financial Management; renamed BlackRock in 1992.
- 2008: the Fed and Treasury hired BlackRock to value and manage Bear Stearns and AIG assets. The jump from ~$1.3T to $3T+ came from buying Barclays Global Investors (with iShares) in 2009 for ~$13.5B, not from the rescue work, and not in 2008 as the transcript said.
- '90% of fund managers fail to beat the S&P 500 over 15 years': SPIVA puts it near 9 in 10 large-cap US funds. Kept as 'around nine in ten'.
- Index ETF fee: BlackRock's IVV charges 0.03%. Active fee given as 'around 1% or more' (transcript mixed 1.5% and 2%).
- 'Big Three own 72% of equity ETF dollars' couldn't be verified; dropped. '438 of 500 companies' is the Fichtner, Heemskerk & Garcia-Bernardo study (University of Amsterdam, published 2017 in Business and Politics), not 'Cambridge researchers': ~88% of S&P 500 firms.
- Texas coal case: filed late November 2024 by Texas and 10 other states (later 13 states). Vanguard settled in February 2026 for $29.5M with no admission of wrongdoing; BlackRock and State Street are still defending. The case is presented as allegations, with the firms' defense included.
- Exact stake percentages in coal and power firms left out: they come from the complaint and change often.
- 'Strait of Hormuz closure cost bankers trillions' dropped: not tied to this story and not verifiable.
