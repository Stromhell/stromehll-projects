# Why Putin Is So Hard to Kill

Working title: **The Armoured Car, the Ghost Train and the Fake Offices: How Putin Stays Alive**

8 parts (cold open + 7 acts), 65 shots, 1214 words, about 8:33 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A red 'WHERE IS PUTIN?' pin that keeps jumping between five residences on a map of Russia. Each act adds one more layer of protection around the pin (car shell, four planes, a train line, three identical offices), and the pin never settles in one place.

**Look:** Calm, serious investigative documentary in the house 2D style, Alium character style (big-eyed, rounded cutouts as in BlackRock). Painted plates: grey Moscow highways in sleet, a snowy forest residence, a jet cabin at night, a dark green train crossing birch forest, a wood-panelled office lit by one desk lamp. Cold blues and greys with one warm accent (the red pin). SFX under the VO (engine hum, rotor wash, train clatter, clock ticks); music only at combine.

## Hooks (yt-script hookscore)

- (55) Every time Putin flies, four identical planes take off. Only one has him on board. Here's why you'll never know which.
- (44) What if the man you see on Russian TV isn't where they say he is? Journalists checked the door handles.

## Fact-check fixes against the transcript

- Aurus Senat specs vary by source: ~6.5 t armoured, 4.4 L twin-turbo V8 hybrid; top speed given as 160-170 km/h; script hedges.
- Il-96-300PU: four-engine Soviet-era design (first flight 1988). 'Four identical planes' is a media report; stated as 'said to be'.
- Dossier Center (Mikhail Khodorkovsky-funded) published the train leak in 2023; company named in reports as Tver-based / 'Tsirkon Servis' contractor. Script says 'a Russian company that builds custom railway carriages'.
- Kremlin denied Putin used the spa carriage; stated on screen.
- Private stations: Bocharov Ruchey (Sochi), Novo-Ogaryovo, Valdai, attributed to Rozhdestvensky.
- Office copies: reported by Proekt (2023) comparing footage; script says 'journalists' and 'reportedly'.
- Body doubles: claim by Kyrylo Budanov (HUR chief until 2025); Kremlin denies; no public proof stated.
- Gleb Karakulov: FSO officer who defected in 2023 and spoke to the Dossier Center; described decoy flights, not true doubles.
- Waste-collection claim: Paris Match / French reporting (2022), stated as 'reports claim'.
- Putin served in the KGB in Dresden 1985-1990.
