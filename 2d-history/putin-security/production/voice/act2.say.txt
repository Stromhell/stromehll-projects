This is his car, a Russian-built Aurus Senat limousine.

Inside it is all leather, soft seats and a small bar. Outside, it is built like a vault.

Picture an ambush on the road into Moscow. Attackers open fire.

The armour is designed to stop heavy rounds, reportedly even armour-piercing ones.

A grenade lands under the car. It rolls on through the smoke.

The fuel tank is sealed against blasts, and the tyres are built to keep running even after they are shot out.

It weighs around six and a half tonnes, but a twin-turbo V8 with a hybrid system can still push it to around 160 or 170 kilometres an hour, depending on the source.

The cabin can seal itself and filter the air, so a gas attack would struggle to reach the passenger. There is an emergency exit too.

For short trips, the car is his shield. For long ones, he takes to the air.
