Since the full-scale invasion of Ukraine, Putin has held many meetings by video, from what is presented as his office at Novo-Ogaryovo.

Russians know that room by sight: the desk, the wood panels, the flags.

But journalists comparing the footage spotted something odd. The door handles sit at different heights.

The thermostat moves around. The wall panels line up differently. The vents and TV stands do not match.

The likely answer: near-copies of the office have been built at other residences, reportedly including Valdai and Sochi.

Rozhdestvensky's reading is that the copies let him record a statement that appears to come from Novo-Ogaryovo while he is somewhere else entirely.

If almost nobody knows where he is, it is very hard to target him.

Reports also say that since the war began he has stayed away from his Sochi summer house out of fear of Ukrainian drones.
