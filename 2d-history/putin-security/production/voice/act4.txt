If this train rolled past your window, you probably would not look twice. Grey carriages, red stripes, like thousands of others in Russia.

Look closer and a few details stand out: several diesel engines at the front, and a small dome on the roof for special communications.

The Kremlin has shown pictures of a meeting room on board. For years, nobody outside knew what the rest looked like.

Then the Dossier Center published documents it says were leaked from inside a Russian company that builds custom railway carriages.

According to those files, the carriages are armoured and so heavy that each one rides on six axles instead of the usual four.

There is a sleeping car, an office car and a restaurant car.

The leaks also describe a gym, a soundproofed beauty salon with a massage table, and a tiled spa with a Turkish steam bath. The Kremlin denies Putin has ever used carriages like these.

Rozhdestvensky says the train does not appear on public timetables. Someone inside Russian Railways could see it, but an ordinary passenger never would.

It runs straight through stations without stopping.

And according to the investigation, there are private stations built for him near his homes: at Bocharov Ruchey in Sochi, at Novo-Ogaryovo outside Moscow, and at Valdai, where he spends much of the year.

So on any given day, it is hard to say which home he is actually in.
