Working title: **The Woman Who Bet She Could Get Jensen Huang (And Why Nobody Warned Him)**

12 parts (cold open + 11 acts), 70 shots, 2134 words, about 14:17 at 160 wpm.

**Running device:** A 'reported by' source strip (NYT / WSJ / FT tags) that stamps onto every claim, plus a redaction bar that slides off a name when the file is unmasked.

**Look:** News-investigation caper in the house 2D style, Gumball/t-shirt character style (same as Kids Who Hacked). Painted plates: a Taipei bar at night, a Computex hall, a Singapore office, a Malaysian data centre, a Kentucky hillside school, a Denny's booth, Washington at night, a Beijing banquet hall, a newsroom. Real people stylised, no likeness, no real logos; every claim attributed on screen to the outlet that reported it. Alice Huang never charged: said on screen. No private-life imagery beyond what was reported (no romance scenes). SFX under VO (camera shutters, keyboard, phone buzz, stamps, crowd), music at combine.
