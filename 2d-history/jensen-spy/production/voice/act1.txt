This is Alice Huang. Her Chinese name is Huang Le. She's a Chinese citizen, and despite the name, she's no relation of his.

According to the Times, she starts out as a reporter for Chinese state television. Then a private banker at big Chinese banks. Then an executive at a Shanghai fund.

In 2023 she takes over a small Singapore company, and it gets a new name: Megaspeed. It began as the Singapore arm of a Chinese gaming company with state-backed investors.

Megaspeed buys Nvidia's best chips, puts them in data centres in Malaysia and Indonesia, and rents out the computing power. One customer, the Financial Times reports, is Alibaba. That way Alibaba uses chips it can't buy in China.

According to the Times, about two billion dollars of Nvidia's most advanced chips go through Megaspeed's Malaysian arm. Bloomberg calls it the single largest buyer of Nvidia chips in Southeast Asia.

When the Times visits its Singapore office on a weekday, there are two employees. When American inspectors visit its data centre in Malaysia, they find equipment still in its original boxes. Where its money comes from is unknown.
