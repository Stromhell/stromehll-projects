Taipei, June 2024, just after midnight. It's Computex week, and the whole AI industry is in town. At a bar, a table of tech executives is winding down.

One of them is a Chinese businesswoman named Alice Huang. She bets them she can get Jensen Huang, the CEO of Nvidia, to come.

This week he can't walk down a street in Taipei without a crowd. Not long after, he walks in. He sits down and drinks whiskey with them. Someone posts a photo on LinkedIn.

Two years later, US intelligence has concluded she was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security. And according to the New York Times, her assignment was him.

Last Sunday, Jensen Huang wrote to every one of his employees: 'I was unfaithful to my wife.' And according to the Times, President Trump was briefed on her about a year before any of it came out. He did nothing with it.

This is how China's spy service got next to the most important man in the AI race. And why nobody warned him.

This film is built on reporting by the New York Times, the Wall Street Journal and the Financial Times.
