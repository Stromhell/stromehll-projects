Description:
A Denny's dishwasher now runs the first company in history worth $5 trillion. Here is how Jensen Huang built Nvidia: the wrong chip, the Sega favour, 30 days of cash, and the CUDA bet Wall Street hated.

In 1993 three engineers met in a Denny's booth in San Jose with about $40,000 and an idea for a graphics chip. Their first product, the NV1, bet on curved surfaces just as Microsoft's DirectX standardised triangles. Nvidia lost its only big customer, asked Sega to pay it anyway, and shipped the RIVA 128 with roughly a month of payroll left. Then came the GeForce, the GPU, CUDA, AlexNet, the failed $40 billion Arm deal, the US chip bans, ChatGPT and, on 29 October 2025, a $5 trillion valuation.
