Jensen Huang was born in Taiwan in nineteen sixty-three. His family later moved to Thailand.

When he was nine, his parents sent him and his older brother to America, to stay with an uncle and get a good education.

The uncle enrolled them at a school in rural Kentucky that he thought was a prestigious boarding school.

It was the Oneida Baptist Institute, a strict school that also took in troubled teenagers.

Jensen was the youngest boy in the dorm. His daily job was cleaning the bathrooms.

He has said he didn't complain, and that it taught him to just get on with the work in front of him.

His parents eventually joined them in Oregon. As a teenager he worked at Denny's, washing dishes and waiting tables.

He studied electrical engineering at Oregon State, then designed chips at AMD and LSI Logic, and took a master's degree at Stanford at night.
