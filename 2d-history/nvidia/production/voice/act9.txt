Training those models takes vast data centres full of GPUs. Nvidia's chips, and its CUDA software, were the standard.

Seventeen years of CUDA meant that most AI code was already written for Nvidia. Switching to a rival chip wasn't just buying new hardware; it meant rewriting software.

Nvidia's value climbed in leaps. One trillion dollars in 2023. Three trillion in 2024. Four trillion in July 2025.

On the twenty-ninth of October 2025, Nvidia became the first company ever worth five trillion dollars.
