Nvidia designs its chips, but doesn't make them. Most are manufactured by T S M C in Taiwan.

In twenty twenty, Jensen tried to buy Arm, the British company whose chip designs sit inside almost every smartphone, for forty billion dollars.

The rest of the industry objected. Regulators in America, Britain and Europe opened investigations, and the US Federal Trade Commission sued to block it.

In twenty twenty-two, Nvidia gave up on the deal.

That same year, the US government banned Nvidia from selling its top AI chips, the A one hundred and H one hundred, to China.

Nvidia designed slower versions to fit inside the rules, like the H eight hundred. In twenty twenty-three, Washington tightened the rules and those were blocked too.

Nvidia was no longer just a chip company. It had become part of a technology contest between the world's two biggest powers.
