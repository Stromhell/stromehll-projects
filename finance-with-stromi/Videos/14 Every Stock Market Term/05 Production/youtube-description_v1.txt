Milo's friend Benny talks about going long on a blue chip, getting shaken out and catching a short squeeze. Milo nods along and understands none of it. So he spends one year in Marketland with Lexi, a tiny pocket dictionary, and learns the whole language of the stock market, month by month.

In this animated explainer you'll learn about 75 stock market terms in plain English: stocks, shares, IPOs and tickers; brokers, assets and liabilities; bid, ask and the spread; market, limit and stop-loss orders; volume and liquidity; bull and bear markets and volatility; market cap, blue chips and penny stocks; balance sheets, EPS, the P/E ratio, book value, dividends, yield and ROI; portfolios, bonds, commodities, index funds, ETFs, mutual funds, hedge funds and IRAs; unicorns, whales, bubbles, panic selling, circuit breakers, black swans and dead cat bounces; going long, short selling, short squeezes, leverage, options and futures; pump and dumps and insider trading; day trading, swing trading, technical vs fundamental analysis and averaging down; and the two boring superpowers, dollar cost averaging and compound interest.
