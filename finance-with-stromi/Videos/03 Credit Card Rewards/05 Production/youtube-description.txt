You paid your credit card in full. You never paid a cent of interest. So who paid for your rewards? In 2024 Americans earned $47.5 billion in credit card rewards, and the Federal Reserve found the "swipe" side of the card business barely breaks even. Here's how credit cards really make money, and the dark side of that 2% cashback.

In this animated explainer we follow Jo at the grocery store and look at her card the way her bank does: the interchange fee the store pays, the toll the card network takes, why prices quietly go up for everyone (even people paying cash), the two businesses hiding inside one card, revolvers vs transactors, $160 billion in interest at a 22% rate, the $1,200 car repair that flips a "free" card, and what happened when Europe and Australia capped the fees. No jargon, just cartoons and plain English.
