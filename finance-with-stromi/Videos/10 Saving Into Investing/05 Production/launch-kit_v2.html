Description 

Wren saved money every month for eleven years. Last month her $10,000 earned eight cents. Her money wasn't lazy. It was stuck in a waiting room, and so is a lot of America's.

In this animated explainer, Wren, Dime, Grandpa Abe, Rico and the rest of the cast walk the step-by-step path from saving to investing: why 0.01% savings lose to 3.7% prices, how compounding turns the same $10,000 into $12,202 or $67,275, why losses feel twice as strong as gains, how to build a 3-6 month cushion in high-yield savings, the two-pocket rule, starting with $50 on autopilot, why index funds beat about 9 in 10 professional pickers, what Warren Buffett told his own family to buy, and why a crash is when steady investors buy shares on sale.
