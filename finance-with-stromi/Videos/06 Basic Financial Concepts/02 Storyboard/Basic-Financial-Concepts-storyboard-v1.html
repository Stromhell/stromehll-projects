B27Corkboard7:37

Corkboard. A card reads: TWO QUARTERS OF FALLING GDP? It gets a red stamp: RULE OF THUMB. A second card: NBER DECIDES. Two dates pinned: 2008-09, 2020 (2 MONTHS).

Narrator You may have heard a recession is two quarters in a row of the economy shrinking. That's a handy rule of thumb, but it isn't the official one. In the US, a panel at the National Bureau of Economic Research makes the call, by looking for a big drop in activity that spreads across the whole economy, and lasts more than a few months. The Great Recession ran from late two thousand seven to mid two thousand nine. The Covid recession in twenty twenty lasted just two months, the shortest on record.

B28Party club8:12

Morning. Mabel opens the blinds, Pip mops, the DJ deck starts again softly. Tag: RECOVERY.

Narrator And every recession so far has ended. Central banks cut interest rates, governments send out help, and slowly people start spending again. The party restarts. That's why Nana's first rule of recessions is boring: have a few months of bills saved before the lights flicker, not after.

Blazing desert, heat shimmer. Wendell crawls in, dusty, clutching a velvet bag of diamonds. Dot runs a lemonade-style stand selling WATER.

Narrator Water keeps you alive. Diamonds don't. So why is a diamond worth thousands and a glass of water almost nothing? People have been arguing about that for more than two hundred and fifty years. B44Desert water stand (new)12:54

Dot's stand sign changes: WATER 2 DIAMONDS. A rain cloud drifts over. Price drops to 1 PEBBLE.

Narrator This is the most useful idea in the whole list. You get paid in proportion to the value you provide and how hard you are to replace. It's what problem can I solve that few other people can.
