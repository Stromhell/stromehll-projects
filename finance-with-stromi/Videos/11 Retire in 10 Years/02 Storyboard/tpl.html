The Ten-Year Orchard

An original Stromi script for Finance video 11. Your transcript was research only: story, cast, skits and every line are new, and all the YouTube-channel and Telegram promotion is gone. Nadia, a 32-year-old diner line cook, decides at 4 a.m. she wants out in ten years. A 1930s game show, THE NUMBER, runs the guess-the-number hook and the reveal. Her money is one apple tree in an orchard; three crows (Inflation, Taxes, Fees) peck the fruit; three kinds of soil are the scenarios; three levers (spend, time, earn) shrink the bill; a payday sprinkler is automation. Skit lines in red sit between narration like Two Thursdays and Frozen Market. Circular ending: ten years later a new cook asks Nadia the same question. Every number recomputed: the honest answer for a $3,000/month life is about $5,200/month, not the $3,000-4,000 the source claimed. Kept distinct from video 10 (no repeat of cushion, index-fund or crash steps; R41 points back to it).
