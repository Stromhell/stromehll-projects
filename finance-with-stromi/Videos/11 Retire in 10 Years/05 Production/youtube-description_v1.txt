Nadia is a 32-year-old line cook who wants out of the diner in ten years, not at sixty-five. So we worked out her number to the dollar. How much do you actually need to invest every month to retire in just ten years? The answer surprised the contestants on THE NUMBER, and it might surprise you.

In this animated explainer, Nadia, her brother Oscar, Barb from the orchard next door and three very greedy crows walk through the whole math: what one month of "retired" really costs, the 25x rule and where the 4% rule came from (Bengen, 1994), why a long early retirement needs closer to 28-30x, why the first years of investing feel like nothing, how inflation, taxes and fees eat your returns, real vs advertised returns, how $1,500 a month grows in safe, mixed and stock-heavy portfolios, the exact monthly number, and the three levers that shrink it: spend less, give it more time, earn more.
