Description 

In 48 hours, America's email, America's skies and America's payroll all broke. Microsoft's Outlook went down across 70+ countries, the FAA stopped every departure in the country for over four hours, and Workday's HR and payroll system failed for around 11,500 businesses. No hackers. So what happened, what did the stock market do, and why should you care?

In this animated explainer we follow Nina (a small accounting office with no email), Jimmy (a best man stuck at the gate with the rings) and Carla (a shift lead whose paycheck runs through one app). We look at the 20-year-old routers behind the FAA ground stop, the $50 billion Congress is being asked for, how airline stocks and Microsoft reacted, what CrowdStrike 2024 taught investors, and the "one well, whole village" problem hiding in your index fund. Plus four questions to ask about your own money.
