## FAA ground stop (Fri 13 Nov 2026)
- 3:54 PM ET to 8:15 PM ET; telecom outage at air route traffic control centres; "extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety". Saturday: 4,100+ cancelled, ~3,200 delayed by CBS's count (FlightAware) https://www.cbsnews.com/news/faa-ground-stop-canceled-flights-travelers-what-we-know/ . - Duffy: router during copper-to-fiber switch "started flooding other routers", "over 1,000 connection attempts every four to five seconds"; blamed contractor L3Harris; CBS sources: Cisco 600-series routers made 2001-2006; FAA disabled the function on ~60 routers; Duffy asks Congress for $50bn emergency ATC funding; no cyberattack; White House: no indication of "nefarious act".
