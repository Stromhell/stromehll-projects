Ledger A

An original Stromi script for Finance video 15, "Rockefeller: The First Confirmed Billionaire (And How He Did It)" (your exact title). The transcript was research only: every line, skit and scene is new, and its host name, sponsor read and course plug are gone. The spine is Ledger A, the little red account book Rockefeller kept from his first job at 50 cents a day. Cold open in 1916: an old man on a porch hands a dime to a girl who asks "how much is a billion?". Ten chapters from Devil Bill to the 1911 break-up that made him richer, then Your Wallet. Skit lines in red sit between narration like Two Thursdays and Frozen Market. Circular ending: back on the porch, he hands over the dime and writes "10 cents" in his ledger. Est. ~15:45 with cards.

Facts checked (20 Nov 2026)

  • Born 8 Jul 1839, first job 26 Sep 1855 (~50c a day), Ledger A
  • $800 saved + $1,000 from his father at 10%; auction Feb 1865 at $72,500
  • Standard Oil 10 Jan 1870; 22 of 26 Cleveland rivals bought in ~6 weeks (1872); ~90% of US refining by ~1880; Trust 1882
  • 39 drops of solder (his own account); kerosene ~26c to ~8c a gallon
  • Tarbell series 1902-04; father's double life exposed 1908 by the New York World (the transcript wrongly credits Tarbell)
  • Break-up 15 May 1911 into 34 companies; he kept ~25% of each
  • 1916 papers said $1 billion; biographer Chernow puts the peak near $900M (1913); about 2% of US GDP either way
  • Dropped: the open-window threat scene and the garbled "steal more money" quote
