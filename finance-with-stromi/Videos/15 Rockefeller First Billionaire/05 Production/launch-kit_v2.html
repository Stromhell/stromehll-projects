Description 

In 1916 a newspaper called John D. Rockefeller the world's first billionaire. He started on 50 cents a day with a little red account book called Ledger A. This is how he did it: the con-man father, the auction, the secret railroad deal, the 39th drop of solder, the journalist who took him on, and the break-up that made him even richer.

In this animated story you'll learn how Rockefeller saved and borrowed his first $1,800, why he bet everything on oil, how Standard Oil used vertical integration, railroad rebates and buyouts to control about 90% of US refining, what the Standard Oil Trust was, how Ida Tarbell's reporting led to the 1911 Supreme Court break-up into 34 companies, why that break-up made him even richer, and how he gave much of it away.
