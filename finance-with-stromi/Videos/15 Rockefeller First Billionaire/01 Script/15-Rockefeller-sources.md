# Finance 15 Rockefeller: sources and fact notes (v1, 20 Nov 2026)

Main references: Ron Chernow, *Titan: The Life of John D. Rockefeller, Sr.* (1998); Ida M. Tarbell, *The History of the Standard Oil Company* (1904); Rockefeller Archive Center (rockarch.org) biography pages; Standard Oil Co. of New Jersey v. United States, 221 U.S. 1 (1911); Britannica entries for Rockefeller, Standard Oil, South Improvement Company, Tidewater Pipeline. Maths in calc15.py.
