## Behavioural research
- Mental accounting: Thaler, "Mental Accounting and Consumer Choice", Marketing Science 1985. KEPT. Thaler won the 2017 Nobel (economic sciences) for behavioural economics contributions incl. mental accounting. KEPT (nobelprize.org).
- House money effect: Thaler & Johnson, "Gambling with the House Money and Trying to Break Even", Management Science 1990. KEPT.
- Willpower "battery" (ego depletion): large 23-lab registered replication (Hagger et al., 2016) found an effect near zero; later multisite work also weak. Script says "big repeat studies came back much weaker than the headlines". KEPT. Matches transcript's own caveat but worded fresh.
