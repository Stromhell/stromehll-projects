In this animated explainer we visit Hattie's HEAD OFFICE, where five departments run her money: Mr. Pigeonhole and mental accounting, Opal's switchboard and why automation beats willpower, the Ghost of What-Else and opportunity cost, cousin Ace on the high wire (risk tolerance vs risk capacity), and the House Rules and Sunday Meeting that turn good intentions into a money system that works for you.
