It all came down to leverage. That’s when borrowed cash is used to increase the potential return of an investment. It’s like a multiplier for your buying power. Instead of using just your own money to buy an asset, you put up a small deposit, a margin, and then borrow extra funds from a broker. If you trade with a 10:1 leverage, the $1,000 controls a $10,000 position. But there’s a catch. The profits and losses are based on the full position, not the deposit, in this case $10,000 The upside can be massive. If an asset increases by just 5% your $10,000 position gains $500. If the same asset falls by 5%, you lose $500, wiping out 505% of your investment. And if the price moves by 10%, your entire deposit is gone.
