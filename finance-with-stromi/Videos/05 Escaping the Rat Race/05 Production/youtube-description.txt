Mike Tyson earned an estimated $300 million in the ring. In 2003 he filed for bankruptcy owing about $23 million. If the most famous fighter on Earth can go broke, the problem was never how much money comes in. So what is money, really, why do so many six-figure earners live paycheck to paycheck, and what did school never teach us about it?

In this animated explainer we follow Jimmy (29, earns $105k, has $11.40 in checking) and Carla (52, earns $48k, has six months of bills saved). We look at what money actually is, why it works like a bathtub, the small leaks that drain it, the real rat race (and the gurus selling a way out), the "ostrich effect" that stops us checking our balance, and a simple five-step plan school skipped. Plus four questions to ask about your own money.
