There are more homes for sale in America than buyers to buy them. So why are house prices still so high? Here's how the US housing market froze, and what it means for your wallet.

In this animated explainer we follow three people stuck in the same market: Linda, who can't sell because she'd lose her 2.9% mortgage; Danielle, a nurse who owes more than her house is worth; and Marcus, a renter priced out at 38. We break down the "golden handcuffs" of low mortgage rates, why builders keep cutting deals while owners won't, the price tag trick sellers play, whether this is 2008 all over again, and what could finally thaw the ice. No jargon, just cartoons and plain English.
