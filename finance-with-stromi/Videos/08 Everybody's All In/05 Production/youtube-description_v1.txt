Every time the stock market hits a record, the bell over Rosa's Diner rings. Lately it rings a lot. American households now hold a record share of their wealth in stocks, about a third, higher than at the 2000 or 2021 peaks. So when the market sneezes, it isn't just traders who catch a cold. It's the diner, the town budget, and the retirement next door.

In this animated explainer, Rosa, Hank, Jules and the rest of Millbrook show why this market is different: how 401(k)s and target-date funds put everybody in without anyone deciding to, how rising stocks now drive spending (and how a 25% drop could undo it), why fund managers can't afford to sit out, how margin debt hit $1.5 trillion, why leveraged ETFs turn a 1% move into billions of forced selling, and what one simple plan looks like for your own wallet.
