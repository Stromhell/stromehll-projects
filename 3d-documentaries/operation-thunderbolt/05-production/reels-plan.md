# Operation Thunderbolt: 15 reels plan

Each reel is cut from the finished acts (vertical 9:16, centre crop; subtitles and overlays re-placed for vertical).
Every reel gets: a new spoken first line in Pushpam's voice (the long video's line assumes context the viewer doesn't have),
2-second on-screen text that uses different words, the Stormi logo top-right, and the "watch the full video on YouTube" end card (reel_brand.sh).
Thumbnails are made in-house (no ElevenLabs): a frame from the render, graded hard, one big 2–4 word line in Anton, one accent colour, a single focal subject.

Hook lines were checked with hookscore. Scores sit in the 40s–50s; the tool only flags weak lines, it can't promise a hit.

## Titles and descriptions

Descriptions open with the hook in one line, give one fact, then point to the full video. Every description ends with the music credit line (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0) when the reel uses act music.

1. **Title:** 4 Planes. 4,000 km. Lights Off. 🇮🇱✈️ #history
   **Description:** In July 1976, four Israeli Hercules flew into Uganda with every light switched off. 106 hostages were waiting. The full story is on our channel. #EntebbeRaid #OperationThunderbolt #history
2. **Title:** How 4 Men Hijacked a Jet in 3 Minutes (1976)
   **Description:** A broken metal detector in Athens let four hijackers board Air France 139 with pistols and grenades. Three minutes later the plane was theirs. Full documentary on the channel. #AirFrance139 #Entebbe #truestory
3. **Title:** She Lied to the Hijackers, and It Worked
   **Description:** On the ground in Libya, one passenger said she was losing her baby. They let her go. She wasn't. #Entebbe1976 #history #shorts
4. **Title:** The Dictator Was Already Waiting for the Hijacked Plane
   **Description:** Flight 139 landed at Entebbe at 3:15 a.m. with no argument from the tower. Did Idi Amin know it was coming? #IdiAmin #Entebbe #history
5. **Title:** Then They Started Reading the Passports…
   **Description:** At Entebbe, the hijackers split the Israeli and Jewish hostages from everyone else. For the older hostages it brought back Europe, thirty years earlier. #Entebbe #history #shorts
6. **Title:** The Captain Who Refused to Be Freed
   **Description:** The hijackers offered to release the Air France crew. Captain Michel Bacos said no: his passengers were still there. #AirFrance139 #hero #history
7. **Title:** The Mistake That Doomed the Hijackers
   **Description:** Freeing 100+ hostages looked like mercy. In Paris, Israeli agents asked every one of them about the terminal. #Entebbe #Mossad #history
8. **Title:** Israel's First Rescue Plan Had a Crocodile Problem 🐊
   **Description:** Plan one: cross Lake Victoria by boat. Nobody had written down the crocodiles. All three early plans got in, and none got out. #Entebbe #militaryhistory
9. **Title:** They Had the Blueprints of the Enemy Airport
   **Description:** An Israeli company had built Entebbe's old terminal. Its engineers still had every plan, so the commandos built a copy and rehearsed. #Entebbe #history
10. **Title:** Flying Below 3 Countries' Radar
    **Description:** Four Hercules flew down the Red Sea just above the waves, under Egyptian, Sudanese and Saudi radar, all the way to Uganda. #C130 #Entebbe #aviation
11. **Title:** The Fake Mercedes That Almost Worked
    **Description:** Israel copied Idi Amin's black Mercedes to drive right up to the terminal. They didn't know he had just bought a white one. #Entebbe #history
12. **Title:** The Commander Was the First to Fall
    **Description:** Yoni Netanyahu led the assault team at Entebbe. He was hit outside the terminal before the raid had really begun. #Yonatan #Entebbe #history
13. **Title:** The Raid Took 30 Minutes
    **Description:** 102 hostages rescued, 11 MiGs destroyed, about 90 minutes on the ground and roughly 30 of fighting. #Entebbe #OperationThunderbolt
14. **Title:** The Hostage Who Never Came Home
    **Description:** Dora Bloch, 75, was taken to a Kampala hospital before the raid. Years later, British records and a Ugandan inquiry said she was killed on Amin's orders. #Entebbe #history
15. **Title:** His Brother Became Prime Minister
    **Description:** The raid was renamed Operation Yonatan after the one commando who died. His younger brother Benjamin later became Israel's prime minister. #Netanyahu #Entebbe #history
