## What changed from the transcript

- **Language:** translated from Hindi into English narration, rewritten in short plain sentences.
- **Sponsor dropped:** both Acko term insurance reads (the "uncertainty" segue in the middle and the reminder at the end).
- **News clip lines dropped:** the English news-reader quotes in the transcript are replaced by narration and an animated newspaper montage.
- **Facts fixed:** landing at Entebbe was the early hours of 28 June, not 29 June; the captain is Michel Bacos; the Israeli friend of Amin is Baruch Bar-Lev; the general is Dan Shomron; Benjamin Netanyahu is prime minister, not president; the planes were on the ground about 90 minutes (the 30-minute assault is kept); the cabinet's final yes came after the planes had already taken off, and they landed at 23:00 Entebbe time.
- **Added from sources:** the pregnant woman released at Benghazi; the numbers released (148) and kept (106, including the 12 crew); the 707 hospital plane and command plane; the reprisals against Kenyans; Dora Bloch's age and the British files and 1987 Ugandan inquiry.
- **Cut, could not source:** the plane being renamed by the hijackers; the one dual-citizen passenger who escaped the selection; "cyber units" (1976); the cannibalism rumour about Amin (unproven and graphic).
- **Kept but attributed on screen:** Amin's complicity ("suspected, never proven" chip on the landing; his help guarding the hostages is documented); the Nobel Peace Prize flattery ("Bar-Lev's account"); Dora Bloch's killing ("British files and Ugandan inquiry").
- **Softened:** "Benjamin Netanyahu became PM because of this operation" becomes "carried his name into politics, became prime minister 20 years later".
- **Wording:** "hijackers" throughout (the transcript also says "terrorists"). Peres's quote keeps "terrorists" because it is his word.

## Style

- **House style:** the 3D cartoon documentary world (round white heads, no nose, ink outlines, mitten hands, detailed lit sets, parchment maps), as in FZ1073. This film reuses FZ1073's airliner, cockpit and night-airport ideas.
- **Tone:** serious and calm, like FZ1073. One light beat only (the crocodiles in Act 6).
- **Palette:** night blue #0f1b2d and runway sodium amber #f2a33a for Entebbe; warm white daylight for Tel Aviv; Uganda map green #3f8f4a; Israel map blue #2f6db5; danger #b8322a. Grade: cold through Acts 3-8, warm sunrise for Act 9.
- **Recurring cast (generic house-style figures with name callouts, never likenesses):** Captain Michel Bacos and his crew; Wilfried Böse, Brigitte Kuhlmann and the two PFLP-EO hijackers; Idi Amin (portrait style plus figure in uniform); Yoni Netanyahu; Rabin, Peres, Shomron; Baruch Bar-Lev; Dora Bloch.
- **Act badge emblems:** aircraft (Acts 0-4, 7, 9), lightning bolt (Acts 5, 6, 8).
