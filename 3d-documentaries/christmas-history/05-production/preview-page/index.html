Title
Jesus Wasn't Born on Christmas? The Real History of Christmas
Description and chapters
Jesus probably wasn't born on 25 December. So why do two billion people celebrate on that day, with a tree from Livonia, a log from the Norse and a saint from Turkey in a red suit?

This is the happy, surprising story of Christmas, told in 3D animation and guided by Flurry the snowman: Roman Saturnalia and the sun god's birthday, Yule logs and Norse midwinter, the real Saint Nicholas, a gift-bringing witch in Italy, kings bringing presents on 6 January, a medieval feast with 28 oxen, the year Christmas was banned, and the queen who made the Christmas tree famous.
