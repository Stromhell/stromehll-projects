## What changed from the transcript

- **Sponsor dropped:** the Odoo read and the toga joke around it.
- **Facts fixed:** Pompey was beaten at Pharsalus in 48 BC and killed in Egypt; the March 45 BC victory (Munda, Spain) was over Pompey's sons and supporters. Spurinna was a man, not a woman. "Tilius" is Tillius Cimber. The man with the scroll is named (Artemidorus of Cnidus).
- **Added:** "Et tu, Brute" is Shakespeare (1599); Suetonius says Caesar said nothing or "You too, child?" in Greek. Only one of the 23 wounds was fatal (the physician Antistius, in Suetonius). Philippi 42 BC and Augustus 27 BC. Brutus and Cassius named as the leaders.
- **Softened for the channel:** the stab to the groin is dropped; the killing is told in silhouettes, a closing ring of togas and cuts to black.
- **Attributed on screen:** the stylus jab (Suetonius), the "sudden death" dinner line (Plutarch), the omens ("ancient writers say").
- **Quote:** Balsdon is paraphrased and attributed, since the exact wording could not be checked.

## Style

- **House style:** the 3D cartoon documentary world, with a marble-and-gold Roman look: white marble sets, deep red and purple togas, warm morning sun in the Curia.
- **Tone:** calm and serious with a couple of light beats (the three rejected plans, the stylus).
- **Palette:** Roman red #a8322d, imperial purple #5b2a6e, gold #d9a441, marble #ece6dc, parchment maps; grade cools from Act 4 to Act 5, warm candlelight in Act 6.
- **Recurring cast:** Caesar (laurel, purple-bordered toga), Mark Antony (broad), Marcus Brutus, Cassius, Decimus Brutus, Trebonius, Cimber, Casca, Calpurnia, Spurinna, Artemidorus, Octavian, Pompey (statue only). Generic house-style figures with name callouts.
- **Act badge emblems:** laurel, crown, dagger (shown sheathed or as an icon, never in motion), eye.
