Description and chapters
On 15 March 44 BC, the most powerful man in the world walked into a meeting of the Senate alone. More than sixty men were waiting for him.

How did Julius Caesar go from Rome's favourite general to dictator for life? Who joined the plot, how did they smuggle daggers into the Senate, and did Caesar really say "Et tu, Brute"? Here is the full story of the Ides of March, and why killing Caesar destroyed the Republic it was meant to save, told in 3D animation.
