# Explainer animation style: analysis of the reference ("Why the Stock...", 15 min, 1080p30)

## Look
- Flat vector cartoon, thin dark outlines, soft cel shading. Simple bean-body people with dot eyes and glasses; personified objects (logos, chips, buildings with faces).
- Lighting does the mood: warm spotlight cones and pools, dark vignettes, neon glow on hero objects, colour grades per scene (sunset pink, neon green, night blue).
- Text is handwritten caps on paper tags, calendars, stamps, sticky notes and signs inside the world. No subtitles.

## Camera
- The camera never stops: slow push-ins, gentle drifts and pans that follow the subject.
- Cuts on narration beats; transitions are rack-focus blurs (blur out, cut, blur in) or a hard cut to a black card.
- Black chapter cards with one short handwritten white line ("Not one.", "The Midterms") held 2 to 3 s.
- Zoom-in inserts: magnifier circles, circular close-ups on a detail, then push the camera into them.

## How the animation works (limited, pose-to-pose)
- Characters mostly hold a pose; acting is in eyes, brows, mouth and one arm. A walk is used only when the idea is movement (walking up a chart line).
- Props do most of the motion: they slide in, drop in, pop with overshoot, fly (papers), or a big hand reaches into frame.
- Pop-in timing: about 0.3 to 0.5 s with overshoot, each one on a sound effect.
- Numbered callouts (1, 2, 3) appear one by one along a path.
- Data is staged physically: line charts are terrain the character walks on, money is stacks, stocks are characters.
- Constant small life: glow pulses, sparks, falling paper, crowd bobbing.

## How the rubber-hose cast adapts (preview v1)
- Same rig and designs from rh.js, placed into spotlight stages, chart landscapes and dim rooms with vignettes and blur cuts.
- Acting cut down to explainer timing: holds, a wave, a sneak, a surprise take.
- Labels switched to handwritten caps tags; black cards for chapter beats; magnifier insert for the reveal.
- Music and SFX made in code (pops, whooshes, footsteps, paper, sting).
