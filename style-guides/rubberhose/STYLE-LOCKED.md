# LOCKED STYLE: Finance with Stromi (approved by Pushpam, 12 Nov 2026)

**Look:** the 2D/3D hybrid. The rubber-hose 2D cast (rh.js) acts inside 3D sets built in code with three.js.

- Reference video: hybrid-preview/Hybrid_2D3D_Preview_v1.mp4 (Drive: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zUpgpZcjnB-w0vw5yIlQWZxFWYMlvUxd/view)
- Full rules: style guide page https://claude.ai/artifact/WcdnCieuxDM4GDnD47L2LJ, section 12 "Hybrid (locked)"
- Code: hybrid-preview/hybrid.html (sets, castCard, slidePose, SHOTS, composite), rh.js + cast.js (characters), hybrid-preview/hsfx.py (foley/music synth), hybrid-preview/rendh.js (renderer)
- Notes: hybrid-preview/HYBRID-NOTES.md

## Must-haves
1. Characters are 2D only, drawn on billboard cards. They use the bean + noodle anatomy, slide with a 2-frame wiggle, and get detailed hands in close-ups.
2. Sets are 3D, lit by one practical key light (chiaroscuro), with fog, soft shadows, a heavy vignette and grain.
3. The camera is always moving. Cuts use a blur.
4. Data is diegetic: whiteboards, paper, tags, scales, stamps, cash. No floating UI.
5. Black handwritten cards appear only at the open, the close and big turns. Use fewer than the reference, and more acting.
6. Sound: a narrator plus cast voices (voice still to be picked), foley made in code, a low minor music bed, and a whoosh on every cut.
