TigerGraph · Storyboard v1Look & feel
Look & feel
Visual direction
Dark, premium SaaS look in the style of the benchmark: deep navy/black stage, soft TG Blue light blooms, frosted-glass UI panels tilted in 3D space, product-style screens instead of characters. The graph itself is the hero: glowing nodes and connectors that light up step by step.
Motion language
Smooth eased camera moves (push-ins, parallax tilts, dolly along the graph), glass panels sliding/expanding, light pulses travelling along edges, kinetic typography for key lines, crisp UI micro-interactions (hover, click ripple, ticks). Transitions are match-cuts through UI elements rather than hard cuts.
Supers (on-screen text)
Lower-left lower-third with TG Orange accent bar, Geist Medium. Every super from the script is placed as written.
Audio
AI voiceover (samples sent for approval before final), premium licensed background music (modern tech / ambient pulse), UI SFX for clicks, ticks, whooshes, data pulses.
Brand colours (from guideline)
TG Blue
#43A4DC
TG Orange
#EC6408
Black
#000000
Status only
tick / gap flag
Typography
Geist Bold headlines · Medium supers · Light body
Brand print font is Urbane. Geist (the brand's listed Office font) is used in these frames; we'll switch to Urbane in animation if a licence/font file is provided.
Logo
Alternate White logo on dark backgrounds, clear space = height of "e", never recoloured or distorted.
