Look & feel
Storyboard v2
Visual direction
Clean white stage taken from tigergraph.com: soft TG Blue gradient panels, white product-style cards with line-icon tiles, thin glowing connectors and a subtle dot texture. The graph is the hero and lights up step by step.
Making it dynamic
Every scene moves in 3D: tilted UI panels, a graph plane the camera dollies and flies along, cards that spring out toward camera, light pulses racing along edges, ripples, stamps and kinetic type. Depth comes from out-of-focus foreground cards and rack-focus. Transitions are match-cuts through UI elements (card flips, zoom-throughs, whip-pans), never plain cuts.
Audio
AI voiceover (samples shared for approval first), premium licensed tech/ambient music, and UI sound design on every hit: clicks, ticks, whooshes, pulses, stamps.
Product demo
Scenes 06–11 will use the client's demo recording when it arrives, framed in this UI. The frames here show the intended layout.
