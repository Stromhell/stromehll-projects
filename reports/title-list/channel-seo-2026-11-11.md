Every video takes one famous empire, war, spy case or company and finds the one decision that made it rise or fall. How did Nvidia go from a Denny's booth to $5 trillion? Why did World War 1 really start? How did a tiny army take Spain, and how was it lost 781 years later? Who killed Micromax?

What you'll find here:
- Wars and empires: World War 1, World War 2, Napoleon, the Mongols, Julius Caesar, Rome
- The rise and fall of companies: Nvidia, Nike, LEGO, BlackRock, McDonald's
- Spies, crime and hackers: real cases, told step by step
- Faith and traditions: where Christmas and New Year really come from

Every story is researched from real sources and told simply, with animation, maps and sound.

New long video every 4 days, plus Shorts every day.
Subscribe and hit the bell so you don't miss the next story.
