# Posting rules (Pushpam's)

1. Full videos go out **at least 2 days apart**.
2. **At most 5 reels a day.** This was raised from 3 on 28 Oct 2026.
3. Everything is scheduled on **Zernio**.
4. Reels go to **YouTube and Instagram**. Full combined videos go to **YouTube only**.
5. Every post goes out at the **ideal US audience time**.
6. A video's reels go out **only after its full video is live**.
7. **Release order:** 3 x 2D history videos before each 3D video (2D, 2D, 2D, 3D, then repeat). Breaks are flagged, and posts aren't moved for this rule without Pushpam's OK.
8. Every file created is saved to GitHub (`Stromhell/stromehll-projects`) and to Drive (`AI/`) with a clear folder structure.

Pushpam's approval covers scheduling and moving posts. It does not cover deleting posts or changing titles, thumbnails or copy.
