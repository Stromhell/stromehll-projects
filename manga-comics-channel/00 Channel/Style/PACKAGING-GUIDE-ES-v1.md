## 1. What the reference does (observed)

Recent views: Luffy's New Gear 6.6 lakh (660K), Gear 6 Monkey Zoan 4.6 lakh, One Piece 1198 3.9 lakh, Black Clover marriages 8 lakh, Boruto 39 1.3 lakh in 1 day. Lengths 12-34 min, mostly 16-20 min. Upload roughly every 1-2 days, each one tied to a new chapter or a hot topic.

### Thumbnails
- 1-2 **huge character faces** fill the frame: eyes, teeth, sweat, extreme emotion (fear, rage, evil grin, shock). Faces crop off the frame edge.
- **Neon glow and rim light**, very saturated: purple/magenta, electric blue, fire red/orange, toxic green. Aura, lightning or energy behind the character. Everything is sharp and highly detailed (looks commissioned/repainted, not a screenshot).
- **Split screen / versus**: hero on one side, threat on the other, often with a diagonal cut or contrasting colour halves (blue vs red).
- **One short line of dialogue in quotes**, bottom centre: white heavy sans, thick black stroke, slight shadow. Examples: "BORUTO F**KED UP!", "WHO WOULD WIN?", "Who Are You?", "Calm Down Luffy, My Son.", "I always had this power". It is a quote spoken by a character, NOT the title.
- Sometimes a **small callout tag** in a top corner ("NEW GEAR!", "DRAGON IS COMING!") with a curved red/yellow arrow pointing at the key detail, or a big two-colour headline at top ("EVERY EVOLUTION").
- Occasional comic speech bubble ("NAH, I'D WIN.") and real-person cutouts for crossover/meme videos.
- Bottom-right stays clear for the duration badge.

### Titles
- Mostly **ALL CAPS hype with a few lowercase words**, 70-110 chars. Structure:
  `[SHOCK CLAIM]! [specific stakes/characters] | [SERIES + CHAPTER]`
- Real examples: "THE END OF BORUTO HAS BEGUN! Naruto & Sasuke's WORST NIGHTMARE Has Arrived | TWO BLUE VORTEX 39"; "LUFFY'S NEW DEADLY AWAKENING CHANGES EVERYTHING! ONLY DEADBEAT DRAGON CAN SAVE HIM! One Piece 1198"; "All 19 Devils & Their TRUE Power Explained! The 7 Levels of Hell | Black Clover"; "UH... WTF! Luffy's GEAR 6 MONKEY ZOAN Was PLANNED SINCE Chapter 1 (ONE PIECE)".
- Recurring words: FINALLY, CHANGES EVERYTHING, EXPLAINED, TRUE POWER, NEW, INSANE, BROKEN, IS OVER, WE WERE WRONG, STRONGEST, REVEALED, ALL / EVERY + number.
- Some shorter curiosity titles ("Luffy's New Gear Situation is Crazy...") do very well (660K).

### Description (Boruto 39)
1. Caps hook sentence restating the title promise.
2. 2 short paragraphs naming every character, power and event (keyword-dense, natural sentences).
3. 2 open questions to the viewer, then "Let us know your theories in the comments!" and a subscribe line naming the series.
4. 3 hashtags: #Series #SeriesArc #SeriesChapterN.
5. Link blocks (sponsor, merch, membership, socials, credits/copyright note, affiliate disclosure), #anime #manga.
6. **Chapters** every 30 s to 2 min, each one a hype mini-title ("Boruto DESTROYS Code with Rasengan Uzuhiko!", "Kashin Koji Predicts Boruto's DEATH!").

### Tags (32 on Boruto 39)
Series + arc + "chapter 39", main characters, character pairs ("boruto and sarada"), versus ("boruto vs code"), "boruto theory", "boruto latest chapter", "boruto manga", channel name, broad ("anime recap", "anime fight"), and big-name side characters (itachi, sharingan).

### Video structure (see STYLE-BREAKDOWN-v1.md)
Cold open with the biggest shock claim in the first seconds, stacked hooks, then chapter walkthrough mixed with theory, ending on an open question. Timestamps cover every beat.

## 2. Our rules for every Stormi Knows Anime video

### Thumbnail checklist
- [ ] 1-2 faces, at least 50% of the frame, extreme emotion, eyes visible.
- [ ] Neon rim light + glow aura, saturated contrasting palette (pick 2: violet, cyan, fire red, gold, toxic green).
- [ ] Versus split when the video has a conflict.
- [ ] One Spanish quote line in "comillas", 2-5 words, white heavy sans (Anton / Bebas Neue / Montserrat Black), 10-14 px black stroke, bottom centre, above the duration badge zone.
- [ ] Optional corner callout ("¡NUEVO GEAR!", "¡LLEGÓ DRAGON!") + red curved arrow.
- [ ] Logo: mascot with headband, small, top-right (channel rule).
- [ ] Readable at 160 px wide. Title words are NOT repeated on the thumbnail.
- [ ] Own art: repainted/original manga-style art (Animagine/our tools) or composited real panels with heavy paint-over and glow. Never their thumbs or commissioned art.
- [ ] 1280x720 JPG under 2 MB. Make 2 variants for the low-view refresh.

Spanish quote ideas: "BORUTO LA CAGÓ", "¿QUIÉN GANARÍA?", "¿Quién eres tú?", "Cálmate, Luffy, hijo mío", "Siempre tuve este poder", "Nah, yo ganaría", "ESTO ES EL FIN".

### Title formula (Spanish)
`[GANCHO EN MAYÚSCULAS]! [personajes + lo que está en juego] | [SERIE + CAPÍTULO]`
- 70-100 chars; the hook must land in the first ~50 chars (mobile cut).
- Keep official English names as fans search them: Two Blue Vortex, Gear 5, Rasengan, Haki, Bankai, Kaioken.
- Use LatAm-neutral Spanish ("capítulo", "explicado", "la cagó" only on thumbs, not titles).
- Power words: ¡POR FIN!, LO CAMBIA TODO, EXPLICADO, EL VERDADERO PODER, NUEVO, ES INCREÍBLE, ROTO, SE ACABÓ, NOS EQUIVOCAMOS, EL MÁS FUERTE, REVELADO, TODOS LOS / LAS + número.
- Templates:
  - "¡EL FIN DE BORUTO HA COMENZADO! La PEOR PESADILLA de Naruto y Sasuke | TWO BLUE VORTEX 39"
  - "¡EL NUEVO DESPERTAR MORTAL DE LUFFY LO CAMBIA TODO! Solo DRAGON puede salvarlo | One Piece 1198"
  - "Los 19 DEMONIOS y su VERDADERO PODER Explicados | Black Clover"
  - "¿QUÉ?! El GEAR 6 de Luffy estaba PLANEADO desde el Capítulo 1 (ONE PIECE)"
  - "La situación del nuevo Gear de Luffy es una locura..."

- Chapters every 1-2 min, each a hype mini-title in Spanish. First line max ~150 chars (shows in search).

### Tags (25-35, ~400-480 chars)
Series EN + ES ("boruto two blue vortex", "boruto capitulo 39", "boruto two blue vortex 39 español"), main characters, pairs, "X vs Y", "teoria boruto", "boruto manga explicado", "boruto ultimo capitulo", "anime en español", "manga explicado", "stormi knows anime", 2-3 big-name side characters.

### Video
- No intro/logo sting (Pushpam rule); open on the biggest shock claim, first 30 s stack 2-3 hooks.
- 12-25 min for chapter breakdowns; timestamps always.
- End on an open question that matches the description questions.
