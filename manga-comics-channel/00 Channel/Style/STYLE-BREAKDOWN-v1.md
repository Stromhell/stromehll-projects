# Manga & Comics channel: style breakdown v1 (20 Nov 2026)

Reference (research only): "THE END OF BORUTO HAS BEGUN! ... | TWO BLUE VORTEX 39", Anime Balls Deep (2.42M subs), 18:35, 112k views.
Studied via Chrome: ~25 frames across the video, auto-transcript, a frame-difference cut scan (2:20 to 4:20), and in-page pitch analysis of the narrator.

## 1. Format and structure
| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open hook | 0:00 to 0:45 | 4 to 5 shock claims back to back ("biggest mistake of his life", "100 times harder", "the entire world now wants him dead"). Fastest cuts of the video. |
| Cue line | 0:45 | Narrator says "With that, let's cue the intro." |
| Logo sting | 0:46 to 1:03 | ~15 s branded intro: big white logo + channel name on a red background, music only. |
| Chapter walkthrough | 1:03 onward | Goes through the chapter in page order, explaining each scene, calling out "red flags", tying back to older chapters. |
| Theory section | last ~5 min | Big theory built from the walkthrough ("love is a defect", foreshadowing, analogies). |
| Outro | last ~20 s | Purple background, a manga panel, suggested-video card left, round logo "subscribe" button right. |

Length ~18.5 min, ~2,700 words, about 150 words per minute overall (around 160 while talking).

## 2. Visual layers (what is on screen)
1. **Manga page / panel as the main shot (about 75% of runtime).** Black-and-white pages filling the 16:9 frame, often sitting on a **light grey crumpled-paper texture** background, sometimes on black.
2. **Slow camera move on every still:** gentle push-in or pan across the page (Ken Burns), so nothing is ever fully static.
3. **Panel pop-out:** a single panel is cut out with a thin black border and soft drop shadow and laid on top of the page, scaled up, to point at the exact moment being discussed.
4. **Colour hero images:** coloured character art on a starfield/space background for big power reveals (high contrast against the B&W pages).
5. **Anime clips:** short official-anime moments for action beats (the continuous-motion runs in the cut scan).
6. **Meme reaction overlays:** live-action reaction clip in picture-in-picture (lower right) during the hook; "WASTED"-style red blood-splatter text overlay for a dramatic joke.
7. **Watermark:** small channel logo bottom-right, whole video.
8. **No on-screen subtitles or captions of the voice-over.** Text on screen is only the manga's own speech bubbles and the meme overlays.

## 3. Editing rhythm
- Hard cuts roughly every **2 to 4 s** on stills; a new panel or a new crop on almost every sentence.
- Motion segments (anime clips, continuous pans) run 5 to 15 s.
- Transitions are mostly **straight cuts**; occasional quick zoom-punch on a reveal. No fancy wipes.
- The picture always follows the words: when the narrator names a character, that face is on screen.

## 4. Audio
- **Narrator:** casual, energetic young male YouTuber, American, conversational slang ("this dude", "cooking is fire", "the hate train is going to go choo choo"), jokes mixed into lore. Pitch analysis shows a wide range (roughly 120 to 320 Hz with lots of jumps), so a lively delivery, not a flat documentary read.
- **Music:** continuous dramatic/epic bed under the voice (auto-captions mark [music] 12 times), louder in the hook and intro sting, ducked under narration.
- **SFX:** meme stings on joke overlays, whooshes on panel pop-outs, impact hits on reveals.

## 5. Script voice
- Opens with the most extreme claim, then stacks 3 to 4 more.
- Talks to the viewer ("remember", "right?", "we can't forget").
- Explains clues like a detective ("that is only one of many red flags"), then predicts.
- Drops jokes and memes every 30 to 60 s to break up lore.

## 6. Title and thumbnail pattern
- ALL CAPS hype title + "|" + series and chapter number ("... | TWO BLUE VORTEX 39").
- Description: 3-paragraph SEO summary full of character names, question hooks, hashtags.

## 7. How we will make it ours (originality rules)
- Our own channel name, logo sting, colours, watermark, mascot reaction stickers instead of copied memes.
- Original scripts and our own theories; reference video is research only.
- Art source decided by Pushpam (real panels he supplies, our own redrawn art, or a mix).
- No subtitles, matching the reference.
